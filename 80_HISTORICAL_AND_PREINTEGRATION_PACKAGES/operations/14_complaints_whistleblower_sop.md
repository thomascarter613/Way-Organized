# Complaints, Whistleblowing, and Non-Retaliation SOP

## Intake channels
Maintain at least: (1) a normal internal contact; and (2) an independent channel controlled by the Safeguarding and Institutional Integrity Committee or external provider. Publish both.

## Intake workflow
1. Date/time stamp; assign case ID.
2. Acknowledge receipt promptly when contact information is available.
3. Triage for immediate safety, mandatory reporting, criminal conduct, evidence preservation, retaliation risk, and conflicts.
4. Route complaints involving senior leadership directly to independent oversight.
5. Adopt interim protections that are nonpunitive where possible.
6. Determine resolution path: information response, management correction, mediation only if freely appropriate, formal investigation, safeguarding referral, HR process, or external authority.
7. Record outcome and appeal rights where applicable.

## Non-retaliation
Prohibited retaliation includes firing/removal, schedule or housing manipulation, threats, shunning directives, public humiliation, doxxing, spiritual condemnation targeted at the reporter, witness pressure, donor pressure, exclusion from ordinary services, or adverse action materially motivated by protected reporting/participation.

## Anonymous reports
Anonymous reports are accepted. Investigators should distinguish inability to corroborate from proof that an anonymous report was false.

## Bad faith
Knowingly fabricated allegations may be misconduct, but inability to substantiate an allegation is not by itself evidence of bad faith.
