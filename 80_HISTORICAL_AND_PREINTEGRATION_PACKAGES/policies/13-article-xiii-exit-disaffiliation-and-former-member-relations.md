# Article XIII. Exit, Disaffiliation, and Former-Member Relations

**Purpose:** Make departure safe enough that continued participation remains genuinely voluntary.

## 13.1 No permission required

A competent adult may resign membership or participation by any clear expression of intent. Leadership approval is not required.

## 13.2 No exit fees

No person shall owe an exit fee, spiritual release fee, repayment of ordinary benevolence, or transfer of personal assets as a condition of leaving, except ordinary debts established independently of membership.

## 13.3 No institutional shunning

The organization shall not direct members to sever ordinary family or social contact with a departed person merely because of departure, disagreement, criticism, or change of belief.

## 13.4 Privacy after exit

Confidential information about a former member shall remain protected. Departure does not authorize public disclosure of confession, counseling information, health information, or private correspondence.

## 13.5 Records and property

Personal property shall be returned promptly. Employment records, earned compensation, benefits, housing matters, and contractual obligations shall be handled under ordinary law and written agreements rather than spiritual leverage.

## 13.6 Criticism

Former members retain the same lawful right to speak about their experience as others. The organization may correct demonstrably false factual claims but shall not use intimidation or confidential information to suppress criticism.
