# Article III. Founder and Leadership Limitations

**Purpose:** Prevent the founder or any later leader from becoming the institution’s unchecked spiritual, corporate, financial, or disciplinary center.

## 3.1 Founder status is historical, not sovereign

The title of founder records historical contribution and carries no inherent veto, life tenure, ownership interest, doctrinal infallibility, disciplinary privilege, or power to bind future governing bodies.

## 3.2 Separation of powers

No individual may simultaneously exercise final authority over doctrine, corporate governance, finance, investigations, and discipline. Material decisions in these domains shall be distributed among independent bodies or officers.

## 3.3 No unilateral board control

The founder or chief executive shall not possess unilateral power to appoint or remove a governing-board majority. Board nominations shall use an independent process with staggered terms and conflict screening.

## 3.4 No self-compensation

No person may vote on, bargain for, or approve their own compensation, benefits, housing, loans, reimbursements, severance, intellectual-property transactions, or other economic benefits.

## 3.5 No self-investigation

A leader accused of misconduct shall have no authority over intake, investigator selection, evidence access decisions, findings, sanctions, appeal, public communication, or retaliation review concerning that allegation.

## 3.6 No dynastic succession

No office may pass automatically to a spouse, child, family member, designated spiritual heir, or favored disciple. Related persons must satisfy the same independent selection process as others.

## 3.7 Term and review

Executive and senior spiritual leadership shall be subject to periodic performance and integrity review by independent directors. Any life-tenure concept is prohibited for corporate or executive authority.

## 3.8 Removal

The governing body may suspend or remove any officer, including the founder, under procedures defined by the bylaws. Removal shall not require the officer’s consent.

## 3.9 Founder recusal

The founder shall recuse from matters involving personal compensation, family employment, related-party contracts, allegations against the founder or close associates, succession, and any matter presenting an actual or apparent conflict.

## 3.10 Founder covenant

Any founder serving in office shall annually sign the same conflict, safeguarding, confidentiality, conduct, and non-retaliation commitments required of other senior leaders, without special exception.
