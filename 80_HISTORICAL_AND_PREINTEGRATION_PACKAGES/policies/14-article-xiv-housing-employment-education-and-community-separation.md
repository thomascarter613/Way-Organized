# Article XIV. Housing, Employment, Education, and Community Separation

**Purpose:** Prevent material dependency from becoming a mechanism of religious obedience.

## 14.1 Separate agreements

Housing, employment, tuition, stipends, loans, and community-residence arrangements shall use written agreements distinct from membership covenants and shall identify termination, notice, grievance, property, and payment terms.

## 14.2 No total dependency by design

The organization shall avoid structures in which one leader controls a person’s housing, employment, money, transportation, education, healthcare access, and social participation simultaneously.

## 14.3 Employment rights

Employees shall receive written job descriptions, compensation terms, supervision, complaint channels, and lawful wage practices. Spiritual language shall not be used to evade ordinary employment obligations.

## 14.4 Communal property

A person contributing property to a community shall receive clear written disclosure of whether the transfer is a gift, lease, loan, investment, or other arrangement and what, if anything, is returnable upon departure.

## 14.5 Emergency transition

Where loss of organizational housing or employment would create immediate vulnerability, the organization should use reasonable transition procedures rather than abrupt deprivation as punishment, subject to safety and law.
