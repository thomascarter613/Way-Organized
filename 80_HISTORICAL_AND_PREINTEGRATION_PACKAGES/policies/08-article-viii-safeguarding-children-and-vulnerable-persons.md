# Article VIII. Safeguarding Children and Vulnerable Persons

**Purpose:** Create prevention, reporting, response, and oversight controls before programs involving minors or vulnerable adults begin.

## 8.1 Zero spiritual exemption

Religious status, confession, pastoral privilege, internal discipline, forgiveness, reconciliation, or the accused person’s rank shall never be used to obstruct legally required reporting or protective action.

## 8.2 Mandatory reporting

The organization shall train all workers and volunteers on applicable reporting law. In Tennessee, the baseline policy shall state that everyone is a mandated reporter of suspected child abuse or neglect and that internal reporting does not replace a report to the appropriate authorities when the legal threshold is met.

## 8.3 Two-adult rule

Programs involving minors shall ordinarily maintain at least two screened adults or equivalent open-and-observable safeguards, with documented exceptions for emergency, healthcare, or other circumstances covered by written policy.

## 8.4 Screening

Roles with access to minors or vulnerable persons shall receive role-appropriate application review, references, background checks, identity verification, training, and periodic rescreening where lawful and appropriate.

## 8.5 One-to-one contact

Private one-to-one contact between an adult leader and a minor shall be limited, observable where practical, and subject to guardian-aware communications rules. Digital communications shall follow the same principle.

## 8.6 Transportation and lodging

Transport, travel, overnight events, bathrooms, changing areas, lodging, and home visits shall use written safeguards appropriate to age and context.

## 8.7 Immediate protective action

Credible safety concerns may justify temporary suspension from access or duties while facts are assessed. Administrative leave is not a determination of guilt.

## 8.8 Survivor-centered response

The organization shall prioritize immediate safety, avoid retaliation or pressure for reconciliation, preserve evidence, provide information about independent support, and refrain from requiring mediation between an alleged victim and alleged perpetrator.

## 8.9 No internal substitution

An internal inquiry shall not substitute for law-enforcement, child-protection, licensing, or other external investigation when those authorities have jurisdiction.
