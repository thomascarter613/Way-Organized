# Article X. Complaints, Whistleblowing, and Non-Retaliation

**Purpose:** Ensure allegations can reach a forum not controlled by the person complained about.

## 10.1 Multiple intake channels

Complaints may be submitted to at least two independent channels, one of which bypasses ordinary management. Anonymous reports shall be accepted while acknowledging evidentiary limitations.

## 10.2 No chain-of-command barrier

A complaint about a senior leader need not be reported to that leader or through that leader’s subordinates.

## 10.3 Acknowledgment and triage

Reports shall be acknowledged promptly and triaged for immediate safety, mandatory reporting, evidence preservation, conflicts, interim measures, and investigator selection.

## 10.4 Anti-retaliation

Retaliation includes adverse employment or ministry action, threats, intimidation, social punishment directed by leadership, reputational attacks using confidential information, interference with housing or benefits, or bad-faith discipline triggered by protected reporting.

## 10.5 Bad-faith reports

Knowingly fabricated allegations may be addressed under ordinary conduct rules, but an allegation is not bad faith merely because it is unsubstantiated, mistaken, difficult to prove, or ultimately not sustained.

## 10.6 Records

Complaint records shall be access-controlled, retained under a written schedule, preserved when litigation or investigation is reasonably anticipated, and not altered or destroyed to protect reputation.
