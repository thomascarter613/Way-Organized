# Article IV. Governing Board Independence

**Purpose:** Create a board capable of governing rather than merely ratifying decisions made by management or charismatic leadership.

## 4.1 Size

The board should ordinarily contain seven to eleven voting directors and shall never contain fewer than five except during a temporary vacancy period expressly allowed by the bylaws.

## 4.2 Independence

At least two-thirds of voting directors shall be independent. An independent director is not an employee, not subordinate to an employee, not an immediate family member of another director or senior staff member, and does not receive material compensation from the organization other than reasonable expense reimbursement or approved board service if legally appropriate.

## 4.3 Staggered terms

Directors shall serve staggered fixed terms. Consecutive service shall be limited by the bylaws, followed by a meaningful break before reappointment except for narrowly defined emergency circumstances.

## 4.4 Independent chair

The board chair should be independent and shall not simultaneously serve as chief executive, chief financial officer, lead clergy executive, or founder with executive authority.

## 4.5 Executive sessions

Independent directors shall meet periodically without staff, founder, or executive leadership present.

## 4.6 Reserved powers

The board retains exclusive authority over executive appointment and removal, executive compensation, annual budget approval, audited or independently reviewed financial statements, major asset transactions, related-party transactions, safeguarding oversight, investigation of senior leaders, and amendments to entrenched protections.

## 4.7 Minutes and records

Material decisions shall be documented contemporaneously, including recusals, conflicts, alternatives considered, comparability data where relevant, and the basis for the decision.

## 4.8 Board access

Directors shall have reasonable independent access to organizational records, outside counsel, auditors, safeguarding advisers, and investigators necessary to discharge fiduciary duties.

## 4.9 No loyalty oath to a person

Directors may affirm the mission and governing documents but shall not be required to pledge personal loyalty or obedience to a founder, leader, family, or faction.
