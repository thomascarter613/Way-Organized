# Article XI. Independent Investigation Protocol

**Purpose:** Protect fact-finding from conflicts and predetermined outcomes.

## 11.1 Independence trigger

Allegations involving the founder, chief executive, senior clergy, a director, safeguarding failures, sexual misconduct, material fraud, retaliation, or systemic abuse shall presumptively be referred to an investigator independent of the implicated chain of command.

## 11.2 Investigator qualification

The investigator shall have appropriate subject-matter competence and no material relationship that would reasonably call impartiality into question.

## 11.3 Terms of reference

The appointing body shall define allegations, scope, reporting line, access to records, confidentiality limits, evidence preservation, and authority to expand scope when related misconduct is discovered.

## 11.4 Fair process

The subject of allegations shall receive a fair opportunity to respond to material allegations and evidence, subject to safety, witness-protection, legal, and investigative constraints.

## 11.5 Standard and findings

The organization shall identify the evidentiary standard used for internal findings. Findings shall distinguish substantiated, unsubstantiated, inconclusive, and policy/system failures rather than collapsing all outcomes into guilt or innocence.

## 11.6 Board control

Management shall not alter an independent investigator’s factual findings. The governing body may separately determine organizational action and shall document departures from recommendations.

## 11.7 External authorities

Nothing in the investigation process shall discourage or delay lawful reporting to police, child-protection agencies, regulators, insurers, or courts.
