# Article XVII. Entrenchment and Amendment

**Purpose:** Prevent a future charismatic majority from casually deleting the safeguards that constrain it.

## 17.1 Tier I entrenched provisions

Freedom of conscience, no infallible living authority, safe exit, non-retaliation, external reporting, board independence, conflict/recusal rules, safeguarding duties, independent investigation of senior leaders, and the right to criticize leadership are entrenched.

## 17.2 Amendment threshold

An entrenched provision may be amended only after advance publication of the proposed change and rationale, a waiting period of at least sixty days, two separate board votes at meetings separated by at least thirty days, and approval by at least eighty percent of all seated independent directors.

## 17.3 No emergency suspension

Entrenched provisions may not be suspended by executive emergency, prophetic declaration, doctrinal decree, unanimous staff agreement, donor demand, or founder instruction.

## 17.4 Adverse-rights review

Any proposed amendment that materially reduces participant rights, board independence, complaint access, financial controls, or safeguarding shall receive review by independent legal counsel before adoption and shall identify the rights being reduced.

## 17.5 Member notice

Where the organization has statutory or bylaw voting members, applicable member approval rights shall be honored in addition to these internal thresholds.

## 17.6 Sunset resistance

Entrenched protections do not expire automatically and shall not be converted to temporary policy by amendment of a lower-level document.
