# Article XVI. External Institutional Integrity Review

**Purpose:** Create recurring adversarial examination rather than relying on internal confidence.

## 16.1 Frequency

At least once every three years, and more often after major incidents or rapid growth, the board shall commission an external institutional integrity review.

## 16.2 Review team

The review should include expertise appropriate to organizational risk, such as nonprofit governance, safeguarding, financial controls, coercive-control/high-control dynamics, employment practices, and religious-studies or pastoral ethics.

## 16.3 Mandate

Reviewers shall be asked to identify how the organization could become coercive, abusive, financially exploitative, leader-centered, retaliatory, opaque, or unsafe notwithstanding its formal policies.

## 16.4 Access

Reviewers shall receive sufficient access to policies, board minutes, complaint statistics, financial controls, leadership structures, and representative interviews to conduct meaningful review, subject to legal privilege and privacy safeguards.

## 16.5 Reporting

The board shall receive the full report. A public or member-facing summary shall disclose material findings, remediation commitments, and completion status, with redactions only as reasonably necessary for safety, privacy, privilege, or law.

## 16.6 Remediation tracking

Material findings shall be assigned owners, deadlines, and board-level follow-up. Repeated unresolved findings shall trigger enhanced oversight.
