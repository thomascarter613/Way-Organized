# Revelation, Doctrine, and Epistemic Integrity Protocol

## Classification vocabulary
Every major teaching or institutional claim should identify its principal status where useful:

- **Evidence / record** - directly observed, documented, or source-based material.
- **Historical inference** - conclusion drawn from evidence with stated uncertainty.
- **Philosophical interpretation** - conceptual interpretation, not direct observation.
- **Theological hypothesis** - religious explanation proposed for consideration.
- **Communal teaching/tradition** - teaching adopted by the community.
- **Personal conviction / claimed revelation** - sincerely held personal spiritual claim.

## Rule for extraordinary claims
Intensity, charisma, office, predictive success, altered state, dream, vision, or claimed divine source does not by itself create corporate authority.

## Doctrinal change record
A material change should include: proposition; source/evidence; competing interpretations; arguments for/against; confidence; practical implications; rights impact; dissent; decision body; vote/consensus record; effective date; review date.

## Hard stops
No revelation claim may: override safeguarding or mandatory reporting; authorize sexual access; transfer personal property without ordinary informed consent; set the claimant's compensation; decide the claimant's discipline; override another adult's healthcare/legal decisions; suspend the charter/bylaws; or require social shunning.
