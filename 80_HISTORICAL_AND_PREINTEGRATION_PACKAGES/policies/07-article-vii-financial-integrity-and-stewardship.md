# Article VII. Financial Integrity and Stewardship

**Purpose:** Prevent money from becoming a mechanism of control or private enrichment.

## 7.1 Voluntary giving

Giving shall be voluntary. No donation level may determine spiritual worth, access to sacraments or rites, access to leadership, salvation, initiation status, or eligibility for ordinary pastoral care.

## 7.2 No hardship exploitation

Fundraising shall not knowingly pressure a person to give money or property in a manner reasonably likely to jeopardize basic housing, food, medical care, dependent care, or financial safety.

## 7.3 Budget transparency

The board shall approve an annual budget. A summarized budget, leadership compensation methodology, major related-party transactions, and year-end financial results shall be made available to members and, where practical, publicly.

## 7.4 Internal controls

No one person shall control authorization, custody, recordkeeping, and reconciliation for material funds. Dual controls shall apply to disbursements above thresholds set by policy.

## 7.5 Compensation

Compensation of senior leaders shall be approved in advance by disinterested directors using appropriate comparability data and contemporaneous documentation. The compensated person and conflicted persons shall not participate in deliberation or vote.

## 7.6 Related-party transactions

A related-party transaction requires disclosure, recusal, independent comparability or competitive evidence where feasible, an affirmative best-interest finding, and documented approval by disinterested directors.

## 7.7 Loans and personal use

Loans to insiders, personal use of organizational assets, housing arrangements, intellectual-property licenses, vehicle use, travel, and expense reimbursements shall be governed by written policies and reviewed for private benefit concerns.

## 7.8 Restricted gifts

Donor restrictions that are accepted shall be honored. Funds solicited for a specific project shall not be materially redirected without lawful authority and appropriate disclosure.

## 7.9 Annual external review

Financial statements shall receive an independent CPA compilation, review, or audit at a level appropriate to organizational size, risk, donor expectations, lender requirements, and law. The board or an independent audit/finance committee shall engage and communicate with the accountant.

## 7.10 No founder ownership

Organizational assets are not the property of the founder, clergy, directors, members, or donors. Dissolution and asset disposition shall follow governing documents and applicable charitable-purpose requirements.
