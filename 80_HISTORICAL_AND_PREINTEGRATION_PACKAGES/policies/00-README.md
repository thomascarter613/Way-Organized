# Institutional Integrity & Anti-Coercion Framework — Policy Pack

Version: 0.1 - Founding Draft
Date: September 18, 2026

This directory decomposes the master framework into adoptable policy modules. Adopt Article I and Article XVII only after counsel aligns them with the charter and bylaws. Lower-level policies may add protections but should not reduce the entrenched rights.

Recommended adoption order: constitutional principles and member rights → founder/board limitations → anti-coercion and epistemic integrity → safeguarding/complaints/investigations → finance/pastoral ethics/exit protections → transparency/external review → implementation controls.
