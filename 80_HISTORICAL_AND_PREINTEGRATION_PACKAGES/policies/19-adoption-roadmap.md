# Adoption Roadmap

## 0–30 days

Adopt interim Member Bill of Rights, Freedom of Conscience Covenant, non-retaliation rule, founder recusal rule, complaint channels, safeguarding baseline, conflicts policy; identify independent directors and counsel.

## 31–60 days

Conform charter/bylaws; create board committees; adopt compensation/related-party process; establish independent complaint intake; approve record-retention and evidence-preservation procedures; select safeguarding training.

## 61–90 days

Complete board and staff training; publish public accountability page; implement finance controls; test complaint pathway; complete child/vulnerable-person screening controls before programming.

## 3–12 months

Commission first external integrity review; obtain appropriate insurance; conduct compensation comparability review; publish first annual integrity and financial summary; remediate findings.

## Annually

Director/officer certifications, policy review, safeguarding refresh, integrity dashboard, executive evaluation, compensation approval, conflict disclosures, public report.

## Every 3 years

External institutional integrity review and governance stress test; reassess board independence and policy effectiveness.
