# Article XVIII. Implementation, Measurement, and Enforcement

**Purpose:** Turn the framework into living controls rather than a document that exists only on paper.

## 18.1 Annual certification

Senior leaders and directors shall annually certify conflicts, policy receipt, safeguarding obligations, non-retaliation duties, and disclosure of material related-party relationships.

## 18.2 Training

Directors, staff, clergy, teachers, counselors, and volunteers shall receive role-appropriate training before exercising authority and at periodic intervals thereafter.

## 18.3 Dashboard

The board shall receive at least quarterly integrity metrics, including complaints received, response times, retaliation allegations, safeguarding incidents, policy exceptions, related-party transactions, leadership turnover, financial-control exceptions, and overdue remediation items.

## 18.4 Speak-up testing

At least annually, the organization shall test whether participants know how to complain, whether channels bypass management, and whether persons reasonably fear retaliation.

## 18.5 Policy exceptions

Exceptions to integrity policies shall be documented, time-limited, justified, and approved at the level specified by policy. No exception may override an entrenched constitutional protection.

## 18.6 Insurance and counsel

The board shall periodically review appropriate insurance, including general liability, directors and officers, employment practices, abuse/molestation coverage where relevant, cyber/privacy coverage, and other risks with qualified professionals.

## 18.7 Document hierarchy

The organizational hierarchy shall be: applicable law and charter; entrenched constitutional protections; bylaws; board policies; management procedures; local practices. Lower-level instruments may add protections but may not contradict higher-level rights.
