# Article XII. Discipline, Removal, and Appeals

**Purpose:** Provide due process and prevent discipline from becoming a tool for silencing dissent.

## 12.1 Defined grounds

Discipline shall be based on published conduct or role standards, not vague disloyalty to a person, criticism of leadership, exposure of misconduct, or failure to display sufficient enthusiasm.

## 12.2 Notice

Except for urgent interim safety measures, the person shall receive notice of the material allegations and potential consequences before final action.

## 12.3 Impartial decision maker

A complainant, accused person, investigator, and final decision maker shall not be the same person. Conflicted decision makers shall recuse.

## 12.4 Proportionality

Responses shall be proportionate and distinguish safety measures, employment consequences, office eligibility, membership status, and spiritual care.

## 12.5 Appeal

Material discipline affecting membership, office, employment, ordination, or reputation shall have an appeal to a person or body not materially involved in the original decision.

## 12.6 No compelled reconciliation

Forgiveness, reconciliation, restoration to office, restoration of contact, and legal accountability are distinct concepts. No harmed person shall be compelled to reconcile or resume contact.

## 12.7 Restoration is not entitlement

Spiritual forgiveness or personal repentance does not create an automatic right to return to leadership, childcare, counseling, financial authority, or other positions of trust.
