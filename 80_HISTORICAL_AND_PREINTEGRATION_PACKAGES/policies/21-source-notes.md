# Source and Legal Review Notes

This pack is a governance draft, not legal advice.
- **Internal Revenue Service, Publication 557 (rev. Jan. 2025).** Federal overview of tax-exempt organizations and excess-benefit rules; includes conditions used for the rebuttable presumption of reasonableness.
- **Internal Revenue Service, Intermediate sanctions — Excess benefit transactions.** Defines excess-benefit transactions involving disqualified persons and applicable tax-exempt organizations.
- **Internal Revenue Service, Instructions for Form 990 (2025).** Describes reasonable compensation as the amount ordinarily paid for like services by like enterprises under like circumstances.
- **Tennessee Department of Children’s Services, Report Child Abuse.** States that everyone in Tennessee is a mandated reporter and provides the statewide reporting process.
- **Tennessee Attorney General / Center for Nonprofit Management, What Every Board Member Should Know: A Guidebook for Tennessee Nonprofits.** Board-governance guidance for Tennessee nonprofit organizations.
- **Tennessee Secretary of State, Business Forms & Fees — Domestic Nonprofit Corporations.** Current filing resources for Tennessee nonprofit corporations.
- **ECFA, Seven Standards of Responsible Stewardship, Standards 2–7.** A useful voluntary benchmark for independent governance, financial oversight, transparency, compensation, related-party transactions, and stewardship; this framework uses those concepts without adopting ECFA’s religious eligibility criteria.
