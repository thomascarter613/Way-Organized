# Conflict of Interest and Related-Party Transaction Policy

## Purpose
Protect charitable assets and decision integrity by identifying interests before they influence action.

## Covered persons
Directors, officers, senior spiritual leaders, finance staff with approval authority, committee members exercising board authority, and other persons designated by the board.

## Disclosure
Covered persons complete an annual disclosure and update it within 14 days after a material change. Disclose financial interests, family relationships, business relationships, gifts, debts, outside offices, vendor relationships, property transactions, and any other circumstance reasonably creating divided loyalty or apparent conflict.

## Decision procedure
1. Chair or secretary records the disclosure.
2. Disinterested decision makers determine whether a conflict exists.
3. The interested person may answer factual questions but then leaves deliberation and vote.
4. Decision makers consider alternatives and fairness to the organization.
5. Minutes record the disclosure, recusal, persons present, alternatives/comparability considered, vote, and rationale.
6. Material related-party transactions require independent legal/accounting review when prudent.

## Prohibited shortcuts
Founder approval, spiritual endorsement, donor pressure, urgency, or board unanimity that includes the interested person does not cure a conflict.

## Enforcement
Failure to disclose or honor recusal requirements may support corrective action, repayment, removal from authority, or discipline, subject to due process.
