# Safeguarding Children and Vulnerable Persons - Tennessee Operations Policy

## Governing rule
No spiritual office, confession practice, clergy privilege claim, reconciliation process, or internal investigation may be used to delay or obstruct a report required by applicable law.

## Tennessee reporting
Tennessee DCS states that everyone in Tennessee is a mandated reporter of suspected child abuse or neglect. When facts reasonably indicate suspected abuse or neglect, personnel shall make the required report promptly through the Tennessee Child Abuse Hotline / reporting system and call 911 for an emergency or life-threatening situation. Internal notice does not replace the external report.

## Prevention controls
- role-appropriate screening and lawful background checks before unsupervised access;
- two-adult or open/observable-environment rule, with narrowly documented exceptions for genuine emergencies;
- no secret electronic communication with minors; guardians included or approved platforms used;
- written transportation, overnight, bathroom/changing-area, photography/media, and physical-contact rules;
- no adult/minor romantic or sexual conduct; no grooming behavior;
- annual safeguarding training for covered personnel;
- code of conduct acknowledgement and boundary training;
- visitor/check-in controls appropriate to program size.

## Response
1. Ensure immediate safety; call 911 when necessary.
2. Make required external report; do not investigate first to decide whether reporting is deserved.
3. Preserve records and avoid suggestive questioning.
4. Notify the safeguarding lead unless that person is implicated; then notify the independent board channel.
5. Apply interim access restrictions when reasonably necessary.
6. Coordinate internal employment/volunteer response so it does not interfere with authorities.
7. Offer survivor-centered support and independent resources without conditioning support on silence, reconciliation, forgiveness, or continued participation.

## Records
Safeguarding files are access-restricted and retained under counsel-approved schedules. Never promise absolute confidentiality where reporting or safety duties apply.
