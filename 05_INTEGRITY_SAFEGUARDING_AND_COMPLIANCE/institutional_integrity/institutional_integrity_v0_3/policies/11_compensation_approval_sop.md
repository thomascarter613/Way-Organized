# Executive and Insider Compensation Approval SOP

Use for the chief executive, founder when compensated, senior leaders treated as insiders/disqualified persons, and material severance or housing/benefit arrangements.

## Required file before compensation starts

1. Role description and expected time commitment.
2. Total-compensation inventory: salary, bonuses, housing, retirement, insurance, allowances, reimbursements, deferred compensation, severance, loans/forgone interest, and other economic benefits.
3. At least three appropriate comparability points when practicable, considering role, geography, organization size, complexity, and qualifications.
4. Written conflict screen identifying all decision makers.
5. Draft compensation resolution.

## Approval
Only disinterested directors vote. Approval must occur in advance. The minutes or compensation memo document the terms, date, decision makers, comparability data, conflicted-person actions/recusal, and basis for concluding compensation is reasonable.

## Annual review
Review total compensation at least annually and whenever duties materially change. Automatic founder increases, percentage-of-donations arrangements, and self-approved benefits are prohibited.
