# Institutional Integrity Governance & Operations Pack

**Organization:** [[ORGANIZATION_NAME]]

This pack operationalizes the Institutional Integrity & Anti-Coercion Framework v0.1. It is designed so the organization can begin with enforceable safeguards rather than promises that depend on the goodwill of a founder or future leader.

## Architecture

1. **Charter / constitutional layer** - formation status, 501(c)(3) guardrails, board independence, founder non-sovereignty, core conscience rights, and entrenchment.
2. **Bylaws** - board structure, officers, committees, reserved powers, removal, due process, financial integrity, safeguarding, and amendment mechanics.
3. **Board schedules and covenants** - founder covenant, independence standard, reserved powers, conflict rules.
4. **Operational policies and SOPs** - complaints, investigations, safeguarding, financial controls, compensation, doctrine/revelation, pastoral ethics, exit, and external review.
5. **Forms and registers** - complaint intake, safeguarding report, evidence log, conflict disclosures, compensation memo, appeals, retaliation report, policy exceptions, and action tracking.
6. **Dashboard** - a separate XLSX workbook tracks quarterly integrity metrics, exceptions, complaints, safeguarding, governance independence, training, finance and external-review actions.

## Corporate membership decision

Tennessee requires the charter to state whether the corporation **will or will not have members**. This package therefore contains two charter variants and two governance schedules. The anti-coercion protections are identical in both. The membership decision should be made deliberately with counsel before filing.

- `governance/02_model_charter_member.md` - statutory voting-member model.
- `governance/03_model_charter_nonmember.md` - no-statutory-member model.
- `governance/05_membership_variant_schedule.md` and `06_nonmember_variant_schedule.md` - corresponding bylaw differences.

## Adoption sequence

1. Select the member or nonmember corporate model.
2. Have Tennessee nonprofit counsel conform the charter/bylaws to the filing, theology, tax posture, and actual founding board.
3. Fill all bracketed placeholders.
4. Seat the initial independent board and adopt the conflict/independence certifications before approving compensation or related-party transactions.
5. Adopt the policy suite and activate external complaint channels before public launch.
6. Complete safeguarding training before any program serving minors or vulnerable adults.
7. Begin quarterly dashboard review and annual integrity certification.
8. Commission the first external integrity review within 18 months of public launch, then at least every three years.

## Legal status

These are governance drafts, not a substitute for legal advice. They intentionally exceed many legal minimums. If a provision conflicts with applicable law, court order, insurer requirement, or valid employment obligation, qualified counsel should conform the language while preserving the underlying anti-coercion purpose to the maximum lawful extent.
