# Compliance Calendar - Baseline

## Monthly
- Bank reconciliation prepared and independently reviewed.
- Restricted-fund reconciliation.
- Payroll/tax deposits and filings if employees exist.
- Complaint/safeguarding case-status review by authorized oversight.
- Access/offboarding review for personnel changes.

## Quarterly
- Board financial packet and budget-to-actual.
- Institutional Integrity Dashboard.
- Conflict/related-party transaction review.
- Complaint, retaliation, safeguarding and policy-exception trend review.
- Board independence calculation.
- Public transparency page accuracy check.

## Annually
- Tennessee Secretary of State annual report: generally due on/before first day of fourth month after fiscal year close, subject to current SOS rule.
- Director/officer conflict and independence disclosures.
- Leadership Integrity Certifications.
- Board self-assessment and committee charter review.
- Compensation review for insiders/senior leaders.
- Insurance renewal and abuse/safeguarding coverage review.
- Safeguarding training and screening refresh as policy/insurer requires.
- Donation-receipt year-end process and donor statements.
- Tennessee solicitation-registration/exemption status re-check.
- Tax filings/information returns applicable to actual classification and activities.
- Records-retention/destruction cycle after hold review.

## Every three years or sooner
- External Institutional Integrity Review (first within 18 months of public launch, then at least every three years).

## Event-driven
- Charter/bylaw changes.
- New location/property -> zoning, occupancy, property-tax exemption, insurance.
- Hiring -> payroll/workers' comp/employment compliance.
- Child/youth program -> full safeguarding activation before service.
- Communal housing -> separate housing/legal/safety controls before occupancy.
- Material unrelated business -> tax/accounting review.
- Major gift/restricted/endowment -> gift-acceptance review.
- Complaint against senior leader -> independent routing.
