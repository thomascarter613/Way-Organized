# Banking, Depository & Payment-Control Resolution

**Corporation:** [[ORGANIZATION_NAME]]

## Depository authorization
The Corporation may establish checking, savings, money-market, merchant-processing, and other lawful financial accounts in its legal name and EIN at institutions approved by the Finance Committee/Board.

## Authorized persons
Initial bank signers / administrators: `[[NAMES / ROLES]]`.

No signer obtains ownership of funds. Access ends promptly upon termination of authority.

## Control matrix

| Transaction | Required authorization |
|---|---|
| Routine budgeted payment below `[[THRESHOLD]]` | one authorized approver + ordinary documentation |
| Payment at/above `[[THRESHOLD]]` | two authorized approvals |
| Payment to founder/director/officer/related party | disinterested approval + documentation regardless of amount |
| New bank/merchant/investment account | Board or Finance Committee as delegated |
| Change of signer/admin | two-authority confirmation; prompt removal of former access |
| Wire/ACH to new vendor above `[[THRESHOLD]]` | out-of-band verification + dual approval |
| Borrowing, lien, guarantee, real-property transaction | Board Reserved Power; legal review as appropriate |

## Prohibitions
- no personal accounts for corporate funds;
- no shared credentials where individual credentials are available;
- no blank checks/pre-signed payment instruments;
- no cash withdrawal without documented business purpose and approval;
- no founder/executive self-approval of reimbursement or compensation;
- no destruction of bank/payment records to avoid oversight.
