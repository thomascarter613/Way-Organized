**RESOLVED**, that the v0.2 policy and SOP suite is adopted effective immediately, including conflicts/related parties, compensation, financial controls, safeguarding, complaints/whistleblowing, independent investigations, discipline/appeals, exit/non-retaliation, pastoral ethics, revelation/doctrine, external integrity review, and annual leadership certification.

## Resolution 6 - Fiscal year and accounting

**RESOLVED**, that the Corporation's initial fiscal year ends `[[MONTH DAY; default December 31]]`; that books shall be maintained on `[[cash/accrual]]` basis as advised by the Corporation's accountant; and that the Treasurer/Finance Committee shall implement the v0.2 Financial Controls SOP.

## Resolution 7 - EIN and tax-exempt preparation

**RESOLVED**, that after legal formation an authorized principal officer may obtain an EIN directly from the IRS and preserve the confirmation notice in the permanent records.

**RESOLVED**, that officers are authorized to prepare, but not submit without Board/counsel approval, Form 1023 and related schedules/supporting documents for recognition under Section 501(c)(3). The Board shall separately approve the requested public-charity/church classification after reviewing the factual record.

## Resolution 8 - Banking and payment authority

**RESOLVED**, that corporate bank/depository accounts may be opened only in the Corporation's legal name and EIN; no corporate funds shall be held in a founder's, officer's, participant's, or employee's personal account.

**RESOLVED**, that bank signers and electronic-payment administrators are designated in the Banking Resolution, subject to dual-approval and related-party controls adopted by the Board.

## Resolution 9 - Complaint and safeguarding channels

**RESOLVED**, that no public launch serving participants shall occur until ordinary and independent complaint channels are active and published; and no activity serving minors/vulnerable persons shall begin until the safeguarding lead, independent escalation path, screening/training, and external-reporting protocol are active.

## Resolution 10 - Tennessee compliance

**RESOLVED**, that officers shall maintain a compliance calendar including the Tennessee Secretary of State annual report deadline, state tax/exemption applications applicable to the Corporation, charitable-solicitation registration/exemption status, and any local licensing obligations.

## Resolution 11 - Corporate Transparency Act / BOI

**RESOLVED**, that the Secretary shall document the current federal BOI rule in the compliance file. As of the source check dated September 19, 2026, FinCEN states that U.S.-created companies are exempt from BOI reporting under the final rule effective August 14, 2026. The Secretary shall re-check the rule if law/guidance changes.

## Resolution 12 - Insurance and risk transfer

**RESOLVED**, that the Corporation shall obtain quotations/advice appropriate to its activities, considering general liability, directors and officers liability, employment-practices liability, abuse/molestation coverage, workers' compensation when applicable, property/auto/cyber coverage, and professional/pastoral exposures. Coverage selections shall be documented; lack of affordable coverage does not authorize bypassing safeguarding controls.

## Resolution 13 - Corporate record book

**RESOLVED**, that the Secretary shall maintain the Corporate Record Book & Compliance Binder v0.3 and document hierarchy register, including permanent records of charter, bylaws, amendments, board/member actions, key tax determinations, and required accounting/governance records.

## Resolution 14 - First 90 days

**RESOLVED**, that management and committees shall execute the v0.2 implementation queue, including expansion to 7-11 directors with at least two-thirds independence, tabletop tests, independent complaint-channel test, first quarterly integrity dashboard, and publication of participant rights and reporting channels.
