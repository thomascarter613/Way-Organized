# Board Annual Governance Cycle

## Q1
- Annual disclosures and independence determinations.
- Prior-year financial close / outside accountant plan.
- Leadership Integrity Certifications.
- Compensation review before changes take effect.
- Annual transparency report approval.

## Q2
- Safeguarding program audit and training status.
- Board skills/succession matrix.
- Policy/document hierarchy review.
- Donation/fundraising compliance review.

## Q3
- Strategic/mission review separated from founder personality or succession.
- External integrity-review readiness / remediation status.
- Insurance renewal preparation.
- Technology/access-control review.

## Q4
- Next-year budget and reserves.
- Committee effectiveness and Board self-assessment.
- Annual compliance calendar reset.
- Fiscal-year-end donor acknowledgment and records plan.

## Every meeting
- conflicts/recusals;
- safeguarding/retaliation exceptions requiring Board knowledge;
- dashboard red flags;
- related-party activity;
- overdue remediation;
- closed executive session where necessary, without using executive session to conceal systemic misconduct.
