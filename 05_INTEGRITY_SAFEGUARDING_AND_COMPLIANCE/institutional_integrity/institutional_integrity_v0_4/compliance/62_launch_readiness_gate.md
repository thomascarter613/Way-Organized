# Public Launch Readiness Gate

A public website may exist before every operational program launches, but no program should claim readiness that the controls do not support.

## Governance - must pass
- [ ] Charter filed and effective.
- [ ] Bylaws/variant schedule adopted.
- [ ] Initial board seated and independence documented.
- [ ] Founder Covenant signed if founder serves.
- [ ] Reserved Powers and conflict policies active.

## Money - before accepting donations
- [ ] EIN and corporate bank account.
- [ ] Bookkeeping and restricted-fund system.
- [ ] Donation receipt/disclosure process.
- [ ] Tennessee charitable-solicitation status documented.
- [ ] Public statement does not falsely imply IRS determination before received.

## People - before ordinary participation
- [ ] Participant Bill of Rights published.
- [ ] Normal + independent complaint channels active/tested.
- [ ] Privacy/confidentiality limits disclosed appropriately.
- [ ] Exit/non-retaliation process active.

## Minors/vulnerable persons - before first program
- [ ] Safeguarding lead and independent escalation.
- [ ] Screening/background checks per policy.
- [ ] Training complete.
- [ ] Two-adult/observable environment rules.
- [ ] Mandatory-reporting quick card current.
- [ ] Insurance review complete.

## Paid leadership - before first payment
- [ ] Role description.
- [ ] Conflict-free decision makers.
- [ ] Comparability data.
- [ ] Total compensation inventory.
- [ ] Advance approval/minutes.

## Communal property/housing - before acquisition/occupancy
- [ ] Separate legal/financial analysis.
- [ ] Written occupancy/lease/license terms.
- [ ] No housing dependence conditioned on silence, religious obedience, or surrender of legal rights.
- [ ] Safety/zoning/insurance/accessibility review.
