# Tennessee Pre-Filing Checklist - Domestic Public Benefit Religious Nonprofit

**Working filing posture:** Tennessee public benefit religious nonprofit corporation; no statutory members at formation; fiscal year December 31.

This is a drafting/control checklist, not a substitute for Tennessee counsel or the Secretary of State's current filing instructions.

## A. Human prerequisites

- [ ] Final legal corporate name cleared.
- [ ] Incorporator identified and legal name confirmed.
- [ ] At least three natural-person directors identified for organization, including the Founder plus at least two additional directors.
- [ ] Separate President and Secretary identified before organizational completion; one individual will not simultaneously hold both offices.
- [ ] Registered agent consents to serve.
- [ ] Independent-Chair recruitment is underway even if not yet completed.

## B. Address information

- [ ] Principal office street/mailing information finalized.
- [ ] Registered office in Tennessee finalized.
- [ ] Registered agent name exactly matches the filing.
- [ ] Public-record/privacy implications reviewed before inserting home addresses.

## C. Charter fields and substantive clauses

Confirm the filing-ready charter contains or correctly addresses:

- [ ] corporate name;
- [ ] public-benefit status;
- [ ] statement that the corporation is religious, as applicable;
- [ ] registered office and agent;
- [ ] incorporator name/address/signature fields required by the current form;
- [ ] principal office information;
- [ ] nonprofit status statement;
- [ ] statement that the corporation **will not have statutory members** at formation;
- [ ] 501(c)(3)-compatible exempt-purpose language;
- [ ] prohibition on private inurement/private benefit consistent with federal tax requirements;
- [ ] political-campaign prohibition and lobbying limitation consistent with federal tax requirements;
- [ ] 501(c)(3)-compatible dissolution/dedication-of-assets language;
- [ ] entrenched founder non-sovereignty / conscience safeguards that counsel determines are appropriate for charter placement;
- [ ] director selection mechanism consistent with the nonmember bylaws.

## D. Cross-document consistency

Before filing, compare the charter against:

- [ ] bylaws and nonmember schedule;
- [ ] Institutional Integrity & Anti-Coercion Framework;
- [ ] Founder Covenant;
- [ ] Reserved Powers Schedule;
- [ ] board-independence standard;
- [ ] Form 1023 narrative and planned public-charity classification;
- [ ] legal/public naming decision;
- [ ] fiscal-year choice.

No subordinate document should grant a power that the charter withholds or remove a right that the charter entrenches.

## E. Filing economics and evidence

Current Tennessee Secretary of State fee schedule checked September 19, 2026:

- domestic nonprofit **Charter (SS-4418): $100**;
- **Application for Name Reservation (SS-9425): $20** if used.

Re-check the live SOS fee page immediately before filing. Save the submitted charter, payment confirmation, stamped/accepted charter, state control number, and any correspondence in the permanent record book.

## F. Do not do before acceptance

- Do not represent the Tennessee corporation as already existing before the charter becomes effective.
- Do not sign ordinary contracts in the unformed corporation's name without counsel addressing pre-incorporation liability/ratification.
- Do not open a corporate bank account before the entity and EIN/bank requirements exist.
- Do not publish a tax-deductibility representation that exceeds the organization's actual federal status.

## G. Immediately after state formation

1. Obtain EIN using the accepted legal name.
2. Convene the organizational board meeting or execute the lawful organizational consents.
3. Adopt bylaws, integrity framework, policies, banking/signing controls, fiscal year, and record-book architecture.
4. Elect/appoint officers, ensuring President and Secretary are different people.
5. Complete director/officer conflicts and independence disclosures.
6. Activate bookkeeping and document-retention systems.
7. Prepare/complete the full Form 1023 evidence package.
8. Confirm state/local charitable-solicitation, tax, employment, zoning, insurance, and program-specific obligations before the relevant activities begin.
