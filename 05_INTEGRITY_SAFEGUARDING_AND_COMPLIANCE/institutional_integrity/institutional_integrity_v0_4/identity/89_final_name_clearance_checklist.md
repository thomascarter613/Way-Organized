# Final Name Clearance Checklist

**Purpose:** Convert a three-name finalist list into one filing candidate without treating a web search as legal clearance.

## Gate 1 - Identity fit

For each finalist, record:

- exact public spelling, capitalization, punctuation, and pronunciation;
- proposed Tennessee corporate name and legal suffix;
- one-sentence description: `[[NAME]] is a religious community devoted to worship, inquiry, formation, service, and responsible study.`;
- whether the name still works for research/publication, pastoral care, charitable service, clergy formation, property, and local assemblies;
- whether a reasonable outsider could mistake the organization for an existing denomination, government body, university, or another organization.

Reject a candidate that requires a long explanation merely to avoid a misleading impression.

## Gate 2 - Tennessee entity screen

Immediately before selection/filing:

1. Search the Tennessee Secretary of State business database for the exact name and confusingly similar roots.
2. Confirm that the proposed corporate name is distinguishable on the Secretary of State's records.
3. Review language for false implications of governmental or organizational affiliation/sponsorship.
4. If the candidate is selected but filing will not occur promptly, consider the current four-month name-reservation mechanism.
5. Save a dated search record or screenshot in the permanent formation file.

Current Tennessee SOS fee schedule checked September 19, 2026: domestic nonprofit name reservation **$20**; nonprofit charter **$100**. Re-check immediately before payment.

## Gate 3 - Trademark and common-law conflict screen

- Search USPTO federal trademark records for exact and similar wording in relevant service classes.
- Search state trademark/business records where counsel recommends.
- Search the open web, news, social platforms, nonprofit directories, religious directories, books/publications, podcasts, and domain history for common-law users.
- Search singular/plural, spacing, hyphenation, abbreviations, phonetic equivalents, and distinctive root words separately.
- Escalate any material same-field use to trademark counsel before selection.

## Gate 4 - Digital identity

For each surviving finalist, record availability or current ownership of:

- primary `.org` and reasonable alternate domain(s);
- common social handles;
- major search-engine result ambiguity;
- email-domain usability;
- obvious typo/confusion risks.

Domain availability is **not** trademark clearance and a trademark result is **not** domain ownership.

## Gate 5 - Outside perception test

Give the name, without explanation, to at least five people who are not involved in the project. Ask:

1. What kind of organization do you think this is?
2. Does it sound religious, political, commercial, academic, or something else?
3. Does it sound controlling, secretive, grandiose, exclusionary, or misleading?
4. Can you pronounce and spell it after hearing it once?
5. What existing organization or tradition does it remind you of?

Record responses; do not argue with them.

## Gate 6 - Selection record

The final board/authorized organizer should retain a short naming memo stating:

- finalists considered;
- searches performed and dates;
- conflicts found and disposition;
- public name;
- legal corporate name;
- assumed-name filing decision if relevant;
- domain(s) authorized for acquisition;
- counsel review, if obtained;
- approving resolution/date.
