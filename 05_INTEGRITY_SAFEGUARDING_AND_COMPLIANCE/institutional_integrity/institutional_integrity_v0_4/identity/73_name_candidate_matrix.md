# Name Candidate Matrix - Working Round 1

This is a **creative and collision-screening worksheet**, not trademark or legal clearance. The web screen was performed September 19, 2026.

| Candidate | Institutional fit | Strength | Main caution | Limited web-screen status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Assembly of Sacred Inquiry** | Religious community + research + worship | Clearly combines communal gathering with disciplined inquiry | "Sacred Inquiry" is used by an existing religion/spirituality blog; exact full phrase did not surface prominently | **Advance to finalist screen** |
| **Community of Sacred Inquiry** | Community-forward, less hierarchical | Warm and accessible | Same "Sacred Inquiry" root collision; sounds slightly less like an organized religious body | **Advance to finalist screen** |
| **House of Sacred Inquiry** | Physical/digital religious house + scholarship | Scales well to a place and institution | "House" may imply a physical site before one exists | **Advance to finalist screen** |
| **Shared Horizon Assembly** | Plural perspectives under one community | Strong metaphor for inquiry without doctrinal closure | "Shared Horizon" is used in consulting/other contexts; less explicitly religious | **Advance with caution** |
| **Open Horizon Assembly** | Openness, transcendence, future orientation | Neutral across traditions | May sound educational/civic rather than religious | **Advance with caution** |
| **Threshold Assembly** | A community at the boundary between traditions and inquiry | Distinctive and memorable | "Threshold assembly" is also an industrial phrase; "threshold" can sound esoteric | **Secondary candidate** |
| **Fellowship of Sacred Inquiry** | Community and shared practice | Warm | "Fellowship" reads Christian to many U.S. audiences | **Secondary candidate** |
| **The Sacred Commons** | Shared sacred life | Strong concept | Already used by an active church/community and other projects | **Do not advance** |
| **The Common Assembly** | Neutral communal term | Simple | Existing U.S. 501(c)(3) uses this exact name | **Do not advance** |
| **Open Sanctuary** | Welcoming spiritual place | Strong public meaning | Multiple active religious/spiritual organizations use it | **Do not advance** |
| **Common Sanctuary** | Shared religious refuge | Strong concept | Existing religious community uses it | **Do not advance** |

## Current working leader

**Assembly of Sacred Inquiry** is the strongest *working* candidate because "assembly" describes a gathered people without committing the project to a single inherited institutional form, while "sacred inquiry" preserves the project's commitment to religious seriousness and continuing investigation.

It should remain a working candidate until Tennessee name, trademark, domain, and broader common-law screening are completed.

## Alternative architecture if the final community name is more poetic

If a highly symbolic public name is later chosen, the legal entity can still use a transparent descriptive form such as:

`[[DISTINCTIVE_NAME]] Religious and Charitable Corporation, Inc.`

while publicly using `[[DISTINCTIVE_NAME]]`. Counsel should confirm the final legal suffix/style and whether an assumed-name filing is needed.
