# Records Retention & Destruction Schedule - Governance Baseline

**Important:** This is a governance baseline, not a litigation/tax/insurance legal opinion. A litigation hold, investigation hold, safeguarding need, contract requirement, insurer requirement, grant condition, or legal rule overrides routine destruction.

## Permanent
- Charter, amendments, bylaws, major governance schedules.
- Board/member formal actions and organizational records.
- EIN and IRS exemption application/determination materials.
- Annual external integrity review final reports and Board remediation summaries.
- Major property deeds, intellectual-property registrations, historical donor restrictions attached permanently to assets/endowments.

## Long-term (minimum target 7 years unless counsel specifies longer)
- Financial statements, general ledger, bank statements/reconciliations, invoices/receipts, grant records, donation acknowledgments.
- Conflict disclosures, related-party and compensation approval files.
- Material contracts after expiration.
- Insurance policies and claim correspondence.

## Personnel
Maintain personnel/payroll/benefit/eligibility records under applicable federal/state retention rules and counsel/HR guidance; do not use a one-size-fits-all destruction date.

## Safeguarding / abuse / serious misconduct
Do not routinely destroy serious safeguarding, abuse, retaliation, or investigation records on a short schedule. Use restricted access and counsel/insurer-approved retention, considering delayed reporting, statutes of limitation, survivor interests, and institutional accountability.

## Destruction controls
- scheduled and documented;
- suspended immediately by legal/investigation hold;
- secure destruction appropriate to sensitivity;
- no selective destruction after a complaint, audit, subpoena, anticipated dispute, or request for evidence.
