# Secure Records Room / Repository Setup

## Folder architecture
- 00 Permanent Corporate
- 10 Board & Committees
- 20 Tax & Regulatory
- 30 Finance & Banking
- 40 Development / Restricted Gifts
- 50 HR / Volunteers (restricted)
- 60 Safeguarding (highly restricted)
- 70 Complaints / Investigations (highly restricted)
- 80 Legal / Privilege (restricted to counsel-authorized)
- 90 Public Transparency
- 99 Archive / Superseded

## Access rules
- least privilege;
- named accounts, no shared master password;
- MFA for sensitive systems;
- board/integrity committee independent access to records needed for oversight;
- founder/chief executive cannot be sole administrator of corporate records;
- immutable/exported copies of signed minutes/resolutions and filed documents;
- routine backup plus tested recovery;
- access log for safeguarding/complaint case repositories when technology permits.

## Offboarding
Disable credentials promptly, recover corporate devices/keys, change shared secrets where unavoidable, preserve records, and document transfer of fiduciary materials.
