# Financial Controls and Stewardship SOP

## Minimum controls

- Board-approved annual budget and quarterly budget-to-actual review.
- Separate roles for authorization, custody/payment, bookkeeping, and reconciliation to the extent staffing permits.
- Monthly bank reconciliations reviewed by someone who did not prepare them.
- Dual approval for payments above a board-set threshold and for all insider/related-party payments.
- No blank checks, shared banking credentials, undocumented cash funds, or personal accounts used as corporate accounts.
- Expense reimbursements require receipts/business purpose; insider exceptions require disinterested approval.
- Restricted gifts tracked by fund/restriction.
- Asset register for real property, vehicles, major equipment, and high-value portable items.
- Written reserves and investment policy once liquid assets become material.
- Quarterly financial packet: statement of activities, financial position, cash report, budget variance, restricted funds, major payables, debt, and related-party activity.
- Annual outside-accountant review level determined by board based on size/risk, with a goal of progressively stronger assurance as resources grow.

## Donation integrity
Giving is voluntary. Fundraising shall not promise spiritual status, healing, salvation, prophetic access, protection from supernatural harm, office, or preferential treatment in exchange for money/property.
