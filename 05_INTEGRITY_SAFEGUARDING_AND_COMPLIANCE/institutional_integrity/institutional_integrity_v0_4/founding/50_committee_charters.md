# Standing Board Committee Charters

## A. Governance & Nominating Committee

**Purpose:** preserve board independence, succession integrity, governing-document compliance, director development, and conflicts administration.

**Core duties:**
- recruit/screen directors against independence criteria;
- administer annual conflict/independence disclosures;
- maintain skills/representation matrix without ideological loyalty tests that undermine oversight;
- oversee board self-assessment and succession;
- review proposed amendments for rights/power-concentration effects;
- recommend removal/suspension processes where governance failures arise.

**Composition:** at least three directors; majority independent; chair must be independent. Founder/chief executive may not chair or constitute a majority.

## B. Finance, Audit & Stewardship Committee

**Purpose:** protect charitable assets and independent financial oversight.

**Core duties:** budget, financial reporting, bank-control review, restricted funds, reserves, external accountant engagement, insider compensation files, related-party transactions, whistleblower coordination for financial matters.

**Composition:** at least three directors; all voting participants on insider compensation/related-party matters must be disinterested. Treasurer may attend; executive management is not a voting member unless the Board expressly permits on non-conflicted matters.

## C. Safeguarding & Institutional Integrity Committee

**Purpose:** independent oversight of safeguarding, complaints against senior leadership, retaliation, investigations, pastoral-power abuse, and external integrity review.

**Authority:** direct access to the Board; authority to retain independent counsel/investigators within Board-approved budget or emergency authority; access to relevant records subject to privilege/privacy controls; power to recommend interim measures.

**Composition:** at least three directors, all independent for matters involving the founder/chief executive. No person implicated in a matter may participate in its oversight.

## D. Doctrine, Research & Epistemic Integrity Council (advisory)

**Purpose:** maintain transparent distinction among evidence, historical inference, philosophical interpretation, theological hypothesis, communal teaching, and personal revelation.

**Limits:** it is not a statutory board committee unless separately constituted; it cannot suspend civil law, safeguarding, financial controls, participant rights, or Board fiduciary duties; it cannot adjudicate complaints against its own members without independent oversight.
