# Action of Incorporator(s)

**Corporation:** [[ORGANIZATION_NAME]]  
**Effective date:** `[[DATE AFTER CHARTER ACCEPTANCE]]`

This action is intended for use when the filed charter does **not** name the initial directors. Tennessee law permits incorporators, after incorporation, to elect directors and complete organization or elect a board that will complete organization. Tennessee also permits incorporator action without a meeting when all incorporators consent and the action is recorded in the corporate records.
