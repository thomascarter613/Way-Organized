# Tax, Regulatory & Filing Resolutions

## EIN
After legal formation, an authorized principal officer shall obtain an EIN directly from the IRS or by Form SS-4. The EIN confirmation shall be preserved permanently. No EIN shall be sought before legal formation.

## Federal 501(c)(3)
Officers may prepare a Form 1023 application and supporting narrative/budgets. Submission requires Board approval after review of:
- organizing-document language;
- actual/proposed activities;
- compensation and related-party arrangements;
- fundraising and public-support assumptions;
- requested public-charity classification;
- Schedule A facts if church classification is requested.

If requesting recognition as a church, do not use Form 1023-EZ under current IRS instructions.

## Tennessee annual report
The Secretary shall calendar the Tennessee nonprofit annual report. Current Tennessee law states that a nonprofit corporation generally files on or before the first day of the fourth month following the close of its fiscal year, unless the Secretary of State sets another date by rule.

## Charitable solicitations
Before public fundraising, determine and document whether the Corporation qualifies as a bona fide religious institution exempt from Tennessee charitable-solicitation registration or must register/claim another exemption. Do not assume that incorporation alone establishes the solicitation exemption.

## Tennessee taxes/exemptions
After federal status and operational facts are available, evaluate/apply as appropriate for:
- Tennessee sales/use tax certificate of exemption for qualifying nonprofit purchases;
- franchise/excise tax treatment and unrelated-business considerations;
- business/sales tax duties for taxable sales;
- property-tax exemption for qualifying property actually used for exempt purposes.

## Federal BOI/CTA
As of the September 19, 2026 source check, FinCEN states that domestic U.S.-created entities are exempt from BOI reporting under the final rule effective August 14, 2026. Record this conclusion and re-check after any material legal change.
