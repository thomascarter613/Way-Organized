# First 90-Day Activation Plan

## Days 0-10 after incorporation
- Complete organizational meeting/consent.
- Obtain EIN; preserve CP575/confirmation.
- Open corporate bank account after resolutions.
- Activate bookkeeping/chart of accounts and restricted-fund classes.
- Publish Participant Bill of Rights and complaint channels.
- Execute Founder Covenant and all director/officer acceptances.
- Establish secure corporate record repository with access controls.

## Days 11-30
- Complete Form 1023 classification evidence file and draft activity narrative/budgets.
- Confirm Tennessee charitable-solicitation status before broad fundraising.
- Obtain insurance proposals.
- Implement donation acknowledgment templates.
- Complete initial training: fiduciary, conflicts, safeguarding, anti-retaliation, complaint routing.
- Run founder-complaint tabletop and child-safety tabletop.

## Days 31-60
- Recruit toward 7-11 director board and >=2/3 independence.
- Test anonymous/independent reporting channel.
- Finalize vendor/payment matrix and monthly close checklist.
- Conduct policy exception audit: target zero exceptions affecting participant rights/safeguarding.
- Build website transparency page: rights, board, reporting, finances/governance summary.

## Days 61-90
- Produce first quarterly integrity dashboard.
- Board reviews compensation/related-party files, if any.
- Complete records-retention and privacy/security review.
- Schedule first external integrity review for no later than 18 months after public launch.
- Approve remediation plan for any failed tabletop/control tests.
