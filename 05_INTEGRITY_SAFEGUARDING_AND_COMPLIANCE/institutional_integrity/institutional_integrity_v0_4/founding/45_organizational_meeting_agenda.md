# Organizational Meeting - Master Agenda

## Pre-meeting evidence file
- Filed Charter and Secretary of State acceptance.
- Director roster and independence/conflict certifications.
- v0.2 Constitutional Governance Package.
- v0.2 Day-One Integrity Operations Manual.
- Founder Covenant.
- Proposed officer slate.
- Committee charters.
- Proposed fiscal/banking/accounting resolutions.
- Filing Decision Gate memorandum.

## Agenda
1. Call to order; confirm notice/quorum.
2. Secretary pro tempore and meeting chair.
3. Confirm filed Charter and incorporator action/initial directors.
4. Record conflicts, recusals, and director-independence determinations.
5. Adopt bylaws and the selected Member or Nonmember Variant Schedule.
6. Adopt Institutional Integrity & Anti-Coercion Framework and Participant Bill of Rights.
7. Adopt Board Reserved Powers Schedule and Board Independence Standard.
8. Adopt Founder Covenant and receive founder signature.
9. Elect/appoint officers.
10. Establish standing board committees and appoint initial members.
11. Adopt conflicts, compensation, finance, safeguarding, complaints, investigations, discipline/appeals, exit/non-retaliation, pastoral ethics, revelation/doctrine, and external-review policies.
12. Establish fiscal year and accounting basis; authorize bookkeeping and financial controls.
13. Authorize EIN application and federal 501(c)(3) preparation.
14. Authorize bank account(s), signers, payment controls, and restricted-fund accounting.
15. Address Tennessee charitable-solicitation exemption/registration review.
16. Address Tennessee tax accounts/exemptions and future property-tax applications if applicable.
17. Confirm current federal BOI/CTA status review.
18. Activate independent complaint and safeguarding escalation channels.
19. Approve insurance procurement review.
20. Establish corporate record book and records-retention schedule.
21. Approve first-90-day implementation plan and dashboard cadence.
22. Other properly presented business.
23. Executive session if needed for compensation/personnel/conflict matters.
24. Adjournment.
