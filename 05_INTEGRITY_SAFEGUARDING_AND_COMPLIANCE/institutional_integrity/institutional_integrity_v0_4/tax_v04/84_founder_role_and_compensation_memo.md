# Founder Role & Compensation - Working Governance Memo

## Proposed role architecture

**Founder** - historical designation only; carries no veto, property ownership, life tenure, doctrinal infallibility, succession power, or immunity from discipline.  
**President / Executive Director** - operational executive accountable to the Board.  
**Research & Religious Formation Lead** - functional portfolio that may be held by the President initially, subject to doctrine/revelation process controls and future delegation.

The Founder should **not** serve as independent Board Chair. The Founder may serve as a director while employed as President/Executive Director but is treated as non-independent.

## Initial compensation

Working baseline:

> The Founder serves without salary until the corporation has an independent Board capable of approving compensation prospectively and the organization has sufficient financial capacity. The Founder may receive reimbursement of documented, legitimate organizational expenses under the same accountable reimbursement policy applicable to other personnel.

This is not intended to create a permanent expectation of unpaid labor.

## Future compensation approval sequence

1. Board adopts a written job description and expected time commitment.
2. Founder discloses all compensation/benefit interests and leaves deliberation/vote.
3. Disinterested directors obtain appropriate comparability information.
4. Board considers the organization's resources, duties, geography, complexity, qualifications, and total compensation.
5. Disinterested directors approve compensation **before** it is paid or increased.
6. Minutes/compensation memo contemporaneously record decision makers, data, terms, recusals, and rationale.
7. Board reviews total compensation at least annually and on material role changes.

## Compensation components to count

Salary is only one element. The review must include housing, allowances, insurance, retirement, bonuses, deferred compensation, severance, personal-expense payments, loans, vehicles, education, and other economic benefits.

## Housing caution

If the organization later provides a residence to the Founder or clergy, the arrangement requires separate tax/legal analysis. It must not become an informal benefit outside the compensation file, and the Board must distinguish organizational property needs from private benefit.

## Founder intellectual property

Before publication or monetization becomes material, create an IP schedule distinguishing:

- pre-existing works/assets personally owned by the Founder;
- work created as an uncompensated volunteer for the corporation;
- work created within compensated employment;
- trademarks/domains/accounts owned by the corporation;
- any licenses/assignments between Founder and corporation.

Any material transfer or license with the Founder is a related-party transaction requiring independent review and fair terms.
