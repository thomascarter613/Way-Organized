# Form 1023 Part IV - Working Narrative of Activities

**Important:** This is a drafting narrative. Final filing language must reflect activities the organization actually conducts or has concrete plans to conduct. Percentages, locations, frequencies, fees, and staffing marked as working assumptions must be confirmed by the Board.

## 1. Regular religious gatherings and worship

The Organization will conduct public religious gatherings in person and/or online. Gatherings are intended to provide communal worship, prayer or contemplative practice, readings, reflection, teaching, music or other forms of religious expression, community discussion, and opportunities for charitable service. The form of worship and liturgical calendar are still being developed and will be documented before the Organization requests classification as a church.

**Conducted by:** Founder/President initially, invited teachers/facilitators, and later authorized clergy or trained leaders.  
**Where:** rented/community spaces and online platforms initially; future owned/leased facilities only when financially prudent.  
**Funding:** charitable contributions and grants; generally open without mandatory payment.  
**Exempt purpose:** advances religion, religious education, community, and charitable service.  
**Working time allocation:** `20%` - confirm before filing.

## 2. Religious study and educational programs

The Organization will conduct classes, seminars, reading groups, lectures, workshops, and structured educational materials concerning religious texts, history, comparative religion, theology, philosophy, ethics, spiritual practices, and related disciplines. Programs may be offered to adults, families, and eventually children subject to safeguarding controls.

Educational programs will not initially be represented as a school unless the Organization actually develops the regular faculty, enrolled student body, curriculum, and facilities relevant to school classification.

**Conducted by:** Founder/research leadership, qualified instructors, invited scholars/practitioners, trained volunteers.  
**Where:** online and rented/community facilities.  
**Funding:** donations/grants and, where appropriate, reasonable program fees with scholarships/waivers as adopted.  
**Exempt purpose:** religious and educational.  
**Working time allocation:** `15%` - confirm.

## 3. Historical and religious research

The Organization will conduct historical, textual, comparative, phenomenological, and interdisciplinary research concerning religious claims, traditions, practices, institutions, sacred texts, historical encounters, and the development of religious ideas. Research methods are intended to distinguish source evidence, inference, interpretation, theological hypothesis, communal teaching, and personal conviction.

Research may result in articles, reports, annotated source collections, educational materials, lectures, databases, or books. The Organization will not condition research conclusions on protecting the reputation of the Founder or institution.

**Conducted by:** Founder/research leader, future research staff, fellows, volunteers, advisers, and collaborating scholars under written arrangements where appropriate.  
**Funding:** contributions, grants, publication/program revenue where exempt-related, and restricted research gifts.  
**Exempt purpose:** religious, educational, and where applicable scholarly research available for public benefit.  
**Working time allocation:** `20%` - confirm.

## 4. Publication and digital religious/educational services

The Organization will maintain a website and digital services publishing religious, educational, historical, governance, and research materials. It may produce newsletters, essays, reports, books, podcasts, videos, digital courses, worship materials, and community resources. Public materials will identify corrections where material errors are discovered.

**Funding:** donations, grants, and reasonable sales/subscription/program revenue related to exempt activities.  
**Intellectual property:** ownership/licensing arrangements involving the Founder or other insiders will be documented in writing and approved through conflict/related-party procedures before material transactions occur.  
**Exempt purpose:** religious and educational dissemination.  
**Working time allocation:** `10%` - confirm.

## 5. Pastoral and spiritual care

The Organization will provide nonclinical pastoral/spiritual care, prayer, listening, spiritual direction, religious rites, and referrals. Pastoral personnel will follow the adopted ethics code, disclose qualifications, maintain boundaries/confidentiality subject to law, and refer medical/mental-health/legal/financial matters beyond their competence to qualified professionals.

No pastoral relationship will authorize control over an adult's marriage, healthcare, finances, residence, employment, family relationships, or legal decisions.

**Funding:** primarily donations; no person will be denied emergency/basic pastoral contact solely for inability to pay.  
**Working time allocation:** `10%` - confirm.

## 6. Charitable assistance and community service

The Organization plans to provide charitable assistance to persons in need and to conduct service projects. Assistance may include food, emergency needs, limited direct aid, referrals, or grants under written eligibility and documentation procedures. Assistance will not be conditioned on conversion, worship attendance, donations, loyalty to leadership, or silence about complaints.

Insiders and their families may not receive charitable assistance except under an independently reviewed policy demonstrating objective eligibility and avoidance of private benefit.

**Funding:** unrestricted/restricted charitable contributions and grants.  
**Working time allocation:** `10%` - confirm.

## 7. Retreats and community formation

The Organization plans periodic retreats, study intensives, community meals, service days, and other formation activities. Retreat participation will be voluntary; the Organization will not use sleep deprivation, food deprivation, isolation, confiscation of communications/identification, or coercive confession as commitment techniques.

**Funding:** donations and reasonable participant fees reflecting costs, with waivers/scholarships where feasible.  
**Working time allocation:** `5%` - confirm.

## 8. Clergy / religious-leader formation and ordination

The Organization intends to develop standards for recognizing or ordaining religious leaders. No instant/self-ordination or fee-only credential will be represented as evidence of training. Before launching ordination, the Board will adopt qualifications, education/formation requirements, supervised practice, ethics/safeguarding standards, complaint/discipline procedures, continuing formation, and credential removal/appeal rules.

**Funding:** donations, grants, and reasonable educational fees if adopted.  
**Working time allocation:** `5%` - confirm.

## 9. Children's religious/educational programming

The Organization plans age-appropriate religious and educational programs for children after its safeguarding system is fully active. Covered workers will complete screening/training, two-adult/observable-environment controls will apply, and Tennessee child-abuse reporting duties will not be displaced by religious processes.

**Working time allocation:** `5%` once activated; adjust the total allocation table when the program starts.

## 10. Intentional community, property acquisition, and housing - development-stage only

The Organization has a long-range plan to explore land/property acquisition, retreat facilities, intentional community, and possibly housing. **This activity is not ready for final Form 1023 narrative treatment until the Board adopts a concrete model** identifying beneficiaries, rents/fees, eligibility, resident rights, employment relationships, eviction/exit rules, property ownership, financing, safety, and the relationship between religious participation and housing.

Before activation, the Board will obtain separate nonprofit, housing, tax, land-use/zoning, employment, insurance, and safeguarding review. Housing will not be used to punish religious disagreement, complaints, or departure.

## Working allocation table

The current working allocation for Activities 1-8 is 95%. A children's-program allocation of 5% would bring the total to 100% once activated. If children's programming is not concrete at filing, reallocate that 5% among Activities 1-8 and omit Activity 9 as a substantial planned program until appropriate. Housing/property development is treated as a future capital/program-development item and is not assigned an operating-time percentage until its model is concrete.
