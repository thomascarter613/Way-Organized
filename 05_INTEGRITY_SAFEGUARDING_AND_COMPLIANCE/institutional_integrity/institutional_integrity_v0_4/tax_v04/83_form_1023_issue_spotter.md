# Form 1023 Issue Spotter & Evidence Checklist

## Organizational documents

- Filed charter with 501(c)(3)-compatible purpose and dissolution language.
- Bylaws and nonmember schedule.
- EIN confirmation.
- Board/officer roster and relationships.
- Conflict policy, Founder Covenant, compensation SOP, safeguarding/complaint policies.

## Part IV activity questions requiring final answers

| Issue | Current working position | Evidence / decision still required |
|---|---|---|
| Worship frequency | Planned | regular day/time and format once established |
| Locations | Online + rented/community space | city/venue approach; leases if any |
| Program fees | Possible reasonable fees | fee schedule / waiver policy |
| Children's programming | Planned after safeguards | launch timing, curriculum, personnel, screening |
| Charitable assistance | Planned | eligibility, approval limits, records, insider exclusion |
| Grants | Possible | grantmaking policy if material |
| Ordination | Planned | curriculum, prerequisites, supervision, standards |
| Housing/intentional community | Development stage | separate detailed legal/program design before filing as concrete activity |
| Intellectual property | Research/publication expected | inventory of pre-existing Founder IP and organizational ownership/license policy |
| Founder compensation | Deferred until independent Board approval | job description, hours, comparability data, compensation resolution |
| Family/business relationships | Unknown until board/staff recruited | disclosures |
| Fundraising | donations, grants, web giving, events | vendors, state solicitation analysis, donor benefits |
| Foreign activity | None stated | confirm |
| Political campaign activity | Organization will not intervene in campaigns | adopt compliance guidance |
| Lobbying | none currently planned as substantial | confirm / consider 501(h) advice only if future lobbying becomes material and eligibility permits |

## Financial-information gate

Because the organization will be newly formed, the current IRS instructions call for a total of **three years** of good-faith projected revenue and expenses when the applicant has completed less than one tax year. The linked v0.4 workbook is the working model.

## Website consistency gate

The final website should be reviewed against the Form 1023 before filing. IRS instructions expressly request the organization's website and expect public information to be consistent with the application.
