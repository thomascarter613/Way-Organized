# Federal Public-Charity Classification - Working Memo

## Working position

Prepare the full Form 1023 and initially request classification as a publicly supported religious organization under **IRC 509(a)(1) and 170(b)(1)(A)(vi)**, subject to the final three-year funding projections and tax-adviser review.

## Why not request church classification immediately?

The organization intends to develop worship, religious government, clergy/ordination, literature, education, and a congregation. But these institutional facts are still being formed. IRS Form 1023 instructions state that church classification uses facts and circumstances and generally requires a congregation or other religious membership group. The organization should not invent a congregation, regular services, ministerial training, history, or established place of worship to satisfy a checklist.

If actual operations mature into church facts, the Board can later evaluate whether to request reclassification.

## Why 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) is the current working choice

This classification generally applies where an organization normally receives a substantial part of support from governmental units, contributions from the general public, or public charities. The project presently anticipates charitable donations and grants as major funding sources rather than relying primarily on investment income or a small closed donor group.

The selection remains conditional because Form 1023 financial data must be consistent with the requested public-charity classification. The three-year projection workbook will test this assumption before filing.

## Form choice

Use the **full Form 1023**. Even if the organization might qualify for Form 1023-EZ as a non-church public charity at some point, the full application is the preferred governance record for this project because it forces disclosure and analysis of planned activities, insiders, compensation, financial arrangements, fundraising, grants, intellectual property, and public-support assumptions.

If the organization later applies specifically for recognition as a church, it is not eligible to use Form 1023-EZ for that request under current IRS instructions.

## Working NTEE code

The likely umbrella code is **X99 - Religion Related, N.E.C.** rather than X90 Interfaith Coalitions unless the actual organization becomes principally a coalition of separate faith organizations. Because the project is developing its own religious community rather than simply coordinating existing religions, X99 is the better working code. Confirm at filing.
