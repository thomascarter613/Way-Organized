# Open Decisions Register - After v0.4

## Decisions the Founder must ultimately make or help make

### Identity
- [ ] Select 3-5 name finalists.
- [ ] Select final public/legal name after clearance.

### People
- [ ] Identify at least two additional directors (legal minimum).
- [ ] Preferably identify four additional directors (formation target).
- [ ] Identify a Secretary separate from the President.
- [ ] Identify independent Chair candidate.

### Activities
- [ ] Confirm which listed programs are genuinely planned for the first 12-24 months rather than aspirations.
- [ ] Confirm worship/gathering format and expected frequency.
- [ ] Confirm whether children's programming is planned before or after Form 1023 filing.
- [ ] Decide whether ordination is an early program or later-stage program.
- [ ] Keep housing/intentional-community work in development stage until a concrete legal model exists.

### Money
- [ ] Complete initial three-year budget assumptions.
- [ ] Confirm expected fundraising channels and whether fees will be charged for any programs.
- [ ] Determine when a salaried Founder/President role becomes financially feasible; the Board, not Founder, sets the amount.

### Filing
- [ ] Insert registered/principal office immediately before Tennessee filing.
- [ ] Obtain counsel review of charter/bylaws and high-risk programs.
- [ ] After incorporation, obtain EIN and finalize Form 1023 evidence.

## Decisions intentionally *not* required yet

- exact future land/property location;
- detailed residential community rules;
- final church classification;
- a permanent worship building;
- a mature clergy hierarchy;
- a complete long-term doctrine;
- a seven-person board on day one, provided Tennessee's minimum and the formation plan are satisfied.
