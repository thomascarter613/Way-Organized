# v0.4 Selected Baseline - Provisional Organizational Decisions

These are design defaults for drafting. They do not become legally operative until adopted/filed by the appropriate persons.

| Topic | Working decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tennessee corporation type | Public benefit religious nonprofit corporation | Intended religious, educational and charitable purposes serve public rather than mutual/private benefit. |
| Statutory members | No statutory members at formation | Avoids creating a corporate electorate before a real community exists; participant rights remain protected by charter/bylaws/policies. |
| Board | 5-seat formation target; 7-11 mature board; >=2/3 independent mature board | Tennessee minimum is 3; stronger internal standard distributes power and supports founder accountability. |
| Founder | Founder + President/Executive Director + Director | Separates historical founder status from corporate sovereignty while allowing operational leadership. |
| Chair | Independent person other than Founder/President | Prevents executive and board oversight from collapsing into one office. |
| Secretary | Separate person from President | Tennessee law requires President and Secretary and forbids one person from holding both offices simultaneously. |
| Treasurer | Prefer independent/separate person | Financial authority should not be concentrated with spiritual/executive authority. |
| Federal application | Full Form 1023 | Provides a detailed record appropriate to a complex religious/educational/charitable organization. |
| Initial public-charity class | 509(a)(1)/170(b)(1)(A)(vi), subject to actual funding model | Intended reliance on donations/grants/public support is compatible with this classification; verify projections before filing. |
| Church classification | Defer until actual congregation/services/government/worship facts exist | IRS applies a facts-and-circumstances church analysis and generally expects a congregation or religious membership group meeting for worship. |
| Founder compensation | Initially unpaid except policy-compliant expense reimbursement; later prospective reasonable compensation after independent approval | Avoids self-dealing while preserving a path to legitimate paid full-time leadership. |
| Fiscal year | December 31 | Selected by Founder. |
| Filing sequence | Prepare -> recruit board -> select name -> counsel review -> file TN charter -> organize -> EIN -> banking -> Form 1023 -> public launch | Reduces avoidable amendments and avoids launching high-risk programs before controls exist. |
