# Founding Board Candidate Screening Questionnaire

Candidate: `[[NAME]]`  Date: `[[DATE]]`  Proposed role: `[[ROLE]]`

## Mission and governance

1. What interests you about governing this organization?
2. What concerns would you want resolved before joining?
3. How do you understand the difference between supporting a founder and exercising independent fiduciary judgment?
4. Could you vote against the Founder/President on compensation, discipline, policy, or strategy if your judgment required it?
5. Could you support an independent investigation of the Founder or another senior religious leader?
6. How comfortable are you with a religious organization that explicitly protects doubt, criticism, outside information, and voluntary exit?

## Time and competence

7. Can you reliably prepare for and attend board/committee meetings?
8. What experience do you bring in governance, finance, law, safeguarding, education, ministry/religion, research, nonprofit operations, fundraising, property, or community work?
9. What board training would you need?

## Conflicts / independence

10. Are you related by family or intimate relationship to the Founder or another proposed director/officer?
11. Do you have an employment, contracting, landlord/tenant, lending, investment, or business relationship with them or the organization?
12. Do you receive or expect significant financial support, housing, employment, pastoral counseling, or other dependency from the Founder or organization?
13. Do you have any relationship that could make it difficult to investigate, discipline, remove, or contradict a senior leader?
14. Identify any other actual or apparent conflict.

## Safeguarding and institutional ethics

15. Would you be willing to make or support an external child-safety report even if a respected leader objected?
16. What does retaliation against a complainant look like to you beyond firing or formal punishment?
17. What should happen if the institution's reputation conflicts with truthful disclosure of serious misconduct?

## Candidate questions

18. What would you need from the organization to serve responsibly?
19. What information would you want access to as a director?
20. What would cause you to resign?
