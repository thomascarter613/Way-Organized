# Priority Officer Profiles

## Independent Board Chair

The Chair should be capable of leading the Board **independently of the President/Founder**. Primary duties include agenda partnership, executive-session leadership, annual Founder/CEO evaluation, conflict escalation, succession/removal process integrity, and ensuring the Board itself follows the anti-coercion framework.

**Preferred background:** nonprofit governance, law, executive leadership, institutional ethics, or comparable fiduciary experience.  
**Must be able to say:** "I support the mission, but I do not work for the Founder."

## Secretary

The Secretary is the institutional-memory role: minutes, resolutions, charter/bylaws, registers, board notices, document versions, officer/director roster, annual-report calendar, and authentication of records.

**Preferred background:** administration, law, compliance, records, governance, or meticulous professional operations.  
**Critical legal point:** the President and Secretary cannot be the same person under current Tennessee nonprofit law.

## Treasurer / Finance Director

The Treasurer leads board-level financial oversight rather than personally doing every bookkeeping task. The role should ensure bank reconciliations, budget review, restricted-fund tracking, approval controls, compensation records, related-party review, and external accountant engagement.

**Preferred background:** accounting, finance, business operations, banking, nonprofit finance, audit, or strong financial literacy.

## Safeguarding & Institutional Integrity Director

This director is the board-level owner of complaint-channel independence, safeguarding oversight, retaliation monitoring, independent investigations, and external integrity review.

**Preferred background:** safeguarding, social services, HR, compliance, investigations, legal ethics, child protection, clinical administration, institutional risk, or comparable work. The person should not be under the Founder's pastoral, employment, housing, or financial control.
