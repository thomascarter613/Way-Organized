# Founding Board Invitation - Reusable Draft

Dear `[[NAME]]`,

I am developing a Tennessee nonprofit religious organization intended to combine religious community, worship, historical/religious research, education, publication, pastoral care, charitable service, and - only as the institution matures - programs such as retreats, clergy formation, children's programming, property, and intentional community.

I am looking for founding directors who will **govern the organization rather than simply support me personally**. A central design requirement is that the founder must remain accountable to an independent board. The governing framework expressly protects freedom of conscience, criticism, safe exit, external reporting, safeguarding, financial transparency, independent investigations, and removal of any leader, including the founder.

I would like to explore whether you might be willing to serve as a founding director, potentially in the role of `[[ROLE]]`.

The role involves real fiduciary responsibility. It does not require personal financial contributions, unquestioning agreement with me, or acceptance of every developing theological conclusion. It does require good-faith support for the organization's charitable/religious mission, willingness to exercise independent judgment, and commitment to the integrity safeguards that constrain leadership.

Before either of us commits, I would provide the draft charter/bylaws, participant Bill of Rights, founder limitations, board role description, conflict/independence questionnaire, and a clear description of expected time and responsibilities. We would then have a candid conversation about the project, its risks, and your questions.

If that conversation would interest you, I would value the opportunity to discuss it.

Sincerely,

`[[FOUNDER NAME]]`
