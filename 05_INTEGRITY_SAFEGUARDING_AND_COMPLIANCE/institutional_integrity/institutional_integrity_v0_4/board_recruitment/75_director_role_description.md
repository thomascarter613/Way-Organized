# Founding Director - Role Description

## Purpose of the role

A director governs the corporation and protects its religious/charitable mission, assets, people, integrity system, and long-term institutional independence. The role is not honorary and does not exist to ratify the Founder's preferences.

## Expected commitments

- Attend approximately `[[6-10]]` board meetings per year during formation, plus committee work.
- Review materials before meetings.
- Complete conflict/independence disclosure and annual integrity certification.
- Complete governance, safeguarding, financial-integrity, and anti-retaliation training appropriate to the role.
- Participate in executive sessions without management where needed.
- Maintain confidentiality while recognizing lawful reporting duties and non-concealment obligations.
- Serve an initial term of `[[2 or 3 years]]` under the final bylaws.

## Core duties

- adopt and oversee strategy, budget, policies, and executive leadership;
- hire/evaluate/remove the President/Executive Director where required;
- approve executive/founder compensation only through disinterested process;
- monitor conflicts and related-party transactions;
- ensure safeguarding and complaint systems remain independent;
- oversee significant property, debt, housing, grants, employment, and expansion decisions;
- preserve participant freedom of conscience, safe exit, due process, and non-retaliation;
- ensure doctrine/revelation processes do not bypass civil or corporate controls;
- ensure truthful public reporting and financial stewardship.

## What directors are not being asked to do

Directors are not required to:

- treat the Founder as a prophet, infallible teacher, or permanent leader;
- agree with every theological proposition;
- provide personal funding;
- assume daily operational management unless separately engaged;
- conceal misconduct to protect the organization;
- guarantee the success of the project.

## Compensation

Founding directors should ordinarily serve without compensation for board service, with policy-compliant reimbursement of reasonable expenses. Any future director compensation requires disinterested board analysis and documentation.
