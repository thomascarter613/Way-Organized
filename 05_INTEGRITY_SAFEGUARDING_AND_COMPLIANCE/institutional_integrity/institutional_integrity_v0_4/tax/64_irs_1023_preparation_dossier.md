# IRS Form 1023 Preparation Dossier

**Corporation:** [[ORGANIZATION_NAME]]

This dossier is an evidence-gathering workbook for tax counsel/CPA and the Board. It is not the IRS form itself.

## Part 1 - Legal organization
- Filed Charter and all amendments.
- Bylaws and member/nonmember schedule.
- EIN confirmation.
- Formation date and fiscal year.
- Directors/officers and relationships.

## Part 2 - Narrative of activities
For each current/planned substantial activity describe:
1. What is the activity?
2. Who conducts it?
3. Where?
4. What percentage of time/resources?
5. How is it funded?
6. Who benefits?
7. Are fees charged?
8. How does it further religious/charitable/educational purposes?

Planned activity families:
- communal worship/gatherings;
- study/religious education;
- historical phenomenology / comparative religion research;
- publication and digital education;
- pastoral/spiritual care within ethical scope;
- charitable relief/community service;
- eventual intentional-community/housing activities, only after separate legal design;
- youth/family activities, only after safeguarding activation.

## Part 3 - Compensation and insiders
Collect role descriptions, hours, compensation components, comparability data, conflicts, recusals, board approvals, housing/benefits, loans, leases, and family/vendor relationships.

## Part 4 - Fundraising
Document website giving, recurring donations, grants, events, major gifts, restricted funds, donor benefits, fundraising contractors, and charitable-solicitation status.

## Part 5 - Financial data
Prepare current-year actuals if any plus good-faith 3-5 year projections with assumptions. Separate contributions, program revenue, investment income, unrelated business, salaries, occupancy, grants, professional fees, technology, insurance, benevolence, and capital expenditures.

## Part 6 - Public charity / church classification decision

### If requesting church classification
Prepare Schedule A evidence addressing the IRS facts-and-circumstances characteristics: legal existence, creed/worship, ecclesiastical government, doctrine/discipline, history, congregation/membership, ministers, literature, established worship place(s), regular religious services, religious instruction, and any ministerial training. Not every characteristic is mandatory, but current IRS instructions generally expect an established congregation or other religious membership group.

### If church facts are not yet mature
Do not manufacture facts. Consider with counsel whether the organization should initially request another public-charity classification based on expected public support and later seek church classification if/when the actual facts support it.

## Part 7 - Governance integrity exhibits
Attach:
- Participant Bill of Rights;
- Founder Covenant;
- Board Reserved Powers;
- conflicts/compensation policy;
- safeguarding and complaint policies;
- dissolution language;
- related-party transaction files if any.

## Board approval checkpoint
Before filing, Board minutes should identify: form/version used, requested classification, reviewed activity narrative, reviewed financials, disclosed insider relationships, counsel/CPA review, and authorized signer.
