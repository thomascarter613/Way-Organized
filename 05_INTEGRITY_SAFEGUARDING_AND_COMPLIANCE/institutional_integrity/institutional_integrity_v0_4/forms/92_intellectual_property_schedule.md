# Founder / Organization Intellectual Property Schedule

**Purpose:** Prevent later ambiguity over what the Founder already owned, what is donated/licensed, and what belongs to the corporation. Complete before material publication, monetization, fundraising around proprietary material, or paid employment.

## Annual certification

At least annually, and before a material financing, licensing, publication, leadership transition, or founder-compensation change, the Secretary or delegated officer should confirm that this schedule is current and that insider transfers/licenses have the required conflict review.
