# Current Legal / Compliance Source Notes - v0.3

**Checked:** September 19, 2026. Counsel/CPA should verify again before filing or relying on an exemption.

## Tennessee organization

- Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-52-102 (2025), Charter: requires, among other things, public/mutual benefit status, religious-corporation statement if applicable, registered office/agent, incorporator, principal office, nonprofit statement, whether the corporation will or will not have members, and dissolution provisions. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-52/section-48-52-102/
- Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-52-105 (2025), Organization of corporation: organizational meeting rules and incorporator action without meeting. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-52/section-48-52-105/
- Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-58-202 (2025), Action without meeting: board action without meeting generally requires all directors to consent to taking action and written consents filed with corporate records, unless governing documents provide otherwise. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-58/part-2/section-48-58-202/
- Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-58-103 (2025): nonprofit board must have at least three natural persons; internal 7-11 / two-thirds-independent standard is voluntary and stricter. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-58/part-1/section-48-58-103/
- Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-58-301 (2025): director duties of good faith, care, and best interests. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-58/part-3/section-48-58-301/
- Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-66-203 (2025): annual report generally due on or before first day of fourth month following close of fiscal year, subject to SOS rule. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-66/part-2/section-48-66-203/
- Tennessee Secretary of State, Business Forms & Fees: current business/nonprofit filing forms and fees. https://sos.tn.gov/businesses/forms-and-fees

## Tennessee charitable solicitations / taxes

- Tennessee Secretary of State states bona fide religious institutions defined by Tennessee law are exempt from charitable-solicitation registration; other exemptions exist. Confirm factual eligibility before relying on this exemption. https://sos.tn.gov/
- Tennessee Department of Revenue, SUT-77: qualifying nonprofits generally must apply for and receive a Sales and Use Tax Certificate of Exemption before tax-exempt purchases; certificate is periodically reissued. https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360058251372-SUT-77-Nonprofit-Exemption-Overview
- Tennessee Department of Revenue, SUT-78: nonprofit exemption on purchases does not automatically exempt taxable sales; taxable sales may require collection/remittance. https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360058700151-SUT-78-Nonprofit-Exemption-Nonprofit-Organizations-Making-Sales-of-Taxable-Products-or-Services
- Tennessee State Board of Equalization: religious/charitable property-tax exemption generally requires application and qualifying ownership/use. https://comptroller.tn.gov/boards/state-board-of-equalization/property-tax-exemptions.html

## Federal tax-exempt organization

- IRS, Employer Identification Number: tax-exempt organizations need an EIN; IRS says form the legal entity before applying for an EIN. https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/employer-identification-number
- IRS Form 1023 instructions: churches may be exempt without filing but may file Form 1023 for a determination letter; Schedule A addresses church classification. https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023
- IRS Form 1023-EZ instructions: an organization seeking recognition as a church is not eligible to use Form 1023-EZ and should file Form 1023 if it wants a determination letter. https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023ez
- IRS charitable-contribution substantiation: donor acknowledgments for $250+ and charity disclosures for quid-pro-quo contributions over $75. https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/charitable-organizations-substantiation-and-disclosure-requirements

## Federal BOI / Corporate Transparency Act

- FinCEN BOI page, updated August 11, 2026: final rule exempts U.S. companies from BOI reporting; final rule became effective August 14, 2026. https://www.fincen.gov/boi

## Drafting note

The documents deliberately exceed many statutory defaults. Any counsel edit should preserve the anti-coercion purpose whenever lawful: independent oversight, no self-compensation, no self-investigation, safe exit, no retaliation/shunning, safeguarding priority, and transparent distinction between claimed revelation and binding corporate authority.
