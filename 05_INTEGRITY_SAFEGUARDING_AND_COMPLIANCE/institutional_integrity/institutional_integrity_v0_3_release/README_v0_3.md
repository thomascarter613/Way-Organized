# Institutional Integrity & Anti-Coercion System - v0.3 Release

Date: September 19, 2026
Status: Founding draft for Tennessee nonprofit counsel / tax-adviser review and later board adoption.

## What v0.3 adds

- Founding Board Adoption Packet: filing gates, incorporator action, organizational meeting, board resolutions, written-consent alternative, director/officer acceptances, standing committee charters, banking controls, tax/regulatory resolutions, donation receipt standard, and 90-day activation plan.
- Corporate Record Book & Compliance Binder: permanent tab index, document/action/person/access registers, retention schedule, compliance calendar, launch gates, public transparency register, annual governance cycle, Form 1023 preparation dossier, church-classification readiness matrix, tax evidence index, and secure records-room architecture.

## Current baseline carried forward

The v0.2 Constitutional Governance Package, Day-One Integrity Operations Manual, and quarterly integrity dashboard remain the operative baseline and are included here unchanged.

## Decisions intentionally not fabricated

1. Legal corporate name.
2. Tennessee statutory-member vs. nonmember model.
3. Actual initial directors and officers.
4. Principal office / registered-agent filing details.
5. Federal public-charity/church classification supported by real operating facts.
6. Final compensation, banking thresholds, insurer requirements, and counsel-specific edits.

The documents use explicit placeholders for these items. Placeholder signatures are not executed corporate actions.
