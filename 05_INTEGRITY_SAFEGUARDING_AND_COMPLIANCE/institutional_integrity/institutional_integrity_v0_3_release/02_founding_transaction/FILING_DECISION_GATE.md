## Gate B - Statutory members
Select exactly one:

- [ ] **Member corporation.** Use v0.2 `02_model_charter_member.md` plus Schedule M. Statutory members receive the rights assigned by Tennessee law and the governing documents.
- [ ] **Nonmember corporation.** Use v0.2 `03_model_charter_nonmember.md` plus Schedule N. Congregants/participants may have robust participatory rights without being statutory corporate members.

Do not use "member" casually in public documents if the nonmember model is selected; use terms such as participant, congregant, covenant participant, affiliate, or supporter unless counsel approves the terminology.

## Gate C - Initial board
Tennessee law requires at least three directors. This governance system uses a stronger formation target: at least five directors at organization, majority independent, expanding to 7-11 directors with at least two-thirds independent within 180 days.

Proposed directors: `[[NAMES]]`

Before election/appointment, each proposed director should complete:
- conflict and independence disclosure;
- acceptance of fiduciary duties;
- safeguarding/integrity acknowledgement;
- background/suitability review appropriate to role.

## Gate D - Federal tax posture
Select a filing strategy with tax counsel/CPA:

- [ ] Seek IRS determination as a **church** using Form 1023 and Schedule A, once the facts support church classification.
- [ ] Seek IRS determination as a **religious public charity** under an appropriate public-support classification, without initially requesting church classification.
- [ ] Rely temporarily on the automatic church exception from Form 1023 only if counsel concludes the organization already satisfies the facts-and-circumstances church standard; still consider applying for a determination letter for donor/institutional certainty.

**Do not use Form 1023-EZ if requesting recognition as a church.** Current IRS instructions specifically make a church applicant ineligible for 1023-EZ.

## Gate E - Launch boundary
No public launch involving donations, minors/vulnerable persons, compensated founder/executive roles, communal housing, or material financial commitments should occur until the corresponding v0.2 controls are active.
