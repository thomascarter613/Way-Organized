# Legal and Compliance Source Notes

Checked September 18, 2026. Counsel should verify current law before filing/adoption.

## Tennessee nonprofit corporation law

- **Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-52-102 (2025), Charter.** Requires charter items including public/mutual benefit classification, religious-corporation statement when applicable, registered office/agent, incorporators, principal office, nonprofit statement, whether the corporation will have members, and dissolution provisions. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-52/section-48-52-102/
- **Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-52-106 (2025), Bylaws.** Bylaws may regulate corporate affairs if not inconsistent with law or charter. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-52/section-48-52-106/
- **Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-58-103 (2025), Number of directors.** Tennessee nonprofit board must consist of three or more natural persons; this pack intentionally sets a higher internal standard after formation. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-58/part-1/section-48-58-103/
- **Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-58-301 (2025), Director standards.** Good faith, ordinary prudence, best interests, disclosure of material information; religious-corporation reliance provision. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-58/part-3/section-48-58-301/
- **Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-58-108 (2025), Director removal.** Includes board removal of a board-elected director without cause by two-thirds unless charter/bylaws require greater threshold. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-58/part-1/section-48-58-108/
- **Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-58-702 (2025), Conflicting-interest transactions.** Addresses circumstances in which conflicting-interest transactions are protected from equitable relief/damages. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-58/part-7/section-48-58-702/
- **Tenn. Code Ann. § 48-66-101 (2025), Corporate records.** Requires permanent records of member/board actions and appropriate accounting records. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-66/part-1/section-48-66-101/
- **Tenn. Code Ann. §§ 48-67-101 to -102.** Tennessee Nonprofit Corporation Act generally applies to religious corporations subject to enumerated exceptions; religious doctrine/canon-law conflicts receive constitutional treatment described in § 48-67-102. https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/nonprofit-corporations/chapter-67/section-48-67-102/

## Tennessee child safety

- Tennessee Department of Children's Services, **Report Child Abuse**. DCS states everyone in Tennessee is a mandated reporter of suspected child abuse or neglect; hotline 1-877-237-0004 and online reporting at https://apps.tn.gov/carat/. Source page: https://www.tn.gov/dcs/program-areas/child-safety/report-child-abuse.html (or current Tennessee DCS successor URL).

## Federal tax-exempt compensation

- IRS, **Rebuttable presumption - Intermediate sanctions.** Describes advance approval by a conflict-free authorized body, appropriate comparability data, and contemporaneous documentation. https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/rebuttable-presumption-intermediate-sanctions
- IRS, **Intermediate sanctions - Compensation.** Reasonable compensation reflects fair-market value for like services by like enterprises under like circumstances and considers forms of compensation. https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/intermediate-sanctions-compensation
- IRS Publication 557 (Jan. 2025 version currently listed), **Tax-Exempt Status for Your Organization**, includes excess-benefit/rebuttable-presumption guidance. https://www.irs.gov/publications/p557
- IRS Publication 1828, **Tax Guide for Churches and Religious Organizations** (IRS currently lists Aug. 2015 edition). Verify current online IRS guidance in addition to the publication before final adoption. https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-publication-1828

## Drafting note

This pack deliberately imposes governance controls more protective than many statutory defaults. Counsel should determine which protections belong in the filed charter versus bylaws or policies, considering amendment mechanics, religious-autonomy doctrines, employment/ministry rules, insurance, tax posture, and practical governance capacity.
