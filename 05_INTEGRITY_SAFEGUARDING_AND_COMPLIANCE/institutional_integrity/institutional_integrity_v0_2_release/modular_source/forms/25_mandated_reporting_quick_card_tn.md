# Tennessee Child-Safety Reporting Quick Card

**Emergency / life-threatening:** Call 911.

**Tennessee Child Abuse Hotline:** 1-877-237-0004  
**Online reporting:** https://apps.tn.gov/carat/

Tennessee DCS states that **everyone in Tennessee is a mandated reporter** of suspected child abuse or neglect. Do not wait for an internal investigation, supervisor permission, clergy approval, or certainty. Preserve only the facts needed and avoid conducting your own forensic interview.

After the required external report, notify the organization's safeguarding lead unless that person is implicated; if implicated, use the independent board complaint channel.

Source checked September 18, 2026: Tennessee Department of Children's Services, Report Child Abuse.
