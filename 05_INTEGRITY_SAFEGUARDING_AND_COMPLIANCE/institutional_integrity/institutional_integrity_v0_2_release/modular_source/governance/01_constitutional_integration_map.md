# Constitutional Integration Map

| Framework safeguard | Charter | Bylaws | Policy/SOP | Operational evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Freedom of conscience | Entrenched principle | Rights article | Anti-coercion + pastoral ethics | Participant notice, complaint metrics |
| No infallible living authority | Founder/authority limitation | Founder article | Revelation/doctrine protocol | Annual founder covenant |
| Safe exit / no shunning | Entrenched principle | Participant rights | Exit/non-retaliation SOP | Exit log without reasons requirement |
| Board independence | Minimum independence standard | Board composition/terms | Independence standard | Annual director certifications |
| No self-compensation | Reserved-power clause | Conflict/compensation sections | Compensation approval SOP | Compensation memo + comparability file |
| No self-investigation | Reserved-power clause | Integrity committee powers | Investigation protocol | Conflict screen + investigator TOR |
| Safeguarding | Entrenched duty to obey civil law | Safeguarding committee | TN safeguarding policy | Training, screening, incident log |
| Financial transparency | Mission/private inurement clauses | Finance article | Financial controls SOP | Budget, quarterly reports, external review |
| Whistleblowing/non-retaliation | Entrenched right | Complaints article | Complaint SOP | Intake log, retaliation reports |
| Epistemic humility | Optional charter principle | Doctrine/research article | Revelation/doctrine protocol | Doctrine change record |
| Entrenchment | Explicit amendment restriction | Two-stage amendment process | Change-control SOP | Amendment impact memo |
| External integrity review | Optional charter notice | Integrity article | Review TOR | Findings + remediation tracker |

## Hierarchy rule

When documents conflict, the controlling order should be: applicable law and valid court orders; charter; bylaws; entrenched framework schedules; board policies; SOPs; forms and guidance. No lower-level document may reduce a right or independence protection granted by a higher-level document.
