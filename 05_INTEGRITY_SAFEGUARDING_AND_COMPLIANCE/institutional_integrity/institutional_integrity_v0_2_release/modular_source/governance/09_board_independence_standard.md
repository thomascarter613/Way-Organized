# Board Independence Standard

## Annual independence test

A voting director is independent only when the Governance and Nominating Committee documents that none of the following materially impair objective judgment:

- current employment by the organization or supervision by an employee;
- immediate-family relationship with a senior employee, executive founder, or director;
- material vendor, landlord, lender, borrower, contractor, or consulting relationship;
- material gifts, housing, tuition, personal support, or other economic dependency from the organization or a senior leader;
- current pastoral/counseling dependency on an executive whose performance the director oversees;
- partnership, shared business, or material joint property with a senior executive;
- relationship that creates a reasonable appearance that the director cannot evaluate leadership independently.

Independence is reassessed whenever circumstances change. A director can remain on the board while temporarily non-independent only if the charter threshold remains satisfied and the conflict is managed; otherwise the board must cure the composition promptly.

## Composition control

At least two-thirds of voting directors shall be independent. Staff directors, if any, shall never form a blocking minority sufficient to prevent investigation, compensation action, or executive removal under ordinary voting thresholds.
