# Independent Investigation Protocol

## Independence trigger
Use an external or otherwise demonstrably independent investigator for allegations involving the founder, chief executive, director, lead clergy executive, safeguarding lead, finance lead, retaliation by leadership, substantial financial impropriety, abuse, or any matter where ordinary management has an actual/apparent conflict.

## Pre-investigation controls
- external-reporting obligations addressed first;
- litigation hold / evidence preservation when appropriate;
- investigator conflict screen completed;
- written terms of reference approved by disinterested authority;
- retaliation and witness-contact instructions issued;
- interim safety measures considered.

## Investigation standard
The investigator should determine facts using a stated civil/administrative standard (normally preponderance / more likely than not unless counsel directs otherwise), separately identify corroborated facts, disputed facts, credibility considerations, policy conclusions, and matters outside scope.

## Fair process
The respondent receives sufficient notice of material allegations and a meaningful opportunity to respond, subject to safety, privilege, and legal limitations. Complainants and witnesses are not promised outcomes or absolute confidentiality.

## Report
The report states scope, methodology, evidence reviewed, interviews, factual findings, policy analysis if requested, limitations, unresolved issues, and recommendations only if within mandate. The board, not the investigator, normally decides corporate sanctions unless mandate provides otherwise.

## Publication
Public summaries should prioritize accountability while protecting victims, minors, medical information, personnel privacy, legal privilege, and investigative integrity.
