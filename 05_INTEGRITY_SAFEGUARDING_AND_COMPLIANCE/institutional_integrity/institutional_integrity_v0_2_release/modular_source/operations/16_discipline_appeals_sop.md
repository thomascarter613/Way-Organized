# Discipline, Removal, and Appeals SOP

## Minimum due process
For material discipline affecting office, employment, voting membership, credentials, or access to significant organizational privileges:

1. Written notice of the rule/standard and material facts alleged.
2. Disclosure of decision maker and conflict check.
3. Reasonable opportunity to provide evidence and response.
4. Impartial decision based on the stated record.
5. Written outcome with findings sufficient to understand the basis.
6. Proportionate corrective action.
7. Appeal to a person/body not materially involved in the original decision.

## Prohibited discipline grounds
Good-faith criticism; protected complaint/report; lawful contact with former members or critics; refusal to donate; refusal of optional ritual/counseling; accessing outside information; obtaining independent legal/medical advice; or resignation.

## Restoration
Forgiveness, reconciliation, membership restoration, and restoration to authority are separate questions. No person is entitled to regain a position of trust merely because apology, repentance, treatment, or forgiveness has occurred.
