# Exit, Disaffiliation, and Former-Participant SOP

1. Any clear communication of resignation is effective without approval.
2. Staff shall not require an exit interview, payment, confession, doctrinal recantation, nondisparagement agreement, or spiritual release.
3. Administrative staff may offer an optional property/records checklist.
4. Personal property, final wages, earned benefits, and deposits are handled under ordinary law/contract, not religious standing.
5. Leaders shall not direct friends or family to sever ordinary relationships because of departure or criticism.
6. Confidential pastoral/personnel information remains protected after exit.
7. Former participants may criticize the organization. The organization may correct factual statements through ordinary lawful means but shall not retaliate or expose confidential information to punish criticism.
8. Threats, stalking, repeated unwanted contact, or safety risks are handled through ordinary safeguarding/security/legal channels, not theological labeling.
