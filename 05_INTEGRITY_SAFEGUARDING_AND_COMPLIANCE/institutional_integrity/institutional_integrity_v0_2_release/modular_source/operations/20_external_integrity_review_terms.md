# External Institutional Integrity Review - Standing Terms of Reference

## Frequency
First review within 18 months of public launch; thereafter at least every three years, with targeted interim reviews after major safeguarding, financial, retaliation, governance, or leadership crises.

## Reviewer independence
Reviewer(s) shall not be current staff, founder relatives, material donors with governance leverage, routine vendors whose revenue depends materially on the organization, or persons with another relationship reasonably compromising independence.

## Scope
- freedom of conscience and participant rights in practice;
- board independence and founder/leader power concentration;
- complaint accessibility and retaliation risk;
- safeguarding systems and case handling;
- investigation independence;
- financial conflicts/related-party transactions/compensation process;
- pastoral boundaries and information control;
- exit practices and former-participant treatment;
- housing/employment/education dependency risks;
- doctrine/revelation process and manufactured certainty risks;
- transparency and remediation culture.

## Evidence access
Subject to privilege and lawful privacy limits, reviewer receives relevant policies, governance records, training data, anonymized complaint/safeguarding statistics, selected case files when authorized, financial-control evidence, leadership certifications, and remediation history.

## Reporting
Classify findings by severity and systemic significance. Separate verified fact, control gap, cultural observation, and recommendation. The board publishes an appropriate summary and a remediation plan, then tracks actions to closure.
