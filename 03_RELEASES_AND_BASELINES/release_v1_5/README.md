# The Way v1.5 - Founding Gathering Circle Print-and-Run Curriculum

Version 1.5 | September 21, 2026

## Purpose

This release turns the 90-day Founding Gathering Circle into a complete 13-session adult pilot that can be printed and run without reconstructing material from prior documents.

## Hard launch gate

Do not begin recurring public pilot use until `[[INDEPENDENT_CONCERN_CONTACT]]` is replaced with a real person or service genuinely able to receive concerns outside the founder/facilitator chain.

The pilot remains:
- pre-incorporation;
- adult-only by default;
- non-fundraising;
- experimental;
- not an official House of Prayer;
- not a claim of IRS church or 501(c)(3) recognition.

## Session sequence

1. Orientation I - What the Way Is
2. Orientation II - How We Know
3. Prayer I - Attention and Honest Address
4. Study I - Reading Witnesses Responsibly
5. Fellowship - The Common Table
6. Service - Good Without Conversion
7. Discernment I - Disagreement Without Defeat
8. Ethics, Power, and Boundaries
9. Open Theology - What We Do Not Yet Know
10. Companion Pathway
11. Founder-Exit Exercise
12. Repair, Complaints, and Leaving Well
13. Day-90 Review and Closure Decision

## Source-witness approach

The curriculum uses short summaries and references rather than reproducing long copyrighted passages. Comparative witnesses are presented as examples from their own traditions, not as claims that all traditions are identical.
