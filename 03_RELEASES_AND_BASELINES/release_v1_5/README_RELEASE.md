# The Way v1.5 - Print-and-Run Pilot Curriculum

Built September 21, 2026.

This release expands the v1.4 Founding Gathering Circle framework into a complete 13-week print-and-run adult pilot curriculum.

## Deliverables produced in the working session

- Founding Gathering Circle Facilitator Binder v1.5 (DOCX/PDF)
- Founding Gathering Circle Participant Workbook v1.5 (DOCX/PDF)
- Thirteen individual Week 1-13 facilitator packets (DOCX/PDF)
- Thirteen editable Markdown week sources

## Weekly sequence

1. Orientation I: What the Way Is
2. Orientation II: How We Know
3. Prayer I: Attention and Honest Address
4. Study I: Reading Witnesses Responsibly
5. Fellowship: The Common Table
6. Service: Good Without Conversion
7. Discernment I: Disagreement Without Defeat
8. Ethics, Power, and Boundaries
9. Open Theology: What We Do Not Yet Know
10. Companion Pathway
11. Founder-Exit Exercise
12. Repair, Complaints, and Leaving Well
13. Day-90 Review and Closure Decision

## Operating constraints

This remains an adult, pre-incorporation, non-fundraising pilot. It is not a recognized House of Prayer, incorporated nonprofit, or IRS-recognized charity.

A genuinely independent complaint/safeguarding contact is a hard launch gate before recurring public sessions.

Every weekly packet includes:
- purpose and learning objectives
- pre-session readiness checks
- primary/internal reading references
- claim-status cues
- 75-minute timed agenda
- core exercise and debrief
- facilitator risk controls
- explicit dissent check
- reproducible participant handout
- take-home practice
- anonymous feedback pulse
- facilitator after-action review
