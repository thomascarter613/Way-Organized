# The Way v1.6 - Pilot Launch Logistics & Venue

Version 1.6 | September 21, 2026

## Purpose

This release moves the Founding Gathering Circle from print-and-run curriculum to launch logistics. It localizes the venue strategy to the Morristown / Jefferson City area, defines zero/low-cost launch controls, supplies venue and volunteer outreach scripts, and establishes the 30-day activation sequence.

## Working venue order

1. Morristown-Hamblen Library - Front Meeting Room
2. Jefferson City Public Library - Conference Room / Hall
3. Morristown Landing

No venue has agreed to host the pilot yet. The list is a research shortlist only.

## Hard launch gates

- Independent concern/safeguarding route is real, named, and reachable.
- Venue permission/reservation is documented and current policy is reviewed.
- Public invitation accurately says pre-incorporation, experimental, adult, and non-fundraising.
- Emergency/site plan is completed.
- A plausible non-founder Week 11 facilitator is identified and trained.
