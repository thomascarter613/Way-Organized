# v0.9 Decision Register

Date: September 20, 2026

## Suffering and evil
**Status:** Established

Do not presume suffering is deserved, divinely inflicted, karmically deserved, or evidence of weak faith; protect lament, care, treatment, and justice.

## Theodicy
**Status:** Open

No single theodicy is adopted. Proposed explanations must face horrendous suffering and may not be used to silence victims.

## Human spiritual end
**Status:** Provisional

Use healing, liberation, reconciliation, awakening, wholeness, and right relationship as comparative/working terms without declaring them metaphysically identical.

## Exclusive salvation
**Status:** Open

No current teaching that only Companions can be saved/liberated; no leader may claim knowledge of another person's ultimate destiny.

## Afterlife
**Status:** Open

No settled teaching yet on resurrection, heaven/hell, rebirth, annihilation, or postmortem consciousness.

## Funerals
**Status:** Established

Speak truthfully: honor the dead, support mourners, distinguish hope from certainty, and never exploit grief.

## Intimate ethics
**Status:** Established

Require capacity, meaningful consent, non-exploitation, honesty, safeguarding, and faithful responsibility to freely undertaken commitments and dependents.

## Marriage theology
**Status:** Open

Marriage/covenant blessing remains reserved pending research on sacramentality, gender/sex, divorce, remarriage, same-sex unions, celibacy, and family plurality.

## Violence
**Status:** Well-supported

Strong presumption toward nonviolence, peacemaking, de-escalation, and protection of vulnerable persons; absolute pacifism remains open.

## Religious violence
**Status:** Established

No House, minister, founder, elder, or council can authorize vigilantism, holy war, coercive enforcement, or revelatory exemption from law.

## Rites
**Status:** Established

Rites may express commitment, memory, transition, healing, and community; metaphysical efficacy must be stated only at the confidence warranted.

## Commissioning
**Status:** Established

Commissioning authorizes defined functions and limits; it creates no sacred caste or immunity.

## Ordination
**Status:** Reserved

Do not adopt until theology requires a claim beyond functional commissioning.

## Sacred time
**Status:** Provisional

Develop a recurring weekly/seasonal rhythm experimentally before constitutionalizing a comprehensive calendar.

## Fasting
**Status:** Provisional

Optional/experimental only, with broad health and conscience exemptions until a theology and safety standard are adopted.

## Other religions
**Status:** Established

Represent source traditions accurately; dialogue does not require erasing difference; no compelled conversion or social isolation.

## Dual belonging
**Status:** Open

Permitted for exploration/participation; formal doctrinal coherence varies by traditions and remains under study.
