# The Way - v0.9 Theological Research Volume II

Version 0.9 - September 20, 2026

This release continues the v0.8 Library of Witnesses research method across nine domains:

- evil and suffering;
- salvation/liberation;
- death and afterlife;
- sexuality, marriage, and family;
- violence and nonviolence;
- rites and sacred mediation;
- ordination and ministry;
- sacred time/calendar;
- interreligious identity and conversion.

The release deliberately establishes ethical and institutional safeguards more strongly than unresolved metaphysical claims.
