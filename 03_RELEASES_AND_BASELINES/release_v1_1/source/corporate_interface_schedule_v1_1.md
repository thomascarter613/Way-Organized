# Corporate Interface Schedule v1.1

| Domain | Primary actor | Scope | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Religious teaching | Assembly / authorized religious process | Teaching status, practice, formation, rites | Cannot bind corporation to illegal or unauthorized civil action. |
| Corporate fiduciary action | Board | Property, contracts, banking, employment, budgets, filings | Must respect chartered purposes and adopted participant-rights safeguards. |
| House operation | Delegated House leadership + corporate oversight | Local gatherings and religious programming | No independent debt, employment, banking, property contracts, or legal personality without delegation. |
| Ministry commissioning | Authorized religious process under Board-adopted policy | Defined religious functions | No automatic compensation, corporate signing power, confidentiality privilege, or lifetime office. |
| Founder | Historical founder + any separately appointed office | Only authority of current written office/commission | No veto, immunity, succession right, or self-set compensation. |
| Complaints/safeguarding | Independent committee/channel; external authorities where required | Safety, allegations, retaliation, investigations | Overrides ordinary reporting lines when independence or law requires. |
