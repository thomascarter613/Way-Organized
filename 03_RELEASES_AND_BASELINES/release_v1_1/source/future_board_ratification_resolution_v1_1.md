# Future Board Ratification Resolution v1.1

**Use only after a lawful Board exists. Do not sign or date as Board action before the corporation exists and the Board actually acts.**

RESOLVED, that the Board recognizes the Constitution of the Way v1.0 as the current religious constitution governing religious identity, participant rights, Companionship, the Assembly, Houses of Prayer, ministry vocabulary, epistemic integrity, teaching status, and religious practice of the community served by the Corporation;

FURTHER RESOLVED, that this recognition does not alter powers assigned by applicable law, the filed charter, or the bylaws to the Board, officers, members if any, or other legal actors;

FURTHER RESOLVED, that no claimed revelation, religious vote, House practice, founder preference, Minister statement, or Council decision may authorize the Corporation to disregard safeguarding, fiduciary, employment, tax, financial-control, reporting, contractual, or other civil obligations;

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board adopts the Teaching & Practice Baseline v1.0 and Companion Handbook v1.0 as current pilot standards for the Corporation’s religious programming, subject to their stated confidence levels and review processes;

FURTHER RESOLVED, that any proposed corporate action materially reducing participant rights, founder limits, non-retaliation rules, safeguarding duties, or epistemic-integrity safeguards shall receive heightened review under the charter/bylaws and Institutional Integrity framework before action.
