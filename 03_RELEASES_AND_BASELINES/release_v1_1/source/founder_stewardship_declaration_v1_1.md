# Pre-Incorporation Founder Stewardship Declaration v1.1

This is a founder/organizer declaration, not a corporate resolution.

I, [[FOUNDER LEGAL NAME]], as founder and organizer of the developing religious community presently using the working language “the Way,” voluntarily adopt the following stewardship commitments for the pre-incorporation period:

1. I will use the Constitution of the Way v1.0, Teaching & Practice Baseline v1.0, Companion Handbook v1.0, and Institutional Integrity & Anti-Coercion framework as the provisional religious and ethical baseline for organizing, research, pilot gatherings, recruitment, and future incorporation work.
2. I acknowledge that this personal adoption does not create a corporation, Board of Directors, statutory membership body, recognized House of Prayer, tax-exempt determination, clergy credential, property right, or legal authority that does not otherwise exist.
3. I will not describe founder status as ownership, infallibility, prophetic supremacy, irrevocable leadership, automatic compensation entitlement, or a right to select a successor.
4. I will not treat private spiritual experience, claimed revelation, dream, vision, intuition, or conviction as sufficient authority to override consent, safeguarding, civil law, financial controls, future corporate governing documents, or another adult’s conscience.
5. I will preserve criticism, outside relationships, safe exit, independent advice, and participants’ right to decline religious identity, donation, fasting, counseling, housing, service, or ministry.
6. I will transfer operational and fiduciary authority to the duly constituted corporate Board when formed and submit founder compensation, related-party transactions, complaints, safeguarding allegations, and conflicts to independent processes.
7. I understand that the future Board and Assembly may correct, amend, or reject my proposals through the lawful processes of their respective domains.
8. I will not backdate later approvals or imply that a future Board ratified an action before actually doing so.
