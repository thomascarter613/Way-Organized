# The Assembly of the Way
## Founding Gathering Circle Participant Packet v1.4

**Date:** September 21, 2026  
**Status:** Adult pre-incorporation pilot.

### What this is
The Founding Gathering Circle is a 90-day experiment in practicing the Way through prayer, study, fellowship, service, discernment, and honest inquiry. It is not yet a recognized House of Prayer, a separate legal entity, or an IRS-recognized charity.

### What this is not
You are not joining a corporation, signing a lifetime vow, making a donation commitment, surrendering conscience, or accepting the founder's interpretations by attending. You may attend, question, disagree, pass, stop attending, or criticize the pilot.

### Your rights
- Keep outside relationships, communities, reading, clinicians, attorneys, advisers, and critics.
- Decline prayer, disclosure, counseling, fasting, service, belonging, or any religious interpretation.
- Leave at any time without permission or spiritual release.
- Raise concerns through the normal contact or the independent route.
- Receive no retaliation for criticism, reporting, declining, or departure.
- Be told when a claim is established, provisional, permitted, open, symbolic, or simply hoped.

### What a typical gathering includes
Welcome and status reminder; brief silence/prayer; source or topic; context and confidence label; discussion/practice; explicit dissent check; closing; anonymous pulse. No donation appeal.

### The 90-day arc
Weeks 1-2: orientation and how we know.  
Weeks 3-4: prayer and study.  
Weeks 5-6: fellowship and service.  
Weeks 7-8: discernment, disagreement, ethics, and power.  
Weeks 9-10: open theology and optional Companion orientation.  
Week 11: founder-exit exercise.  
Week 12: complaints, repair, and leaving well.  
Week 13: evidence review and decision.

### Optional Companion identity
No decision is expected during the pilot. A person may participate without becoming a Companion. If someone later chooses the Companion identity, that decision remains voluntary and revocable and does not create statutory corporate membership.
