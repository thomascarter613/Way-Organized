# Facilitator Quick Cards v1.4

## Opening card
"Welcome. This is a voluntary Founding Gathering Circle. You may observe, pass, step out, disagree, or leave. This is a pre-incorporation pilot and not a recognized House of Prayer or tax-exempt organization. No donation is being requested. If anything feels coercive or unsafe, you may use the independent concern route shown in your packet."

## Claim-label card
Before presenting a material claim, identify it as one or more of: primary source/witness; historical reconstruction; interpretation; current teaching; provisional practice; permitted interpretation; open question; symbolic/ritual language; hope.

## Dissent card
Ask: "What is the strongest disagreement with what was just said? What evidence would change our present view? Did anyone feel pressure to agree?"

## Correction card
If you materially overstate a claim: stop, name the error, provide the correction, record it in the correction log, and repeat the correction at the next relevant gathering if the error could persist socially.

## Stop card
If there is credible danger, abuse, serious fraud, retaliation, stalking, sexual misconduct, or a safeguarding issue, suspend the affected activity. Preserve facts, use external/emergency reporting where appropriate, and do not substitute reconciliation for safety.
