# HTERP-WP-007 — Provisional Constructive Framework

**Status:** completed as provisional framework  
**Version:** 0.8  
**Nature:** constructive, revisable, nonbinding  
**Relationship to HTERP:** downstream of evidence, not a replacement for evidence.

## Purpose

Translate the first six HTERP work packages into a religious/philosophical framework that can actually be practiced **without pretending unresolved metaphysical questions have been solved**.

## Critical distinction

Every proposition must belong to one of four categories:

### R — Research conclusion
Supported by HTERP evidence.

### F — Faith postulate
Freely adopted as a devotional/metaphysical commitment, while acknowledged as not established by HTERP.

### P — Permitted interpretation
Compatible with the framework but not required.

### O — Open question
No required answer.

This prevents faith from masquerading as historical proof and prevents historical uncertainty from making religious practice impossible.

## Working identity

The framework has an **Abrahamic-prophetic spine** because that is the intended constructive project:
- Torah;
- Hebrew Prophets;
- historically recoverable Jesus tradition;
- Qur'anic proclamation and earliest recoverable Muhammad tradition.

But this spine is a **chosen religious center**, not an HTERP finding that these sources outrank every other human tradition metaphysically.

Additional streams enter through graded comparative layers:
- Zarathustra/Gathas;
- Psalms/wisdom traditions;
- early Jewish/Christian/Islamic contemplative material;
- Guru Nanak/Sikh bani;
- Buddhist ethical/contemplative material;
- Vedic/Upanishadic insight where compatible;
- other global wisdom and sacred traditions where ethically and contextually appropriate.

## Working devotional postulate

**F-001 — The Most High**

For purposes of worship and religious life, the framework provisionally adopts:

> There is an ultimate transcendent Source of reality, truth, and moral significance, beyond complete human comprehension, whom the Abrahamic prophetic traditions address through names such as El, YHWH, God, and Allah.

This is a **faith postulate**, not an HTERP empirical conclusion.

The framework does **not yet require** the further propositions that:
- all historical divine names refer to exactly one ontological entity;
- the Source is adequately described by any one inherited theology;
- the Source is exclusively personal rather than also transcending person/nonperson categories.

## Core religious principle

> **Seek what is true; practice what is just and merciful; cultivate humility before what exceeds human knowledge; and revise belief when stronger evidence requires it.**
