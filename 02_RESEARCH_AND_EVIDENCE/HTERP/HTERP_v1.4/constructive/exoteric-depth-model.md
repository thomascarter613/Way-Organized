# Exoteric / Depth Model

## Public Way
Everything necessary for belonging and ordinary practice is public:
- ethical covenant;
- prayer;
- study;
- service;
- community;
- repentance/repair;
- basic theology and its uncertainties.

## Study Way
Deeper work in:
- Hebrew/Aramaic/Greek/Arabic/Avestan/Sanskrit as appropriate;
- textual criticism;
- history;
- comparative religion;
- philosophy;
- science.

## Contemplative Way
Progressive training in:
- silence;
- attention;
- prayer;
- remembrance;
- meditation;
- ethical self-examination.

## Advanced Practice
May require prerequisites/supervision when:
- psychologically intense;
- physiologically demanding;
- tradition-specific;
- community-restricted.

## Research Way
Qualified practitioners may participate in HTERP protocols testing:
- prophecy;
- anomalous information;
- contemplative phenomenology;
- channeling/mediumship;
- prediction.

## Non-negotiable rule

> **There is no secret doctrine necessary for salvation, dignity, or full moral membership that contradicts the public teaching.**

Depth means greater discipline and understanding, not a hidden religion for insiders.
