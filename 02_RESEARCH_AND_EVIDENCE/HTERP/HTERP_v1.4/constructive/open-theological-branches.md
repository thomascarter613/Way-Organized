# Open Theological Branches

These are legitimate positions inside the provisional framework unless later evidence/constitutional review narrows them.

## Jesus branch
- J1: human prophet/teacher
- J2: Messiah with unique divine commissioning
- J3: uniquely divine Son / incarnational interpretation
- J4: other model consistent with evidence and ethical covenant

No branch may misrepresent competing evidence.

## Muhammad branch
- M1: genuine prophet but not necessarily final in every possible sense
- M2: final normative legislative prophet
- M3: inspired reformer/teacher without supernatural revelation
- M4: unresolved

## Revelation branch
- R1: genuine personal divine communication filtered through humans
- R2: contact with transcendent reality, partly personified
- R3: heterogeneous mixture of transcendent and endogenous events
- R4: entirely natural human religious cognition
- R5: unresolved

## Afterlife branch
- A1: resurrection/judgment
- A2: continuing conscious intermediate state + resurrection
- A3: rebirth/reincarnation model
- A4: symbolic/agnostic continuation
- A5: no conscious survival
- A6: unresolved

## Spirit branch
- S1: angels/spirits are real created intelligences
- S2: some are symbolic/cognitive forms, others may be real
- S3: primarily psychological/cultural phenomena
- S4: unresolved

## Scripture branch
All branches must accept historical-critical examination.
Disagreement concerns the theological meaning of the resulting texts, not whether evidence may be suppressed.

## Boundary
A permitted branch becomes impermissible only if it:
- requires deliberate falsification;
- legitimizes abuse/coercion;
- denies others' full human dignity;
- demands rejection of well-established evidence merely to preserve authority.
