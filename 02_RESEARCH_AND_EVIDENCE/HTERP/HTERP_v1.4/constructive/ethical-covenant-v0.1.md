# Ethical Covenant v0.1

A member/practitioner can affirm this covenant without assenting to every open metaphysical question.

## 1. Truth
Seek truth; do not knowingly manipulate evidence or bear false witness.

## 2. Justice
Do not exploit the weak. Resist corruption, partiality, and abuse of power.

## 3. Mercy
Prefer restoration, compassion, and forgiveness where they do not enable continuing harm.

## 4. Human dignity
Treat every person as possessing moral worth independent of belief, status, sex, ethnicity, nationality, wealth, or membership.

## 5. Restraint
Train appetite, anger, ego, consumption, sexuality, speech, and power rather than allowing desire to rule without reflection.

## 6. Generosity
Share resources, time, skill, hospitality, and care with those in need.

## 7. Humility
Do not confuse confidence with certainty. Admit error.

## 8. Fidelity
Keep promises and responsibilities unless doing so would perpetuate serious wrongdoing.

## 9. Knowledge
Study deeply. Ask difficult questions. Do not fear evidence.

## 10. Stewardship
Treat body, community, animals, land, and material resources as things entrusted rather than merely consumed.

## 11. Peace
Do not glorify violence. Defensive or protective force, where unavoidable, remains morally grave and constrained.

## 12. Transformation
Judge religious practice partly by its fruits in character, conduct, relationships, and justice.

## Safeguard
No claimed revelation overrides the prohibitions against abuse, coercion, exploitation, or deliberate deception without an evidentiary standard far beyond private certainty.
