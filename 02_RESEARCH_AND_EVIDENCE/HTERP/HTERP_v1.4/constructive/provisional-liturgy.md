# Provisional Prayer and Liturgy Set

These prayers intentionally avoid settling open Christological, prophetic-finality, or spirit-world questions.

## Dawn

> Most High, Source of truth and life,
> turn me from falsehood toward truth,
> from injustice toward justice,
> from hardness toward mercy,
> from pride toward humility,
> and from heedlessness toward remembrance.
> Give me courage to repair what I damage,
> wisdom to question what I merely assume,
> and strength to do the good I already know.
> Amen.

## Midday

> Most High, bring me back to what matters.
> Let my work be honest,
> my speech truthful,
> my power restrained,
> and my dealings just.
> Where I have wandered, correct me.

## Evening

> Most High, nothing in me is hidden from truth.
> I give thanks for the good received and done.
> I acknowledge the harm, falsehood, neglect, and selfishness of this day.
> Show me what can be repaired.
> Grant mercy to those I have harmed and to me,
> and make tomorrow more faithful than today.

## Before study

> Source of truth, let me prefer what is true to what is familiar.
> Keep me from defending error because it is mine,
> and from rejecting wisdom because it comes from another.

## Before meals

> For life, land, labor, creatures, hands, and community, we give thanks.
> May what sustains us strengthen us to sustain others.

## Confession / repair

The preferred sequence is:
1. acknowledge specifically;
2. stop the harmful behavior;
3. repair what can be repaired;
4. seek forgiveness without demanding it;
5. change the conditions that produced the harm.

Prayer does not substitute for restitution.
