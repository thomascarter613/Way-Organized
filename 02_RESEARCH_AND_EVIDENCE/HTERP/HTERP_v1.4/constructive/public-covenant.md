# Public Covenant v0.1

We seek the truth without fear.

We turn toward the Most High while admitting that no human name, image, institution, or text contains the whole of ultimate reality.

We receive the Torah, the Prophets, the witness to Jesus, and the Qur'anic proclamation as the central inherited stream of this community, studying them critically, reverently, and without pretending that later interpretations are identical with their earliest layers.

We welcome wisdom from other traditions where it survives examination and accords with truth, justice, mercy, humility, and human dignity.

We will not use revelation, office, tradition, or secrecy to excuse abuse or deception.

We will pray, study, serve, give, practice restraint, repair harm, and remain willing to learn.

Where we know, we will say we know.
Where we believe, we will say we believe.
Where we do not know, we will say we do not know.
