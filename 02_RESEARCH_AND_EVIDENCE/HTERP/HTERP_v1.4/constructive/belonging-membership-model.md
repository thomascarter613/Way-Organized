# Belonging and Membership Model

## Principle
Belonging is based primarily on **covenant and practice**, not forced certainty about open metaphysics.

## Seeker
May attend, study, pray, and participate without affirming the faith postulates.

## Practitioner
Freely adopts the Ethical Covenant and Rule of Practice.

## Covenant Member
Affirms:
- commitment to truth-seeking;
- ethical covenant;
- noncoercion;
- willingness to practice;
- willingness to revise error.

A Covenant Member need not settle every O-category theological question.

## Teacher / Guide
Requires:
- demonstrated knowledge;
- ethical record;
- safeguarding training;
- ability to represent uncertainty honestly;
- community authorization;
- periodic review.

Claimed visions or charisma are **not** qualifications by themselves.

## Research Steward
Maintains source standards, HTERP methodology, data integrity, and version history.

## No second-class doubter
A member who honestly says “I do not know” about an open question is not spiritually inferior to someone who claims certainty.

## Exit
Membership is voluntary. Leaving does not justify shunning, threats, family separation, financial punishment, or claims that ordinary civil rights are lost.
