# Authority and Canon Architecture

## Level 0 — Reality / Truth
Highest authority in principle.
No human text is identical with Level 0.

## Level 1 — Abrahamic Prophetic Spine
Chosen constructive center, not an HTERP truth-ranking.

### Torah
Studied historically and theologically, with distinction among narrative, covenant, ritual law, civil law, moral teaching, and later interpretation.

### Prophets
Especially material concerned with:
- fidelity to the Most High;
- justice;
- mercy;
- corruption;
- idolatry;
- repentance;
- transformation.

### Jesus Witness Corpus
Not “everything later Christianity says.”
Includes:
- earliest recoverable sayings/actions;
- kingdom proclamation;
- ethical teaching;
- prayer;
- prophetic critique;
- earliest encounter traditions;
graded by source confidence.

### Qur'anic / Muhammad Witness Corpus
Distinguishes:
- Qur'an as early proclamation;
- earliest recoverable biographical material;
- later hadith;
- tafsir/fiqh/theology.

No later report receives automatic equality with the earliest layer.

## Level 2 — Parallel Prophetic / High-Value Witnesses
Candidate sources with unusual comparative relevance:
- Gathas/Zarathustra;
- Psalms and wisdom traditions;
- founder-associated Sikh bani/Guru Nanak;
- other early prophetic/revelatory sources as research develops.

## Level 3 — Contemplative and Wisdom Library
Includes material selected for insight/practice rather than binding revelation:
- Sufi;
- Jewish mystical;
- Christian contemplative;
- Buddhist;
- Yoga/Upanishadic;
- philosophical/ethical;
- other global traditions.

## Level 4 — Interpretive Traditions
Rabbinic, patristic, theological, legal, mystical, philosophical, and later commentarial corpora.

Valuable, never automatically binding.

## Level 5 — Contemporary Knowledge
History, archaeology, linguistics, psychology, neuroscience, science, philosophy, ethics, and HTERP research.

## Open-canon rule
New material may enter Levels 2–5 by documented review.
Level 1 changes require a major constitutional review because it defines the chosen identity of the framework.

## No inerrancy shortcut
A text can be foundational without every proposition being treated as historically, scientifically, or morally infallible.
