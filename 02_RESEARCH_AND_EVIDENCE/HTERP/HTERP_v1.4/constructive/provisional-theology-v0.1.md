# Provisional Theology v0.1

## F-001 — The Most High
**Status:** Faith postulate.

There is an ultimate transcendent Source of reality, truth, and moral significance, exceeding every human name and image.

## F-002 — Divine transcendence
**Status:** Faith postulate with strong Abrahamic precedent.

The Most High is not adequately reducible to a human-shaped corporeal being or an idol.

## F-003 — Names are referential, not exhaustive
**Status:** Provisional interpretation.

El, Elyon, YHWH, God, Allah, and other titles may function as historically conditioned ways of referring toward the divine. Historical identity between cults/names must still be studied; ontological identity is not assumed merely from later harmonization.

## F-004 — Revelation may be real but filtered
**Status:** Permitted central model, not established conclusion.

If the Most High communicates with humans, communication is mediated through human cognition, culture, language, memory, and institutions.

## F-005 — No prophet is presently declared infallible
**Status:** Constitutional rule.

Prophets/teachers may be genuine witnesses and still be mediated through human limitations or later transmission.

## F-006 — The moral life matters
**Status:** Faith/ethical commitment strongly supported as cross-tradition practice.

Truth, justice, mercy, generosity, humility, restraint, and transformation are treated as central signs of religious maturity.

## F-007 — Idolatry generalized
**Status:** Constructive interpretation.

Idolatry includes not only worship of images but treating any finite thing as ultimate:
- wealth;
- power;
- nation;
- institution;
- leader;
- scripture-as-object;
- ideology;
- ego.

## F-008 — Humanity is epistemically limited
**Status:** Research-supported philosophical conclusion.

Religious confidence must remain proportionate to evidence.

## O-001 — Jesus
Open questions:
- prophet?
- Messiah, and in what sense?
- Son of God, and in what sense?
- preexistent/divine?
- how should resurrection claims be understood?

The framework may honor Jesus deeply without requiring a final Christological answer at v0.1.

## O-002 — Muhammad
Open questions:
- genuine prophet?
- degree of reliability of individual traditions?
- meaning/finality of “seal of the prophets”?
- whether prophecy in some broader sense can continue?

No finality doctrine is presently mandatory.

## O-003 — Torah law
Open:
- which laws represent enduring moral principles;
- which are covenant-specific/historical;
- how Jesus/Qur'an relate to Torah;
- what ritual practices should be voluntarily restored.

## O-004 — Spirit world
Angels, jinn, spirits, ancestors, deceased persons, and other apparent agents remain open ontological questions.

## O-005 — Afterlife
Resurrection, intermediate states, reincarnation/rebirth, judgment, and other models remain under investigation.

## O-006 — Ultimate personality
The framework worships the Most High in personal language as a devotional practice while acknowledging that ultimate reality may exceed ordinary personal/nonpersonal categories.
