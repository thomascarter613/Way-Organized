# Rule of Practice v0.1

This is a provisional rule designed to be usable before final metaphysics are settled.

## Daily

### Dawn — Orientation
- wash or otherwise prepare intentionally;
- stand, bow, kneel, sit, or prostrate according to ability;
- acknowledge the Most High / Ultimate Reality;
- state an intention for truth, justice, mercy, and restraint;
- brief reading from the Level 1 spine;
- five to fifteen minutes of silence or contemplative attention.

### Midday — Recollection
- pause ordinary work;
- short prayer/remembrance;
- examine whether the morning has aligned with the Ethical Covenant;
- correct course immediately where possible.

### Evening — Examen
Review:
- where was I truthful?
- where did I act unjustly?
- whom did I help?
- whom did I harm?
- what appetite or fear governed me?
- what needs repair tomorrow?
Then gratitude, repentance, and silence.

## Weekly

### Sacred assembly / rest period
A seven-day rhythm is provisionally retained from the Abrahamic spine.

The community may choose a stable weekly sacred period devoted to:
- rest from unnecessary commerce/work;
- shared prayer;
- Torah/Prophets/Jesus/Qur'an study;
- comparative wisdom reading;
- common meal;
- charity/service;
- family/community reconciliation.

Whether the sacred day is Friday, Saturday, Sunday, or another agreed period is not yet doctrinally fixed.

## Regular disciplines

### Study
Historical and devotional reading occur together but are labeled separately.

### Fasting
Voluntary fasting may be used for discipline, solidarity, prayer, and self-knowledge, with health/safety exceptions.

### Giving
Set aside a regular portion of resources or labor for direct aid and community good.

### Service
Religious life must include outward acts, not contemplation alone.

### Silence
Practice periods without speech/media to observe cognition and desire.

### Physical care
Sleep, nutrition, exercise, medical care, and mental-health care are not signs of weak faith.

## Advanced practice
Long fasting, sleep restriction, intense retreat, breath manipulation, prolonged isolation, and psychologically destabilizing techniques require safety review and should not be treated as necessary for spiritual legitimacy.

## Anti-performance rule
No one receives spiritual rank merely for:
- visions;
- tongues;
- prophecy;
- ecstatic states;
- ascetic extremity;
- claimed supernatural gifts.

Character and accountability outrank spectacle.
