# HTERP Hypothesis Ledger — v0.4 After WP-003

## H0 — Construction / literary development / fraud
Still established as a partial cause. WP-003 further strengthens the literary-memory component for Abraham, Moses, Melchizedek, and scriptural founder narratives where contemporary documentation is unavailable.

## H1 — Cognitive / neurological mediation
Unchanged in basic standing: strongly established partial cause. WP-003 adds dreams as an ancient central channel, increasing the relevance of ordinary dream-generation mechanisms to ancient revelation claims.

## H2 — Cultural/social shaping
Strengthened further. Dream manuals, omen sciences, El/YHWH integration, Zhou Heaven, Upanishadic knowledge systems, and organized Daoist revelation all show that categories of “what the beyond is” and “how it speaks” are historically structured.

## H3 — Transcendent impersonal reality
Remains open. Upanishadic and some Indian/Chinese philosophical developments supply major non-agent models of ultimate reality/knowledge, but no independent external verification follows from textual history alone.

## H4 — Transcendent personal intelligence/intelligences
Remains open. The history of El, YHWH, ancestors, gods, celestial beings, and revelatory agents is compatible with multiple H4 models, but historical cult development cannot itself verify entity ontology.

## H5 — Mixed causal model
Remains operationally required.

## H6 — Category conflation
Strengthened. “God,” “deity,” “ancestor,” “spirit,” “Heaven,” “Dao,” “brahman,” “awakened knowledge,” and “revelation” are not interchangeable categories.

## H7 — Progressive-integration hypothesis
New research hypothesis: some religious traditions arise by integrating older divine names, cults, practices, and concepts into a progressively more unified theological framework.

Evidence compatible:
- El/YHWH integration;
- absorption/reinterpretation of older divine-council language;
- Daoist appropriation/revelatory rereading of earlier Dao texts;
- scriptural systems that reinterpret previous revelation traditions.

H7 is historical, not yet metaphysical. It does not decide whether integration reflects theological discovery, political/social consolidation, genuine progressive revelation, or mixtures of these.
