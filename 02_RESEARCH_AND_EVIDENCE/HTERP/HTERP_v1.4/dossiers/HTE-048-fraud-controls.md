# HTE-048 — Fraud and Pseudo-Contact Controls

## Purpose

Any research program studying spirits, revelation, prophecy, channeling, or hidden information that lacks a fraud-control corpus will systematically overestimate extraordinary explanations.

Fraud is therefore not an insult category. It is an ordinary causal hypothesis to be tested.

## Historical evidence

Nineteenth- and early-twentieth-century Spiritualism generated a large literature of séances, physical mediumship, spirit photography, slate writing, trumpet voices, and other claimed communications.

Harry Houdini's 1920s campaign against fraudulent mediums is well documented in the Library of Congress Houdini collection. It records exposure techniques and public demonstrations involving methods such as slate writing, spirit photography, and trumpet mediumship.

The Society for Psychical Research also developed experimental controls specifically because apparently successful thought-transmission and mediumistic performances could be undermined by deception or uncontrolled signaling.

## What this establishes

Humans can deliberately produce highly convincing simulations of:
- messages from the dead;
- hidden writing;
- apparitions/photographs;
- voice phenomena;
- apparently inaccessible information;
- “miraculous” physical effects.

Therefore:
**information anomaly ≠ spirit contact** until ordinary access and deceptive technique are actively excluded.

## Fraud-control checklist

1. Search participant and investigator access to target information.
2. Separate subject from target generation.
3. Use secure randomization.
4. Timestamp and hash predictions before outcome.
5. Record the full session, not only hits.
6. Prevent cueing and sensory leakage.
7. Predefine success criteria.
8. Use independent judges blind to condition.
9. Preserve misses and failed sessions.
10. Check selective reporting.
11. Replicate with adversarial observers.
12. Audit financial/status incentives.

## Hypothesis analysis
H0 is **known to be true for some cases**. It cannot be generalized without evidence, but every evidential design must be strong enough to survive it.

## Important limitation
Exposing one fraudulent medium says nothing about another case. Fraud controls increase rigor; they do not license universal dismissal.
