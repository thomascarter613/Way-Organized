# HTE-049 — Classical Yoga and Samādhi

## Why added
WP-004 requires a cultivated non-Abrahamic contemplative control distinct from the earlier Upanishadic and Vedic dossiers.

## Historical development
Oxford Bibliographies places major meditative developments in the Upanishadic period and the systematic Yoga Sūtra tradition in the early centuries CE. The Yoga Sūtras synthesize earlier practices of concentration, breath/mental discipline, and liberating cognition.

## Encounter type
This is not ordinarily “a god gives a message.”

It is better modeled as:
**disciplined attentional practice → increasingly concentrated states → altered relation between awareness and mental activity → liberating discriminative knowledge**.

Theistic elements exist in classical Yoga, including Īśvara, but the system's epistemic mechanism is not reducible to messenger prophecy.

## Phenomenology / claimed capacities
Classical Yoga literature discusses:
- concentration;
- meditation;
- samādhi;
- altered self/mental-process relation;
- extraordinary cognition/perception;
- liberation.

Claims of extraordinary powers should enter HTERP-AI only when prospectively testable; textual claims alone are historical evidence of belief/practice.

## Comparison value
Yoga demonstrates that deliberate cognitive training can be construed as a route to knowledge normally unavailable to ordinary consciousness.
