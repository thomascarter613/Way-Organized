# HTE-033B — Nyikina/Marlaloo Songline Public Documentation

## Scope
Public AIATSIS documentation only.

AIATSIS describes the Marlaloo songline as a living song/ceremony tradition linking people, Country, sacred sites, story, ancestral events, and authorized knowledge holders. Elders determine legitimate succession and transmission.

This is principally a **community-governed chain of sacred knowledge**, not a recoverable single ancient encounter. Its research value lies in oral preservation, geographical anchoring, social authorization, and the relation between sacred story and practical knowledge.

Where publicly appropriate and community-authorized, externally checkable geographical/environmental claims could help measure transmission fidelity independently of metaphysical claims.

**Baseline:** AIATSIS, “The Marlaloo Songline”; “Songs of Australia.”
