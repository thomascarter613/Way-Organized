# HTE-020 — Mahavira and the Jain Liberation Tradition

**Figure:** Vardhamana Mahavira, remembered as a contemporary of the Buddha.  
**Mode:** ILL / perfected knowledge rather than creator-deity revelation.

Oxford scholarship describes Jainism as emerging around a charismatic ascetic teacher, Mahavira, who organized followers and taught a demanding path centered on nonviolence, truthfulness, non-stealing, non-possession, celibacy/continence, asceticism, correct knowledge, and liberation from rebirth.

Jain tradition places Mahavira as the twenty-fourth Tirthankara rather than the absolute beginning of truth. A Jina is a liberated knower/teacher; the cosmology does not depend on a creator God delivering revelation.

## Source caution
As with Buddhism, the surviving textual system is not a contemporaneous transcript of Mahavira. South Asian oral/manuscript history requires separate reconstruction of founder teaching, early community, canon, and later narrative.

## HTERP significance
Mahavira strengthens the category **authoritative awakening/omniscient liberation teacher without creator-revelation**.
