# HTE-034 — Maya Divination and Divine Communication

**Modes:** DIV, ritual communication; VIS/MSG in some contexts.

A 2024 study of the Maya community of Chan identifies a building containing divining tokens and an incised conjuring pattern, showing divination embedded in community life. Metropolitan Museum documentation describes ritual music, dance, prayer, food, and drink as channels for communicating with divine powers.

The practice evidence is strong; subjective phenomenology is much weaker. Maya evidence is geographically independent of Old World prophecy, but generic divination is a universal response to uncertainty and therefore weak metaphysical convergence by itself.

**Baseline:** Cynthia Robin, “Time as Ordinary Practice: A Divination Building at Chan” (2024); Met Maya ritual-vessel documentation.
