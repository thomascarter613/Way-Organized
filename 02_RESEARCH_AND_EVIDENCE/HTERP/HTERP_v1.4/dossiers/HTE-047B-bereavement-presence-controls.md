# HTE-047B — Bereavement Sensed-Presence Controls

Interdisciplinary review literature reports sensory and quasi-sensory experiences of deceased persons across sensed presence, visual, auditory, tactile, olfactory, and dream modalities. Many are benign and meaningful rather than pathological.

For apparition/mediumship analysis, HTERP distinguishes spontaneous private bereavement experience from information-bearing encounter, shared perception, mediumistic performance, and later interpretation.

A felt presence or emotionally fitting message is weak evidence for survival because such experiences occur naturally in bereavement. Stronger evidence requires specific inaccessible information documented before verification.

**Baseline:** interdisciplinary bereavement review (2020) and later phenomenology reviews.
