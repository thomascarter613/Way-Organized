# HTE-010 — Abraham Traditions

## Historical status
HTERP does not currently treat Abraham as a directly documented historical experiencer. Ronald Hendel's historical-memory approach is a useful baseline: the patriarchal narratives combine historical memory, folklore, cultural identity, and literary construction.

Hendel argues that some elements may preserve genuinely ancient memories, especially:
- pre-Israelite El worship;
- old ancestral/patriarchal traditions;
- memory of an Amorite ancestral homeland.

At the same time, there is no clear evidence allowing us to recover an original Abraham, date him securely, or verify individual Genesis episodes as biography.

## Encounter profile in Genesis
Abraham traditions include:
- divine speech/call;
- covenant/promise;
- visions;
- dream-like revelation;
- angelic/divine visitors;
- intercession;
- testing/sacrifice tradition.

These belong to different literary layers and should not be flattened into one event record.

## HTERP result
Use Abraham as a **tradition-complex about ancestral encounter**, not as P1/P2 phenomenological evidence.

## Baseline
Ronald Hendel, *Remembering Abraham* (OUP, 2005), especially “Historical Memories in the Patriarchal Narratives.”
