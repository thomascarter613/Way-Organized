# HTE-017 — Ezekiel

**Setting:** Judahite exile in Babylonia, early sixth century BCE.  
**Mode:** VIS, MSG, visionary transport/JRN-like phenomena.

Ezekiel 1–3 contains an unusually elaborate commission: storm/fire/luminous phenomena, composite living beings, wheels, throne imagery, bodily collapse, divine speech, scroll ingestion, and commissioning. Later material contains visionary transport, temple visions, dry bones, and a future temple.

Modern scholarship ties the book strongly to the Babylonian destruction/exile crisis. The richness of the phenomenology is valuable, but detail itself does not establish literal autobiography. Luminous figures, presence, collapse, transport, and vivid imagery have natural control analogues in dreams and some neurological states.

**Baseline:** Andrew Mein, “Ezekiel: Structure, Themes, and Contested Issues,” *Oxford Handbook of the Prophets*.
