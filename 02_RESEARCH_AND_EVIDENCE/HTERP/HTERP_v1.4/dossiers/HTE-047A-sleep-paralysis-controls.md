# HTE-047A — Sleep Paralysis / Hypnagogic-Hypnopompic Controls

Recent review literature documents felt presence/intruder experiences, human or nonhuman figures, auditory and visual hallucinations, terror, chest pressure, floating/flying, autoscopy, and out-of-body sensations during sleep paralysis. Cultural narratives strongly affect interpretation.

HTERP therefore treats these features as nondiscriminating without additional evidence:
- bedside figure;
- voice during sleep-wake transition;
- paralysis;
- chest pressure;
- sensed evil presence;
- floating/out-of-body sensation.

This does not prove every nocturnal religious encounter is sleep paralysis. It provides a demonstrated natural mechanism producing closely overlapping phenomenology.

**Baseline:** 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis and 2024 mechanisms review of sleep paralysis.
