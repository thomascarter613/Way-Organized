# HTE-004 — Mesopotamian Technical Divination

**Mode:** DIV.  
**Major forms:** extispicy, celestial omen interpretation, lots, oil/incense/flour signs, birth omens, terrestrial/atmospheric signs.

Mesopotamian divination was a highly developed scholarly enterprise. Oxford scholarship emphasizes the large textual record, sustained transmission, and centrality of disciplines such as extispicy and omen astrology in Babylonian and Assyrian court culture.

Scott Noegel's survey stresses how broad the sign-world could become: solicited techniques existed alongside unsolicited dreams, malformed births, necromancy, celestial events, animal behavior, and other phenomena.

## HTERP significance
This is evidence of **systematized inference from signs**, not necessarily altered consciousness. It creates an important ancestor/comparison class for later omen, oracle, and prophetic systems.

## Key research question
Can any technical divination corpus be scored prospectively or retrospectively under strict pre-event dating and outcome controls?
