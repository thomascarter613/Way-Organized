# HTE-022 — Daoist Revelatory Traditions

## Organized revelation
Oxford scholarship describes organized Daoism as emerging in the second century CE around revelation attributed to a divinized Laozi to Zhang Ling/Zhang Daoling, forming the Celestial Masters.

Later Shangqing (“Highest Clarity”) traditions are based on revelation corpora associated with the fourth century CE and emphasize visualization, inner deities, meditation, chants, talismans, and revealed texts.

## HTERP significance
Daoism is important because it demonstrates a historically documentable process in which:
**earlier philosophical/sacred text → divine re-reading → founder revelation → organized religious lineage → later competing revelation corpora**.

It therefore provides a powerful comparison with Abrahamic succession claims without sharing their genealogy.

## Source caution
Do not treat the ancient *Daodejing* and second-century organized Daoist revelation as one unchanged phenomenon.
