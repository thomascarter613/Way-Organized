# HTE-051 — Experimental Mediumship / Anomalous Information

## Why this is a priority case
Unlike most ancient revelation claims, mediumship can be tested under blinded conditions with objectively scorable information.

## Positive literature
A 2020 meta-analysis of 18 experiments reported an aggregate performance above chance and concluded that some mediums appeared to obtain information through unknown means.

A 2022 triple-blind study with nine mediums and 38 readings reported:
- intended readings identified 65.8% of the time versus 50% chance;
- higher scores for intended than control readings.

## Negative literature
A rigorous 2005 test designed to prevent common methodological weaknesses reported results that **did not support genuine mediumistic ability**.

Therefore the experimental literature is not one-sided.

## Why H4 does not follow from above-chance scoring
Even if an anomalous-information effect replicates, candidate explanations would include:
- unnoticed leakage;
- analysis flexibility;
- sensory information not adequately controlled;
- fraud;
- telepathy from living persons, if one permits psi hypotheses;
- precognition/clairvoyance;
- statistical artifact;
- communication with deceased persons.

“Above chance” and “survival after death” are different propositions.

## HTERP provisional classification
**AI-2/AI-3 candidate, disputed.**

Some studies use strong blinding, but HTERP requires:
- preregistration;
- adversarial multisite replication;
- independent statisticians;
- standardized target generation;
- public full-data reporting;
- labs with no organizational stake in positive outcome.

## Proposed HTERP replication
Three-site design:
1. proponent-led laboratory;
2. skeptical laboratory;
3. neutral academic replication site.

Shared protocol and randomization server.
Medium receives a minimal unique target code rather than identifying biographical data where possible.
Sitters score target + multiple decoys.
All sessions recorded.
Analysis locked before unblinding.

## Current conclusion
Mediumship is **the strongest live anomalous-information lead in the HTERP corpus so far**, but the existing literature is insufficient to identify a discarnate source.

## Baseline sources
- Sarraf, Woodley of Menie & Tressoldi (2020), meta-analysis.
- Tressoldi et al. (2022), triple-blind study.
- O'Keeffe & Wiseman (2005), rigorous null test.
