# HTE-024 — Paul: First-Person Vision and Revelation Claims

**Period:** mid-first century CE.  
**Mode:** VIS, MSG, JRN.

Paul's undisputed letters claim a foundational appearance/revelation of Christ (Galatians 1; 1 Corinthians 15) and later “visions and revelations,” including the ascent/heavenly experience in 2 Corinthians 12. Because these are first-person authorial claims, Paul is one of the strongest ancient cases for establishing that an experiencer himself reported such events.

That strength must not be confused with independent verification of the external object. The letters do not by themselves establish whether anyone else perceived the event, what neurocognitive state occurred, or the metaphysical identity of the figure.

**Baseline:** Matthew T. Sharp, “Visions,” *Divination and Philosophy in the Letters of Paul*; Oxford Bibliographies, “Second Corinthians.”
