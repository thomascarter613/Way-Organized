# HTE-038 — Emanuel Swedenborg

## Identification
- **Life:** 1688–1772
- **Earlier career:** scientist, engineer, mining official, natural philosopher
- **Transition:** intense dream/vision period in the 1740s
- **Modes:** DRM, VIS, AUD, CHA/JRN-like spirit-world communication

## Source advantage
Swedenborg kept a dream diary during 1743–44. Kelly Bulkeley describes it as a remarkable first-person record of the spiritual awakening that preceded his mature visionary Christianity.

His later works claim sustained intercourse with spirits, angels, heaven, and hell.

## Phenomenology
Reported features include:
- vivid dreams;
- voices;
- visions;
- sensed spiritual presences;
- extended dialogues;
- highly elaborated otherworld cosmography.

Modern historical-psychological analysis notes that later attempts to diagnose Swedenborg with schizophrenia or epilepsy are underdetermined; his experiences can be discussed as “hallucinations in the sane” without retrospective pathology.

## Anomalous-information claims
The Stockholm fire episode is the most famous claimed clairvoyant event: Swedenborg, while in Gothenburg in 1759, was later reported to have described a fire then occurring in Stockholm. Kant investigated reports some years later and initially regarded the case as unusually impressive.

### HTERP status
**AI-1 historical candidate**, not AI-3:
- event precedes telecommunications;
- some near-contemporary investigation exists;
- but no secure pre-event timestamped record of Swedenborg's statements survives at the standard required by HTERP-AI;
- witness chains and exact timing remain historical reconstruction.

## Result
Swedenborg is excellent evidence for sustained first-person visionary phenomenology and a poor substitute for a controlled anomalous-information experiment.

## Baseline sources

- Simon R. Jones & Charles Fernyhough, “Talking back to the spirits.”
- historical literature on the Stockholm-fire claim, treated as contested.
