# HTE-015 — Isaiah Traditions

**Historical anchor:** eighth-century BCE Isaiah ben Amoz.  
**Mode:** VIS, MSG.

Isaiah 6 gives a classic throne-room commission: enthroned YHWH, seraphim, holiness, purification, and commission. Yet the canonical Book of Isaiah extends far beyond one eighth-century lifetime. Modern scholarship treats the book as an Isaianic tradition repeatedly preserved, expanded, and shaped by tradents across centuries.

HTERP models: **historical prophetic core → Isaianic tradition/school → multi-stage literary book**. The literary detail of a vision is evidence about the surviving tradition, not automatically an autobiographical transcript.

**Baseline:** Ulrich Berges, “Isaiah: Structure, Themes, and Contested Issues,” *Oxford Handbook of the Prophets*; *Oxford Handbook of Isaiah*.
