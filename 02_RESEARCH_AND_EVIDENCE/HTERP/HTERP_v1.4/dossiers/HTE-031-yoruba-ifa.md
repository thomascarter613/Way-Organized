# HTE-031 — Yoruba Ifá Divination

**Practitioner:** babaláwo.  
**Mode:** DIV.  
**Claimed divine intelligence:** Ọ̀rúnmìlà, associated with wisdom/divination/destiny.

Ifá uses sacred palm nuts or chain procedures, trays, tappers, and an extensive odù/verse corpus. Metropolitan Museum documentation describes Ifá priests as communicating with Ọ̀rúnmìlà to obtain insight concerning an individual's destiny; a seventeenth–nineteenth-century Owo ivory vessel demonstrates material depth before modern ethnography.

The epistemic sequence is roughly: ritual sign generation → odù identification → retrieval/interpretation of verse corpus → counsel/ritual action. It is therefore structurally closer to expert divination than spontaneous auditory prophecy.

Ifá is prospectively testable in principle using blinded clients, predefined objective outcomes, comparison conditions, and cold-reading/information-leakage controls.

**Baseline:** Met Àgéré Ifá vessel; Met *Art and Oracle*; Smithsonian Ifá divination cup.
