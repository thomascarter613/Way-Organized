# HTE-016 — Jeremiah

**Setting:** late seventh–early sixth centuries BCE Judah.  
**Mode:** MSG, VIS, symbolic action.

Jeremiah 1 portrays divine word, vocation, objection, reassurance, visionary signs, and commission. The book also contains laments/confessions, symbolic acts, political conflict, and judgment oracles.

Its textual history is especially important: Masoretic and Septuagintal forms differ notably in ordering and length, and contemporary scholarship treats textual variation as evidence for literary evolution. HTERP therefore grades individual units and witnesses rather than treating one canonical form as a pristine encounter record.

**Baseline:** Mark Leuchter, “Jeremiah: Structure, Themes, and Contested Issues”; Eberhard Bons, “Textual Criticism of the Prophetic Corpus.”
