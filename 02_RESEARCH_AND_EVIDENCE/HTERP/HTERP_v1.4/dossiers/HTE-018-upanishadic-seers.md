# HTE-018 — Classical Upanishadic Seers

**Mode:** ILL, UNI, philosophical-dialogical sacred insight.

The older Upanishads were composed from roughly the eighth century BCE onward. Oxford's 2024 guide emphasizes central concerns including **atman**, **brahman**, karma, rebirth, liberation, knowledge, and sacred sound.

The epistemic model differs sharply from messenger prophecy. Much of the material presents teacher-student dialogue and transformative knowledge about the relation of inner self and cosmic reality. Liberation follows insight into reality rather than obedience to a speaking creator's commissioned message.

## HTERP significance
Upanishadic material gives a major ancient example of **ultimate knowledge as realization/identity insight**, helping distinguish H3-style impersonal/structural transcendence from H4-style agent revelation.

## Dependency
It belongs to the Vedic cultural continuum and therefore is not independent of earlier Vedic categories.

## Baseline
Signe Cohen, *The Classical Upaniṣads: A Guide* (OUP, 2024).
