# HTE-009 — Greek Oracle Traditions

**Scope:** Delphi, Dodona, Didyma, Claros and related consultation traditions.  
**Mode:** DIV; possible POS/MSG in literary descriptions.

Nissinen distinguishes direct epigraphic evidence—especially consultation tablets from Dodona and inscriptions from Didyma/Claros—from literary narratives about famous oracles. No original Delphic oracle report survives, despite Delphi's fame.

Typical questions concern marriage, travel, property, war, health, civic decisions, ritual obligations, and uncertain outcomes. The key HTERP rule is sanctuary-specific evidence grading: direct consultation tablets and later famous stories are not the same source class.

**Baseline:** Nissinen, “Greek Sources,” *Ancient Prophecy*; Esther Eidinow, *Oracles, Curses, and Risk Among the Ancient Greeks*.
