# HTE-033A — Yolngu Wangarr / Djang’kawu Public Tradition

## Scope and ethics
Publicly documented material only. Restricted ceremonial knowledge is not treated as data to be extracted.

National Museum of Australia documentation describes *Wangarr* as a Yolngu term referring to ancestral past and ancestral beings. Public Yalangbara material integrates ancestral beings, sacred objects, clan/moiety organization, ceremony, and place; some knowledge and objects are explicitly restricted.

HTERP therefore does **not** model this as “one visionary founded a religion.” It is better represented as a living sacred-law/land/ancestral transmission system in which ontology, place, kinship, ritual, and authority are integrated.

The tradition tests whether HTERP can handle non-founder sacred systems without forcing them into Western prophecy categories.

**Baseline:** National Museum of Australia, Yalangbara glossary/materials.
