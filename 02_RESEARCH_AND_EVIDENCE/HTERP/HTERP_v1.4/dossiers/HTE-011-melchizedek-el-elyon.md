# HTE-011 — Melchizedek / El Elyon

## Source
Genesis 14 presents Melchizedek as king of Salem and priest of **El Elyon**, blessing Abram in the name of the creator/possessor of heaven and earth.

## Historical problem
The age, unity, and compositional history of Genesis 14 are heavily disputed. Robert Cargill's 2019 study argues for substantial scribal development and specifically proposes that the identification of El Elyon with YHWH in Gen 14:22 is secondary. This is one scholarly reconstruction, not consensus established beyond dispute.

## HTERP significance
The passage demonstrates at minimum that the received biblical tradition can imagine a legitimate non-Abrahamic priest of “God Most High.” Historically it may preserve or construct a bridge between older El/Elyon language and Yahwistic theology.

## Rule
Do not use Melchizedek as direct evidence that a historical pre-Israelite monotheistic priesthood is proven. Treat him as a high-value textual witness to Israelite memory/theological integration of older divine traditions.

## Baseline
Robert R. Cargill, *Melchizedek, King of Sodom* (OUP, 2019), especially “From El Elyon to YHWH.”
