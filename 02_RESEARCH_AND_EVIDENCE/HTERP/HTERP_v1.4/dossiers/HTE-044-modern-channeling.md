# HTE-044 — Modern Channeling

## Definition
Channeling is the claimed communication of information through a living person from a source said to exist outside the channeler's ordinary self.

## Clinical/psychological status
A 2022 review argues that many channeling experiences are better described as nonpathological nonordinary or dissociative-trance/possession-like experiences than automatically as mental disorder.

That answers a clinical classification question, not the entity question.

## Competing source models
- deliberate performance/fraud;
- imaginative role enactment;
- dissociation;
- implicit/unconscious knowledge;
- culturally trained voice/persona generation;
- anomalous information acquisition;
- discarnate/noncorporeal agent.

## HTERP requirement
Entity identity cannot be inferred from the channeler's conviction or the voice's claimed name.

A strong agent case would require:
- information unavailable to channeler/interviewer;
- stable cross-session identity;
- blinded independent scoring;
- resistance to cueing;
- preregistered questions;
- reproducible performance.

## Result
Channeling is an excellent H1/H2/H4 discrimination target because the alleged source is explicit and can be interrogated prospectively.

## Baseline source
“Channeling: A Non-pathological Possession and Dissociative Identity Experience or Something Else?” (2022, PMC).
