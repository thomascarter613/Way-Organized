# HTE-014 — Amos and Hosea

**Setting:** eighth-century BCE Israel/Judah contexts.  
**Mode:** MSG, VIS, symbolic prophetic action.

Modern scholarship increasingly distinguishes historical prophets from the literary books carrying their names. The Book of Amos is sophisticated prophetic poetry whose biography and redaction history are more difficult to reconstruct than older readings assumed. Recent Hosea scholarship explicitly distinguishes a possible eighth-century historical prophet, later cultural memory of that prophet, and the final book; some models posit deep redactional transformation.

The books preserve divine speech, visions/symbolic imagery, covenantal accusation, social critique, and calls to return to YHWH. HTERP therefore analyzes units rather than treating every verse as a verbatim transcript.

**Baseline:** *Oxford Handbook of the Minor Prophets*, “Amos”; *Oxford Handbook of Hosea* (2024), historical/remembered prophet and redaction chapters.
