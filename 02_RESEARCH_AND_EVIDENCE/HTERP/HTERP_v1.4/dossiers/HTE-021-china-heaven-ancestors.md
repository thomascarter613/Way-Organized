# HTE-021 — Shang-to-Zhou Ancestors and Heaven

## Shang baseline
Archaeology and oracle bones show a royal religion in which ancestor communication by divination was essential to governance.

## Zhou development
Richard von Glahn's historical synthesis describes the Zhou as inheriting much Shang ritual culture while developing an early **cult of Heaven (Tian)** with a more moral-equilibrium orientation.

## HTERP significance
This creates a valuable internal civilizational development:
**ancestor/divinatory royal communication → moralized Heaven/political order**.

It resembles, at a very high level, the moralization of divine authority seen elsewhere, but historical independence does not itself demonstrate a single transcendent source.

## Key questions
- when does Tian function as agent versus impersonal order?
- how does Mandate-of-Heaven moral logic alter earlier ancestor/divination practice?
- how much continuity versus reinterpretation exists between Shang Di/ancestors and Zhou Tian?

## Baseline
Richard von Glahn, “Ancestors, Ghosts, and Gods in Ancient China.”
