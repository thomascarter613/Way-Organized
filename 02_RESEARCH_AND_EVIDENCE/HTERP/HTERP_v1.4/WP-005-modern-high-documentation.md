# HTERP-WP-005 — Modern High-Documentation Cases

**Status:** completed baseline adversarial pass  
**Version:** 0.6  
**Scope:** HTE-038–044 plus HTE-051 experimental mediumship/anomalous information.

## Objective

Exploit the documentary advantages of modern cases:
- founder-era manuscripts;
- newspapers and diaries;
- multiple versions of testimony;
- institutional investigation;
- audio/video in contemporary cases;
- blinded experiments;
- known fraud mechanisms;
- prospective testing.

Modernity does **not** make a supernatural claim true. It makes the evidence architecture easier to inspect.

## Cases

- HTE-038 Emanuel Swedenborg
- HTE-039 nineteenth-century Spiritualism
- HTE-040 Joseph Smith
- HTE-041 Bahá'u'lláh
- HTE-042 Marian apparition traditions, especially Lourdes and Fátima
- HTE-043 contemporary Pentecostal/charismatic prophecy
- HTE-044 modern channeling
- HTE-051 experimental mediumship / anomalous information

## Major results

### W5-01 — Modern founder narratives permit version-growth analysis
Joseph Smith's First Vision is preserved in multiple firsthand and contemporary accounts written during his lifetime. This is methodologically valuable because development can be studied directly rather than imagined.

### W5-02 — A first-person archive can document emergence of a visionary worldview
Swedenborg's 1743–44 dream diary gives unusually close access to the transition from scientist/administrator to visionary religious author. It establishes phenomenological self-report much better than external spirit ontology.

### W5-03 — Spiritualism simultaneously strengthens H0 and creates testable H4 claims
The movement contains documented fraud and performance techniques, but it also generated controlled experimental traditions designed specifically to eliminate cueing and deception. It is therefore a better laboratory than either blanket belief or blanket dismissal.

### W5-04 — Apparitions must separate primary visionaries from crowd phenomena
At Lourdes, Bernadette's companions did not share her visual apparition. At Fátima, three children were the apparition claimants while a large crowd later reported heterogeneous solar phenomena. “Thousands witnessed the apparition” is therefore analytically misleading.

### W5-05 — Institutional recognition is not metaphysical verification
The Catholic Church's 2024 norms explicitly distinguish pastoral permission/spiritual fruits from declaring an event supernatural. HTERP adopts an analogous distinction.

### W5-06 — Pentecostal prophecy is highly suitable for prospective testing
Modern prophecies can be recorded before outcomes. Yet there is little reason to rely on retrospective “hits” when PRED-3/PRED-4 designs are possible.

### W5-07 — Channeling is often nonpathological but remains ontologically unresolved
Clinical/psychological literature supports describing many channeling cases as nonordinary or dissociative-trance-like experiences without assuming psychiatric disorder. That classification does not establish discarnate entities.

### W5-08 — Mediumship is the most important live anomalous-information candidate found so far
Some blinded experiments and a 2020 meta-analysis report above-chance information accuracy; other rigorous tests have reported null results. The literature is therefore **contested, not settled**. HTERP promotes it to a high-priority adversarial replication target rather than treating it as proof of survival after death.
