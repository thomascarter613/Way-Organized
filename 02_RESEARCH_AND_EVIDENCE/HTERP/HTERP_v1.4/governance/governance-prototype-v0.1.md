# Governance Prototype v0.1

## Principle
Charisma, revelation claims, money, and governance authority should not concentrate in one person.

## Community Assembly
All Covenant Members in good standing.

Responsibilities:
- elect/remove council members;
- approve major budgets;
- ratify constitutional amendments;
- receive annual reports.

## Stewardship Council
Prototype: 5–9 members with staggered terms.

Responsibilities:
- ordinary governance;
- finances;
- staffing;
- safeguarding oversight;
- community operations.

No lifetime seats.

## Teaching Council
Qualified teachers/readers.

Responsibilities:
- historical/source accuracy;
- curriculum;
- liturgy/study guidance;
- representing open questions honestly.

Cannot alone control finances or constitutional change.

## HTERP / Evidence Council
Maintains:
- research standards;
- source registry;
- doctrine status labels;
- version history;
- empirical protocols.

At least one member should be independent of the teaching leadership where feasible.

## Safeguarding Officer / Committee
Independent reporting access.
Cannot be subordinate solely to a leader accused in a complaint.

## Founder role
Possible roles:
- founding teacher;
- initial convener;
- research director.

Founder has:
- no automatic doctrinal infallibility;
- no unilateral amendment power;
- no guaranteed lifetime executive control;
- no immunity from removal.

## Terms
Prototype:
- council: 3-year staggered terms;
- chair: 1-year renewable, capped consecutive service;
- teachers periodically reaffirmed based on conduct and competence.

## Decisions
Ordinary: majority.
Major budget/leadership removal: defined supermajority.
Constitutional/ethical covenant change: 3/4 Assembly plus review period.
Core safeguarding protections cannot be suspended by emergency charismatic claim.

## Transparency
Publish:
- governing documents;
- doctrine versions;
- financial summaries;
- conflict disclosures;
- disciplinary process;
- amendment history.
