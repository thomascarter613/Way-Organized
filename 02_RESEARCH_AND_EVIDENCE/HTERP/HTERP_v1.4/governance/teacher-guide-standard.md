# Teacher / Guide Formation Standard

A teacher is not certified because of visions or charisma.

## Required competencies
- Level 1 source knowledge;
- TRACE method;
- ability to distinguish history from theology;
- basic comparative-religion literacy;
- safeguarding;
- conflict-of-interest awareness;
- referral boundaries for medical/mental-health/legal issues;
- facilitation without coercion.

## Character expectations
- truthfulness;
- financial transparency;
- sexual boundaries;
- willingness to be corrected;
- no demand for personal loyalty;
- no manipulation through prophecy.

## Disqualifying / suspension concerns
- abuse;
- fraud;
- financial exploitation;
- concealment of safeguarding violations;
- repeated misrepresentation of evidence;
- retaliation against questioners/complainants.

## Supernatural claims
A teacher who reports a revelation submits it as:
**personal encounter claim**, not immediate doctrine.

It enters the same HTERP process as everyone else's claim.
