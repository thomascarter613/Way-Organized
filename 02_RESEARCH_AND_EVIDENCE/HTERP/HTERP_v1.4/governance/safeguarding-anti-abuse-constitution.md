# Safeguarding and Anti-Abuse Constitution

## 1. No revelation exemption
“I was told by God” does not exempt anyone from ordinary ethical, legal, financial, medical, or safeguarding accountability.

## 2. Child safety
- screening/background checks where lawful and appropriate;
- two-adult/open-door practices;
- no isolated secret spiritual counseling with minors;
- mandatory reporting duties followed;
- parents/guardians informed except where doing so would endanger the child and law/professional safeguarding requires another path.

## 3. Sexual boundaries
Teachers/leaders may not use spiritual authority to obtain sexual access.
Consent compromised by spiritual coercion is not acceptable.

## 4. Financial boundaries
- published budgets;
- dual authorization for significant expenditure;
- no leader personal control of community funds;
- no prosperity claims that pressure vulnerable people;
- no required giving beyond means.

## 5. Medical boundaries
No teacher may order a member to stop medication or reject qualified medical care.

## 6. Mental-health boundary
Visions/voices are not automatically sanctified or pathologized.
If an experience causes danger, severe impairment, sleeplessness, paranoia, command hallucinations, or loss of functioning, qualified clinical evaluation is encouraged immediately.

## 7. Ascetic boundary
No leader may require:
- dangerous fasting;
- sleep deprivation;
- dehydration;
- psychoactive substances;
- isolation;
as tests of loyalty.

## 8. Confession boundaries
Confidential pastoral conversation does not become a tool for blackmail or control.
Legal reporting obligations prevail where applicable.

## 9. Exit rights
No shunning, threats, custody pressure, financial punishment, or claims of supernatural retaliation for leaving.

## 10. Independent complaint channel
Complaints against leaders must reach people who do not report solely to the accused leader.

## 11. Records
Safeguarding incidents, decisions, and conflicts of interest are documented appropriately and securely.

## 12. Founder limitation
The founder is subject to the same rules and may be investigated, disciplined, removed from office, or contradicted.
