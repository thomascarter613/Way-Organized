# Naming Study — Candidate Directions

No final name is selected in WP-008.

## Criteria
A durable name should:
- not falsely claim antiquity;
- not impersonate Judaism, Christianity, or Islam;
- not imply HTERP has proven a disputed theology;
- work for a community, path, and body of practice;
- be pronounceable and nonsectarian enough for public use;
- allow the Abrahamic-prophetic spine to remain visible;
- avoid founder-personality branding.

## Candidate families

### Most-High family
- Way of the Most High
- Covenant of the Most High
- Community of the Most High

Strength: directly echoes *Elyon* / “Most High.”
Risk: may sound like a claim of exclusive divine authorization.

### Truth/Way family
- Way of Truth and Mercy
- Covenant Way
- The Seeking Way
- Way of Truth

Strength: emphasizes epistemology and ethics.
Risk: generic names may already be heavily used.

### Prophetic family
- The Prophetic Way
- Covenant of the Prophets
- Prophetic Commonwealth

Strength: makes the Abrahamic-prophetic center clear.
Risk: may imply all included figures have been proven prophets.

### Source/Unity family
- Way of the Source
- Community of the One
- Covenant of the Source

Strength: broad.
Risk: metaphysical assumptions or New Age associations.

## Current working recommendation
Continue using **the Provisional Way** or **HTERP Constructive Framework** during the pilot.

Select a permanent name only after:
- 30-day pilot feedback;
- legal/domain/name collision research;
- community testing;
- clarity on how strongly the final theology uses “Most High.”
