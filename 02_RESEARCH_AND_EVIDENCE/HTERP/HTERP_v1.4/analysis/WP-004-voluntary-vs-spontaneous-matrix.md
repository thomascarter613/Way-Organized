## Result

The spontaneous/cultivated distinction is causal evidence.

If a state reliably follows a practice, then H1/H2 gain explanatory power through:
- expectation;
- attentional training;
- interoception;
- sensory reduction;
- autonomic change;
- social learning.

This still does not settle metaphysics, because a tradition can claim that practice is a technique for perceiving a real domain. It does, however, mean the burden shifts toward **external discriminating evidence**.
