# Calibration Synthesis — HTERP v0.2

## What we can presently say with confidence

### 1. Human beings really do report encounter-like experiences
This is not in serious doubt. Ancient archives, scriptures, first-person writings, clinical records, and modern experiments document the phenomenon.

### 2. The same broad phenomenological family has multiple demonstrated causes
At minimum:
- normal dreaming;
- pharmacological alteration;
- focal neurological events;
- culturally patterned ritual;
- deliberate fraud;
- sincere spontaneous religious experience.

Therefore no single surface feature can identify cause.

### 3. Historical documentation is often strongest where metaphysical inference is weakest
A tablet can prove a prophetic bureaucracy existed. A brain scan can prove a seizure occurred. Neither establishes a transcendent source.

### 4. The strongest current evidence supports H1 and H2 as real contributors
Neurocognitive mechanisms and cultural shaping are not speculative; they are empirically observable.

### 5. H0 is demonstrably true in a subset
Some extraordinary-contact claims are fraudulent or literarily constructed.

### 6. H3/H4 remain open but underdetermined
Nothing in the calibration corpus currently *requires* an impersonal transcendent reality or personal noncorporeal intelligence to explain the observations. Equally, the current corpus does not logically disprove them.

### 7. H5 is the required research posture
The data already demonstrate multiple causal pathways. Any future global theory must explain heterogeneity rather than flatten it.

## What would count as genuinely stronger evidence for transcendence?

### Personal-intelligence hypothesis H4
Especially valuable:
- information unavailable to the experiencer;
- securely fixed before external verification;
- repeated under blinded conditions;
- source gives coherent responses beyond chance;
- no ordinary sensory/data leakage;
- no adaptive post-hoc interpretation.

### Impersonal-transcendent hypothesis H3
Especially valuable:
- nonordinary states reliably yield independently verifiable information or structure;
- cross-cultural practices converge prospectively on discoveries not culturally available;
- repeatable effects exceed neurocognitive/cultural predictions.

## What not to count as decisive evidence
- sincerity;
- charisma;
- intensity;
- moral beauty;
- antiquity;
- large follower count;
- personal transformation;
- ineffability;
- “felt more real than real”;
- multiple dependent texts repeating one tradition;
- fulfilled predictions recorded only after the event.

## Current direction

Proceed in parallel:

1. **Historical expansion:** complete the global encounter corpus.
2. **Discriminating-evidence search:** anomalous information and prospective predictions.
3. **Mechanism controls:** neuroscience, sleep, psychedelics, meditation, dissociation.
4. **Transmission analysis:** founder → memory → text → institution.
5. **Constructive theology firewall:** no creed until the evidence program reaches synthesis.

## Decision gate for theology

HTERP should not draft a binding theology until:
- Wave 1 and Wave 2 dossiers are substantially complete;
- the dependency graph covers major transmission routes;
- the anomalous-information corpus has been subjected to adversarial review;
- contradiction localization has been performed;
- H0–H5 have been updated against the full evidence.

A provisional spiritual-practice comparison can occur earlier, but it must not be presented as revealed truth.
