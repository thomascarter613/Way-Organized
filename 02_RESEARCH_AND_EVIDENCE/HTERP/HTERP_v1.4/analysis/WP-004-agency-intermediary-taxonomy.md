# WP-004 — Agency / Intermediary Taxonomy

## A0 — No external agent required
Examples: some Buddhist insight/cessation models; some Yoga liberative cognition.

## A1 — Ultimate reality construed as present but not conversational
Examples: some contemplative/unitive traditions.

## A2 — Personal supreme source
God is encountered as present, loving, judging, or revealing.
Examples: Teresa, Sufi God-centered experience.

## A3 — Messenger intermediary
Angel or emissary mediates a higher source.
Examples: prophetic/angelic traditions.

## A4 — Local/ancestral/spirit agents
Agents are plural and embedded in a cosmology.
Examples: many possession/shamanic traditions.

## A5 — Executive agent
Another being is believed to take partial/total control of speech/body.
Examples: mediumistic possession.

## A6 — Symbolic/personified figure
Visionary figure may function as symbolic personification or literary agent.
Examples: some apocalyptic/allegorical visionary texts.

## Research rule
Do not infer that A2–A6 refer to ontologically different beings merely because traditions label them differently.
Do not infer they are the same being merely because their phenomenology overlaps.

Entity identity requires its own evidence:
- self-identification stability;
- information continuity;
- behavioral signature;
- cross-case independence;
- contradiction analysis.
