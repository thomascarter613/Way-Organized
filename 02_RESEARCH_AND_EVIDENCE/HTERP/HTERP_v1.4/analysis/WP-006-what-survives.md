# WP-006 — What Survives Scrutiny?

## Tier A — Robustly established

1. **Human nonordinary experience is real as an experience.**
2. **Such experience is global and historically deep.**
3. **Multiple known mechanisms can generate encounter-like states.**
4. **Culture strongly shapes interpretation and transmission.**
5. **Religious traditions change over time.**
6. **Founder/teacher and later religion must be distinguished.**
7. **Fraud and sincere experience both occur.**
8. **Noetic certainty is not self-authentication.**
9. **Practices can reproducibly alter consciousness/attention.**
10. **No single global theology emerges from the earliest evidence.**
11. **Some traditions preserve source material far better than others for particular questions.**
12. **Anomalous-information claims are empirically testable in principle.**

## Tier B — Recurrent and plausible, but not metaphysically diagnostic

1. Humans often interpret extraordinary experience as contact with agency.
2. Many traditions regard character transformation as more important than intellectual assent alone.
3. Truthfulness, compassion/generosity, restraint, justice, humility, and disciplined practice recur across multiple streams.
4. Specialist mediation and initiation repeatedly emerge.
5. Later institutions often systematize, regulate, or reinterpret charismatic origins.
6. Names and attributes of divine agents can merge historically.
7. Similar practices can independently arise because humans share bodies, cognition, social problems, and environments.

## Tier C — Genuinely open

1. Does an objective transcendent reality exist?
2. Is ultimate reality personal, impersonal, both, or neither?
3. Do noncorporeal intelligences exist?
4. If so, is there one supreme intelligence or many?
5. Are any historical prophetic claims externally sourced?
6. Does consciousness survive bodily death?
7. Is experimental mediumship detecting a replicable anomaly?
8. Can prophecy ever outperform well-designed forecasting prospectively?
9. Are mystical practices only endogenous state technologies or sometimes perceptual technologies?
10. Are El, YHWH, the God addressed by Jesus, and Allah ontologically one entity beyond historical referential continuity?
11. What afterlife model, if any, is true?
12. Does the universe possess objective moral structure?

## Tier D — Not justified

At present HTERP does not justify:
- declaring one scripture wholly inerrant;
- declaring one prophet infallible;
- declaring all religions equally true;
- declaring all religions false;
- declaring all gods/spirits one being;
- declaring all visions hallucinations;
- declaring mediumship proven survival;
- declaring a final prophet from comparative evidence;
- constructing a coercive legal order from uncertain revelation;
- hiding essential truth in a secret elite doctrine.
