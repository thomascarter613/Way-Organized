# WP-003 Concept Genealogy

## Ancient Near Eastern information ecology
omen/sign systems → extispicy/astrology → dream interpretation → temple/court oracles → ecstatic/messenger prophecy

## West-Semitic/Israelite development
El ancestral/high-god traditions + local divine-council religion
→ Yahwistic southern/wilderness traditions
→ El–YHWH identification/consolidation
→ national Yahwism with prophets, temple, monarchy
→ prophetic critique and exclusive loyalty
→ increasingly explicit monotheism

## Indian development sampled by HTERP
Vedic ritual/sacred speech
→ Upanishadic internalized metaphysical knowledge
→ śramaṇa liberation movements (Buddhist/Jain)
→ awakening/omniscient liberation models not dependent on creator revelation

## Chinese development sampled by HTERP
Shang royal ancestor/divination system
→ Zhou moralized Heaven/Mandate concepts
→ classical philosophical Dao discourse
→ second-century organized Daoist revelation
→ later Shangqing revelatory/visualization corpora

## Comparative result
Several civilizations show movement from **ritual information management** toward more universal moral, metaphysical, or salvific frameworks. The sequences differ substantially and should not be forced into one “progress” narrative.
