# WP-005 — Joseph Smith First-Vision Version Analysis

## Documentary sequence
Joseph Smith Papers identifies four firsthand/Smith-directed account groups from his lifetime:
- ca. 1832;
- 1835;
- 1838;
- 1842.

It also preserves multiple accounts by contemporaries who heard Smith recount the experience.

## Analytical method
For each version code:
- date;
- author/scribe;
- audience;
- stated purpose;
- age/date assigned to event;
- initiating concern;
- number/identity of beings described;
- message content;
- adversarial/supernatural elements;
- institutional implications;
- wording shared with other accounts.

## Interpretation rules

### Variation does not equal contradiction
Memory and audience can alter emphasis.

### Harmonization is not automatic
Later detail cannot be projected backward unless the earlier account supports it.

### Earliest version has special but not absolute weight
It is closest among surviving firsthand records, but still roughly a decade after the claimed event.

### Canonization changes salience
The 1838 account became the dominant institutional form. That makes it important for reception history, not automatically more historically accurate.

## HTERP use
This case will serve as the template for **founder-narrative version growth** in all modern revelatory movements.
