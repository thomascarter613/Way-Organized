# Prior Program Baseline — PRC, RJC, and EFC

**Provenance:** reconstructed from the active ChatGPT conversation state on 2026-09-21.

## Method

The project separates historical attestation, phenomenology, founder attribution, textual stability, genealogical dependence/contact, and metaphysical interpretation. No composite truth score is used.

## PRC — Prophetic / Revelatory Corpus

Comparative cases include Mari, Abraham, Melchizedek, Moses, Balaam, Amos, Isaiah, Zarathustra, Rigvedic rishis, Buddha, Jesus, Muhammad/Qur'an, Guru Nanak, and later controls.

## RJC — Recovered Jesus Corpus

Purpose: recover historically strong Jesus material before later doctrinal synthesis.

Core profile:
- Kingdom of God central;
- Jewish/Torah context;
- repentance and Israel renewal;
- parables;
- healing/exorcistic activity historically probable;
- Twelve/restoration symbolism;
- prayer, enemy-love, generosity, non-retaliation;
- Temple action;
- crucifixion under Pilate.

Christological rule: Jesus' own likely self-understanding must remain separate from early post-Easter exaltation, Gospel framing, and later conciliar metaphysics.

## EFC — Eschatological Figure Corpus

Tracks later expected/returning/restorative figures: Jewish Messiah, Elijah, Messiah ben Joseph, returning Jesus, Antichrist, Mahdi, Twelfth Imam, returning Isa, Dajjal, Saošyant, Maitreya, Kalki.

## Governing caution

Similarity never by itself establishes common source, same entity, same future person, or independent corroboration. Common ancestry and contact must be subtracted before convergence is treated as evidence.
