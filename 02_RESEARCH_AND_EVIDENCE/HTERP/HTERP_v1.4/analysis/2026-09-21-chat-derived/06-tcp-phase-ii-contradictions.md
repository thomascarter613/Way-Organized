## Conclusion

The naive thesis that all major religions teach the same doctrine in different words fails. The traditions disagree about what exists.

Five live explanatory models remain:
H1 strict single-source; H2 progressive revelation; H3 experiential pluralism; H4 multiple transcendent agents; H5 hybrid.

A narrower invariant survives: ordinary ego-driven perception is inadequate, humans become misaligned through ignorance/falsehood/craving/injustice/pride, and transformation toward deeper reality/order is needed.
