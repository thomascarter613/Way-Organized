## HM-001 — Constrained Hybrid Model

Investigate separately:
1. extraordinary-experience evidence;
2. transmission reliability;
3. teaching reliability;
4. independence;
5. anomalous information/insight;
6. moral/cognitive coherence;
7. predictive/explanatory power;
8. ordinary psychological explanation;
9. institutional alteration incentives;
10. contradictions with stronger evidence.

## Revelation pipeline

Ultimate/transcendent reality → causal event → phenomenological experience → human interpretation → expression → transmission → systematization → institution.

A contradiction can enter at different layers.

A genuine experience does not guarantee correct causal interpretation. Historical authenticity does not guarantee metaphysical truth. A true proposition does not validate an entire religion as an indivisible system.

## Conclusion

Hard ontological contradictions require at least one of:
one proposition false; one provisional; one metaphorical; human mediation introduced error; or different sources were involved.

Different words alone do not solve the problem.
