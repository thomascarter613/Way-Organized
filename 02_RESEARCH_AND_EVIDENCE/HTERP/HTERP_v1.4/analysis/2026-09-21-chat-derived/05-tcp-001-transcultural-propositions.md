# TCP-001 — Transcultural Proposition Corpus

## Rule

Recurrence matters only after common ancestry, contact, translation artifacts, and later borrowing are accounted for.

Codes: GI genealogical inheritance; CT contact-entangled; RI relatively independent; IN internal recurrence; NE not equivalent.

## Findings

1. Objective truth/order beyond preference — strong abstract candidate.
2. Falsehood/ignorance creates misalignment — very strong.
3. Human transformation required, not mere assent — very strong.
4. Ritual alone insufficient — strong.
5. Justice/treatment of vulnerable people spiritually significant — strong.
6. Self-centered attachment distorts perception/conduct — strong at ethical/psychological level, not universal no-self metaphysics.
7. One personal Creator — partial convergence only; not universal.
8. Exceptional access to transcendent/deeper reality — historically very strong as a human claim; mechanisms differ.
9. Moral consequences beyond immediate social enforcement — strong.
10. Continued existence beyond death — strong recurrence, divergent mechanisms.
11. General resurrection — important but concentrated in a contact-entangled West Asian/Iranian/Abrahamic complex.
12. Rebirth — regional/genealogical, especially South Asian.
13. Final cosmic restoration — partial; linear/cyclic divergence.
14. Truth declines/corrupts — historically widespread but low evidential value for transcendence.
15. Restorer after decline — strong structural recurrence, weak individual-identity evidence.

## Deep invariant

Reality is not whatever humans wish; ordinary cognition/desire are unreliable; falsehood, ignorance, injustice, craving, pride, or self-centeredness produce misalignment; humans can gain better access to truth; transformation and ethical conduct matter; ritual alone is insufficient; choices have deeper consequences; traditions often expect renewal after decline.

## Two explanatory families

Relational: personal transcendent source → revelation → sin/falsehood → repentance/obedience → restored relationship.

Insight: objective structure → ignorance → craving/misperception → insight/discipline → liberation.

Historical comparison cannot decide automatically whether these are different realities or different modes of one deeper reality.
