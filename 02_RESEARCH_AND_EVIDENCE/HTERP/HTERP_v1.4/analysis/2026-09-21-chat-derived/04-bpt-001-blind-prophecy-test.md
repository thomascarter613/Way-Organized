# BPT-001 — Blind Prophecy Test Corpus

## Purpose

Freeze predictions at their earliest defensible pre-claimant form, derive recognition criteria before matching claimants, and prevent retrospective cherry-picking.

## Result codes

L literal correspondence; S structural; R reinterpretive; C contradiction; U unresolved/future.

## Frozen cards

### Davidic Promise

Core: Davidic house/dynasty, royal authority, divinely sanctioned rule, enduring kingdom.

Not automatically included: virgin birth, resurrection, return from heaven, incarnation, universal judgment, two-stage mission.

### Daniel 7

Humanlike figure; heaven/cloud imagery; approaches Ancient of Days; receives authority; universal/everlasting dominion; linked to vindication of holy people.

The text itself does not name Jesus or predict crucifixion/resurrection.

### Early Mahdi Core

Crisis/injustice → restorer associated with Muhammad's house → restoration of justice/order.

Low identification power.

### Classical Sunni Mahdi Profile

Male; Muhammad's family; some Fatimid strands; end-time injustice; justice/equity; some seven-year traditions.

Moderate role specificity, not unique personal identification.

### Pre-Twelver Qa'im

House of Muhammad/Ali; wronged authority; hidden/absent restorer possible; rising/vindication; truth/rule.

The exact mature Twelver sequence cannot be treated with equal confidence as an independently frozen pre-874 prediction.

### Maitreya

Gautama's dispensation declines/disappears → future Metteyya → full awakening → Dharma teaching → flourishing community.

Distinctive role; weak near-term identity data.

### Gathic Saošyant

Earliest benefactor/helper concept, including plural future benefactors; not yet one detailed final savior.

### Younger-Avestan Saošyant

Final benefactor → defeat of evil → resurrection → renovation.

### Kalki

Extreme Kali Yuga degeneration → Kalki → mature Visnu-avatar identity → defeats corrupt order → restores dharma → renewed age.

### He Whom God Shall Make Manifest

Future Babi Manifestation with superseding/consummating revelatory authority. Strong role specificity, low personal specificity, chronologically flexible.

## Negative controls

A righteous leader restores justice during corruption.
A great teacher restores lost truth.

Both are too generic to discriminate among claimants.

## Main result

No tested case yet yields:
highly specific prior prediction + unique uncontrollable identifiers + one plausible candidate + literal fulfillment + no major disconfirmation + minimal reinterpretation.

This does not prove religious claims false. It limits what prophecy alone currently demonstrates historically.
