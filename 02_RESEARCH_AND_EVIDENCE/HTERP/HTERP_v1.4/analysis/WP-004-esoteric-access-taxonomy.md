# WP-004 — Esoteric Access Taxonomy

The word “esoteric” conflates several very different restrictions.

## E0 — Fully public
Teaching/practice available without special qualification.

## E1 — Prerequisite depth
Advanced material is public in principle but requires prior study/practice.

## E2 — Safety restriction
Practice restricted because proponents believe unprepared use can cause psychological, ritual, or spiritual harm.

## E3 — Initiatory authorization
Access follows formal initiation or lineage transmission.

## E4 — Role restriction
Knowledge belongs to priests, diviners, healers, monastics, elders, or another authorized role.

## E5 — Community-sovereignty restriction
Knowledge is restricted because it belongs to a people/community and is not available for extraction by outsiders.

## E6 — Strategic secrecy
Information concealed to preserve power, prestige, competitive advantage, or institutional authority.

## E7 — Claimed salvific secret
A group claims essential saving truth is reserved for insiders.

### HTERP ethical rule
E5 material is not “missing evidence” that researchers are entitled to acquire. Public evidence is analyzed; community restrictions are respected.

### Theological safeguard
No teaching essential to a future HTERP-derived public path should depend on unverifiable secret claims. Advanced practice can have prerequisites without creating a hidden doctrine that contradicts the public core.
