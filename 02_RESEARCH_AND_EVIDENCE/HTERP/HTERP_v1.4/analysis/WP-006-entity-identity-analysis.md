# WP-006 — Entity Identity Analysis

## Problem

Religious reports repeatedly name or identify apparent agents:
El, YHWH, Ahura Mazda, angels, Christ, Mary, spirits, ancestors, deities, deceased persons, channeled entities, and others.

A shared label does not prove one entity.
Different labels do not prove different entities.

## Same-Entity Test

### EID-1 — Historical continuity
Is there a demonstrable chain connecting the names/cults?

### EID-2 — Self-identification
Does the apparent agent consistently identify itself?

### EID-3 — Attribute continuity
Are core attributes stable rather than merely generic?

### EID-4 — Message continuity
Are teachings compatible at a specificity greater than common moral generalities?

### EID-5 — Information continuity
Does the entity demonstrate stable access to information across independent encounters?

### EID-6 — Behavioral signature
Does interaction style remain distinctive across independent experiencers?

### EID-7 — Independence
Can similarity be explained by scripture, cultural exposure, expectation, or direct transmission?

### EID-8 — Contradiction burden
Does the same alleged entity issue materially incompatible claims?

### EID-9 — Adversarial verification
Can the claimed entity perform under blinded conditions that exclude cueing/leakage?

## Application: El / YHWH
Historical evidence supports a process in which older El traditions and Yahwistic traditions became identified/integrated in Israelite religion.

That historical statement does **not** establish either:
- two ontologically real beings that were mistakenly merged; or
- one real being known by two names from the beginning.

The ontology remains open.

## Application: “same God” across Abrahamic traditions
Judaism, Christianity, and Islam share historical genealogies and explicitly refer to the God of Abraham/Moses in overlapping traditions. That establishes referential intention and historical continuity far better than it establishes agreement about every divine attribute.

## Application: global entity identity
No global case currently meets EID-1 through EID-9 strongly enough to identify all claimed supreme beings/spirits as one intelligence.

## Constructive consequence
The provisional project may use a deliberately nonexclusive term such as:

**Ultimate Reality / the Most High / the Source**

while treating specific named-agent identities as theological hypotheses rather than settled ontology.
