# WP-005 — Apparition Witness Topology

## Witness classes

**W0 — experiencer only:** person reports seeing/hearing entity.

**W1 — behavior witness:** observer sees experiencer react but not entity.

**W2 — shared entity witness:** multiple persons independently report substantially overlapping entity perception.

**W3 — secondary physical-phenomenon witness:** observers report light, sun, smell, sound, movement, etc., but not the entity.

**W4 — instrument witness:** photograph/sensor/recording captures independently analyzable event.

**W5 — information witness:** observer can verify specific content supplied during event.

## Lourdes
Primarily W0 + W1. Bernadette reported the figure; companions did not see the apparition itself.

## Fátima
Child apparition claims: W0/W2 among the three visionaries depending on episode/content.
October crowd: primarily W3 reports concerning the sun/sky, with substantial heterogeneity.

## Rule
Never convert W3 into W2 in retelling.
“70,000 witnesses” does not mean 70,000 people saw Mary.
