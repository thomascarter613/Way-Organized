# WP-002 Dependency and Independence Analysis

## Three forms of independence
1. **Historical-contact independence:** could A have borrowed from B?
2. **Problem independence:** are both responding to universal problems such as death, illness, weather, war, uncertainty?
3. **Mechanism independence:** could one common human mechanism generate both phenomena?

A convergence is strong only when it survives all three screens.

## Major pairings
- **Shang ↔ Mari:** historical borrowing less likely; problem-dependent because both manage uncertainty.
- **Rigveda ↔ Gathas:** substantially inherited Indo-Iranian background; not independent.
- **Hebrew prophets ↔ ancient Near East:** culturally connected zone; messenger-prophecy parallels are not separate supernatural confirmations.
- **Hebrew prophets ↔ Zarathustra:** later contact/influence remains chronology-specific and debated.
- **Jesus ↔ Hebrew prophets:** direct cultural/scriptural dependence.
- **Paul ↔ Jesus/Jewish apocalypticism:** direct/historical dependence in interpretation.
- **Muhammad ↔ Jewish/Christian/Arabian late antiquity:** historically connected religious environment.
- **Buddha ↔ Vedic/śramaṇa India:** common Indian environment, but awakening is structurally distinct from Vedic messenger revelation.
- **Guru Nanak ↔ South Asian/Islamic world:** historical contact unsurprising; originality must be evaluated at synthesis/content level.
- **Ifá ↔ ancient Mediterranean prophecy:** no simple direct dependence established; generic divination still answers universal uncertainty.
- **Maya ↔ Old World divination:** strong historical independence pre-Columbian, but generic divination remains low-specificity convergence.
- **Aboriginal songline systems ↔ prophetic systems:** historically independent but often not the same event category.

## Rule
**Independent category mismatch is not convergence.** Two traditions being sacred/religious does not make them two instances of the same encounter type.
