# WP-005 — Modern Evidence Ladder

## M0 — Retrospective anecdote
No contemporaneous record; flexible retelling.

## M1 — Contemporaneous self-report
Claim written/recorded by experiencer near event.

## M2 — Independent contemporaneous witness
Witness account fixed near event, but no blinding/control.

## M3 — Multiple contemporaneous witnesses with mapped sightlines/information access
Useful especially for physical/apparitional claims.

## M4 — Prospective timestamp
Claim fixed before outcome.

## M5 — Blinded controlled information test
Cueing/leakage reduced; target and scoring controlled.

## M6 — Preregistered adversarial experiment
Protocol fixed publicly; proponent and skeptic agree on analysis.

## M7 — Independent multisite replication
Distinct teams reproduce effect with full data.

### Rule
Historical significance and metaphysical evidential strength are separate.
A world-changing M1 claim can have lower causal-discrimination strength than a small M6 experiment.
