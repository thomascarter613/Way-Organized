# WP-006 — Constructive Theology Readiness Gate

## Gate result
**Ready for provisional construction; not ready for binding creed.**

## Permitted in WP-007

### 1. Epistemological constitution
Rules about evidence, uncertainty, interpretation, and revision.

### 2. Layered authority
For example:
- observed/recoverable evidence;
- strong inference;
- inherited wisdom;
- optional speculation.

### 3. Provisional God-language
A neutral placeholder such as **Ultimate Reality**, **the Most High**, or **the Source**, while named identities remain open hypotheses.

### 4. Ethical/practice framework
Practices justified by evidence, human flourishing, and cross-tradition convergence may be tested without declaring their metaphysical explanations certain.

### 5. Public/advanced-practice distinction
Advanced practices may require prerequisites, supervision, or community authority, but essential ethical/salvific claims may not be hidden.

### 6. Open canon/source library
Texts may be graded by provenance and usefulness rather than declared uniformly inerrant.

## Not yet permitted as research conclusions

- final identity of God;
- Trinity / non-Trinity as established by HTERP;
- finality of Muhammad;
- divinity/nondivinity of Jesus as established by HTERP;
- reincarnation versus resurrection decision;
- objective existence of angels/jinn/spirits;
- exclusive salvific path;
- infallible founder;
- closed canon.

These can be represented in WP-007 as **branches or hypotheses**, not settled conclusions.

## Constitutional principle

> **Where evidence is uncertain, doctrine must not pretend certainty.**

This principle becomes the bridge from HTERP research to any future religious framework.
