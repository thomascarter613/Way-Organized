# HTERP Hypothesis Ledger — v0.5 After WP-004

## H0 — Construction / literary development / fraud
**Established partial cause.**
WP-004 adds literary visionary genres and hagiographical growth as major filters. The existence of a text about ascent, miracle, or saintly knowledge is not proof of an event in the form narrated.

## H1 — Cognitive / neurological generation or mediation
**Strongly established partial cause.**
WP-004 strengthens H1 because many states are deliberately cultivated using procedures known to alter attention, arousal, self-processing, expectation, and perception.

## H2 — Cultural / social shaping
**Pervasively established.**
Agent identity, cosmology, permissible interpretations, training methods, and even what counts as a successful experience are socially learned.

## H3 — Transcendent impersonal reality
**Open.**
Yoga, Buddhist, and some unitive/apophatic material remain compatible with access to a nonpersonal ultimate structure, but no WP-004 case independently requires this explanation.

## H4 — Transcendent personal intelligence/intelligences
**Open.**
Prophecy, possession, ascent, Teresa, and shamanic traditions remain compatible with real agency, but natural controls reproduce many agentive features.

## H5 — Mixed model
**Operationally required.**
The category now includes intentionally cultivated states, spontaneous states, literary visionary forms, social-role possession, and claimed agent contact.

## H6 — Category conflation
**Further strengthened.**
“Mysticism” joins “revelation” as an analytically dangerous umbrella. Buddhist absorption, Teresian prayer, Hekhalot ascent, and possession should not be treated as one event merely because all are nonordinary.

## H7 — Progressive integration
**Still viable historical model.**
Scriptural traditions repeatedly reinterpret earlier experiences into later contemplative systems: Ezekiel → Hekhalot; Qur'anic piety → Sufi disciplines; early Buddhist teaching → diverse meditation systems.

## H8 — Practice-mediated access hypothesis
Some traditions may have discovered practices that reliably generate access to:
- unusual but endogenous human states (H1), or
- genuine aspects of transcendent reality (H3/H4).

### Status
**New, underdetermined.**
Practice reproducibility alone supports H1. H3/H4 require discriminating information/effects beyond phenomenology.

## Current strongest result
There is robust evidence for a **human capacity-space of nonordinary experience** that can be reached through multiple pathways and interpreted through multiple ontologies.

Whether that capacity-space ever functions as perception of an external transcendent domain remains the central unresolved question.
