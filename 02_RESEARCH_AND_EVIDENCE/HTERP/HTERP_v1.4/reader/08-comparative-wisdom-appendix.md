# Reader VIII — Comparative Wisdom / Contemplative Appendix

This section remains open and curated.

## Candidate Jewish
- selected Psalms;
- Job;
- Ecclesiastes;
- Proverbs;
- later Jewish contemplative material with provenance notes.

## Candidate Christian
- Didache;
- selected early Christian ethics;
- Desert tradition;
- Teresa of Ávila;
- apophatic theology.

## Candidate Islamic
- early ascetic material;
- Hasan al-Basri traditions where source confidence permits;
- selected Sufi texts;
- ethical/adab literature.

## Candidate South Asian
- selected Upanishadic passages;
- classical Yoga;
- Buddhist meditation texts.

## Candidate global
Tradition-specific public material may be included where:
- community context is respected;
- restricted knowledge is not extracted;
- provenance is clear;
- inclusion serves genuine comparison rather than decorative universalism.

## Admission criteria
A text enters the appendix when it has:
1. identifiable provenance;
2. substantial ethical/contemplative value;
3. enough context to avoid distortion;
4. relevance to a live HTERP question;
5. no need to pretend it agrees with the Abrahamic spine.
