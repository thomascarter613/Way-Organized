# Qur'an 96 — al-ʿAlaq

## T
Corpus Coranicum places the sura in an early Meccan group.

The Islamic tradition that 96:1–5 were literally Muhammad's first revealed verses is ancient and important, but current Corpus Coranicum commentary cautions that this specific biographical placement is probably a later prophetological construction rather than something established directly by the sura.

It also argues that verses 6–19 need not be treated as a later addition simply to preserve the “first revelation” chronology.

## R
Themes:
- recitation/reading in the Lord's name;
- creation of the human;
- teaching/knowledge;
- human transgression/self-sufficiency;
- opposition to prayer;
- prostration/nearness.

## A
The opening imperative need not be a transcript of one datable cave scene; it can function within proclamation to a wider audience.

## C
Compare:
- later sira first-revelation narrative;
- biblical prophetic-call motifs;
- early Meccan warning discourse.

## E
Strong:
An early Qur'anic proclamation centered on Lord, creation, knowledge, dependence, and worship.

Open:
whether it records the exact first moment of Muhammad's revelatory career.

## Constructive status
**Core source; do not use later biography to circularly interpret every detail.**
