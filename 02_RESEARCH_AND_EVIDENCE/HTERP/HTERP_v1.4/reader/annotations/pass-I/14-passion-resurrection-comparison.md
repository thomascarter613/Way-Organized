# Passion and Resurrection Witness Comparison

## T
The crucifixion of Jesus under Roman authority is among the strongest historical claims about his life.

Resurrection is evidenced historically first as:
- very early belief;
- appearance claims;
- transformed community proclamation.

Paul's 1 Corinthians 15 tradition predates the written Gospels.

The Gospel narratives provide later, richer narrative forms.

## R
Separate questions:
1. Was Jesus crucified? — very strong.
2. Was he buried? — historically plausible, details debated.
3. Did followers claim appearances? — very strong.
4. What did they experience? — varies by source.
5. Was the tomb empty? — historically debated.
6. Did bodily resurrection objectively occur? — metaphysical/historical inference beyond direct verification.

## Textual issue
The earliest recoverable ending of Mark likely ends at 16:8; later longer endings are secondary.
