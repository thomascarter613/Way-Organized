# Isaiah 6 — Throne Vision and Commission

## T
Isaiah 6 belongs to the Isaianic corpus whose final form developed over centuries. It plausibly preserves an early prophetic call/commission tradition associated with Isaiah, but the literary form remains crafted.

## R
Features:
- throne/temple vision;
- seraphim;
- holiness acclamation;
- perceived impurity;
- purification;
- commission;
- difficult hardening/judgment message.

## A
Possible approaches:
- autobiographical visionary report;
- liturgical/theological literary construction;
- an experience later shaped into temple symbolism;
- combination.

## C
Compare:
- Ezekiel 1;
- later Hekhalot/Merkavah;
- Revelation 4;
- neurological/mystical visual-control cases.

## E
Strong:
A foundational Israelite visionary-commission text.

Open:
Exact phenomenology and external ontology.

## Constructive status
**Retain for humility, vocation, and holiness; treat hardening/judgment component as difficult text requiring ethical/theological analysis.**
