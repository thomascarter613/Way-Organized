# Deuteronomy 32:8–9 — Divine Council Text

## T
This passage has an important textual variant.

The Masoretic tradition reads approximately “sons of Israel,” while Dead Sea Scroll and ancient Greek evidence supports an older reading involving “sons of God/divine beings.”

Many textual critics regard the divine-being reading as earlier.

## R
On that reading:
- Elyon apportions nations;
- divine beings/sons of God correspond to nations;
- YHWH's portion is Jacob/Israel.

## A
Two major theological reconstructions:
1. Elyon/El and YHWH are distinguishable in an older layer;
2. Elyon is already a title of YHWH, who assigns subordinate beings to nations.

The textual variant alone does not settle the identity question.

## C
Compare:
- Ugaritic divine-council patterns;
- Psalm 82;
- Genesis 14 El Elyon;
- later monotheistic reinterpretation.

## E
Strong:
The older text probably reflects divine-council conceptuality.

Open:
Whether it preserves an early stage in which YHWH was ontologically subordinate/distinct from El/Elyon.

## Constructive status
**Retain as required difficult evidence against simplistic claims that every biblical layer expresses identical later monotheism.**
