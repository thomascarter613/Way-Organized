# Selected Kingdom Parables — Mark 4 and Parallels

## T
Parables are among the characteristic forms of the Jesus tradition, but individual versions differ across Gospels.

## R
Recurring themes:
- hidden growth;
- disproportionate outcome;
- judgment/discernment;
- reversal;
- invitation;
- response to God's reign.

## A
Do not assume every later allegorical detail came from Jesus.
Early church interpretation sometimes extends or reframes parables.

## C
Compare parallel versions before harmonization.

## E
Strong:
Parabolic proclamation of God's reign belongs near the center of the Jesus tradition.

## Constructive status
**Retain as central teaching form.**
Interpret the kingdom ethically and eschatologically without prematurely settling its metaphysics.
