# Qur'an 1 — al-Fātiḥa

## T
The Fātiḥa functions as the canonical opening and central liturgical prayer of the Qur'an. Canonical position is not the same as chronological first proclamation.

## R
Themes:
- praise;
- lordship;
- mercy;
- judgment;
- exclusive worship/dependence;
- guidance/path.

## A
It may be read simultaneously as:
- liturgical prayer;
- summary theology;
- orientation of the reciter.

## C
Compare:
- Psalms;
- Jewish/Christian prayer forms;
- Lord's Prayer at structural rather than dependence-by-assumption level.

## E
Strong:
A compact early Islamic theology of worship and guidance.

## Constructive status
**Core devotional source in the Abrahamic spine**, while preserving its Islamic meaning.
