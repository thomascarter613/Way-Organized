# Qur'an 19 — Mary and Jesus

## T
Sura Maryam is generally situated within the Meccan Qur'anic proclamation in common relative chronologies, though detailed dating models differ.

## R
The sura presents:
- Zechariah/John;
- Mary;
- miraculous conception/birth of Jesus;
- Jesus speaking as servant of God and prophet;
- Abraham;
- Moses and other figures;
- strong divine mercy and eschatological themes.

## A
It both shares with and significantly reinterprets Christian traditions.
Do not treat similarity as proof of either direct textual copying or independent revelation without specific evidence.

## C
Compare:
- Luke 1–2;
- noncanonical late-antique Christian Mary/infancy traditions;
- Qur'an 3 and 5;
- later Islamic Christology.

## E
Strong:
Early Islamic reception/reframing of Jesus and Mary.

Not independent:
historical evidence for first-century Jesus beyond what earlier sources provide.

## Constructive status
**Required Level 1 text because it places the Jesus question inside the Abrahamic spine's internal disagreement.**
