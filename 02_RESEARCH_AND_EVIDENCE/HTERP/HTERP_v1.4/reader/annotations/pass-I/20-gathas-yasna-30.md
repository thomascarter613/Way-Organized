# Gathas — Yasna 30

## T
Yasna 30 belongs to the Old Avestan Gathic corpus. Translation is difficult because Old Avestan is linguistically remote and later Middle Persian interpretations do not simply reproduce the original.

## R
The passage is central to discussions of:
- truth/order (`aša`);
- deceit/lie (`druj`);
- moral choice;
- spirit/mentality contrasts;
- consequences of alignment.

## A
Do not automatically import the fully developed later Zoroastrian cosmic dualism into every Gathic term.

Iranica emphasizes that the first four Gathas overwhelmingly address Ahura Mazda and combine mystical invocation with highly formal poetry.

## C
Compare:
- Vedic `ṛta`/order vocabulary historically through Indo-Iranian inheritance;
- biblical truth/righteousness;
- later Zoroastrian dualism.

## E
Strong:
Founder-proximate or very early Iranian ethical-religious poetry structured around truth and falsehood.

Open:
exact metaphysics of the “two spirits” and relation to later dualism.

## Constructive status
**Level 2 high-value prophetic witness.**
