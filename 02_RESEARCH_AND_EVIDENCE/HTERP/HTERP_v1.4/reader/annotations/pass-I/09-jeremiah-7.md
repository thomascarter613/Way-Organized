# Jeremiah 7 — Temple Sermon

## T
Jeremiah's book has substantial literary and textual complexity, including significant differences between Masoretic and Greek forms.

## R
The sermon attacks confidence that possession of the temple guarantees safety while injustice, violence, and idolatry persist.

## A
The text can be read as:
- prophetic anti-corruption preaching;
- Deuteronomistic shaping of Jeremiah memory;
- both.

## C
Compare:
- Amos 5;
- Isaiah 1;
- Jesus' temple critique;
- institutional religion versus ethical conduct across traditions.

## E
Strong:
The Jeremiah tradition subordinates sacred institution to ethical fidelity.

**Core anti-institutional-idolatry text.**
