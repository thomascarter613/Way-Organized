# Amos 5 — Justice and Cult

## T
Amos contains eighth-century BCE prophetic material within a later literary book. Exact unit histories remain debated.

## R
Amos 5 attacks a community that maintains religious festivals/sacrifices while practicing injustice.

## A
This is not necessarily a rejection of ritual as such.
A strong reading is rejection of cult divorced from justice.

## C
Compare:
- Isaiah 1;
- Jeremiah 7;
- Micah 6;
- Jesus' prophetic critiques;
- Qur'anic criticism of hypocritical religiosity.

## E
Strong:
Moral critique is central to the Amos tradition.

Not established:
That every line is verbatim speech of the historical Amos.

## Constructive status
**Core ethical-prophetic text.**
Principle:
ritual cannot compensate for exploitation.
