# Matthew 5–7 — Sermon on the Mount

## T
The Sermon is a Matthean literary composition containing sayings material with different source histories. It should not be treated as a stenographic transcript of one historical sermon.

## R
Themes:
- beatitudes;
- Torah and righteousness;
- anger/reconciliation;
- sexuality/integrity;
- oath/speech;
- retaliation;
- enemy-love;
- almsgiving/prayer/fasting;
- wealth/anxiety;
- judgment;
- discernment;
- doing rather than merely professing.

## A
Historical-Jesus analysis must proceed saying by saying. Shared Matthean/Lukan material and Markan parallels can matter, but hypothetical-source reconstructions do not equal surviving documents.

## C
Compare:
- Torah;
- Prophets;
- Luke's sermon material;
- rabbinic/Jewish ethical context;
- Qur'anic ethical teaching.

## E
Strong:
The Sermon is an early and extremely influential witness to Jesus-centered ethical tradition.

Open:
exact original setting and wording of every unit.

## Constructive status
**Primary ethical reading corpus**, subject to passage-level source analysis.
