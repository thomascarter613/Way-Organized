# Ezekiel 1–3 — Vision and Commission

## T
Ezekiel is a highly literary prophetic work situated in the Babylonian exile context. Its opening vision is one of the most elaborate encounter narratives in the Hebrew Bible.

## R
Reported features:
- storm/fire/luminosity;
- four living beings;
- wheels;
- throne;
- humanlike glory;
- collapse;
- voice;
- scroll ingestion;
- commission.

## A
Possible explanatory layers:
- visionary experience;
- temple/divine-throne symbolism;
- Babylonian visual environment;
- literary composition;
- neurocognitive analogues.

No historical diagnosis is justified.

## C
Compare:
- Isaiah 6;
- Mesopotamian throne/iconographic environments;
- Revelation;
- Hekhalot;
- visionary controls.

## E
Strong:
Detailed literary representation of visionary prophecy.

Weak:
Independent evidence that the described throne apparatus existed externally.

## Constructive status
**Retain as visionary witness, not cosmographic map.**
