# Reader IV — Qur'anic / Muhammad Witness Corpus

## Constructive role
Level 1 spine.

## Critical distinction
The Qur'an and later sira/hadith answer different historical questions.

## Qur'an
Treat as the earliest major textual witness to the proclamation of the Islamic movement.

Reader tracks:
- broad Meccan/Medinan context models where relevant;
- manuscript/textual history;
- intra-Qur'anic themes;
- relationship to Jewish, Christian, Arabian, and late-antique discourse.

## Muhammad biography layer

### Qur'an
Minimal biography, high source proximity.

### Early sira reconstruction
Use earliest recoverable strata and transmission analysis.

### Hadith
Grade by:
- collection date;
- isnad analysis;
- matn/content;
- earlier parallels;
- legal/theological incentives.

No hadith is automatically equated with Qur'anic source proximity.

## Key Arabic terms
- **Allāh** — God
- **rabb** — lord/sustainer
- **al-Raḥmān** — the Merciful/Compassionate
- **waḥy** — revelation
- **nabī** — prophet
- **rasūl** — messenger
- **īmān** — faith/trust
- **islām** — submission
- **taqwā** — God-consciousness/piety
- **raḥma** — mercy
- **ʿadl / qisṭ** — justice/equity

## Difficult-text rule
War, punishment, gender/family law, legal sanctions, and exclusivist passages are included and contextually studied, not hidden.
