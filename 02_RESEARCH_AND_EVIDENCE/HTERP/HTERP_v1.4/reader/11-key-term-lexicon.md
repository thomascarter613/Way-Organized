# Key-Term Lexicon — Starter

This is a translation-warning list, not a dictionary.

## Hebrew
- El — god/God; also historical divine name/title
- Elohim — deity/God/divine beings depending context
- YHWH — Israelite divine name
- Elyon — Most High
- Shaddai — divine epithet of uncertain original meaning
- berit — covenant
- hesed — covenantal loyalty/kindness; context-sensitive
- mishpat — judgment/justice
- tsedaqah — righteousness/justice
- ruach — wind/breath/spirit

## Greek
- theos — god/God
- pneuma — wind/breath/spirit
- basileia — kingdom/reign
- metanoia — change of mind/repentance
- dikaiosyne — righteousness/justice
- agape — love
- eleos — mercy

## Arabic
- Allah — God
- rabb — lord/sustainer
- rahma — mercy
- wahy — revelation
- nabi — prophet
- rasul — messenger
- iman — faith/trust
- islam — submission
- taqwa — piety/God-consciousness
- din — religion/judgment/way, context-dependent
- adl/qist — justice/equity

## Old Avestan
- Ahura Mazda — Wise Lord
- asha — truth/right order
- druj — lie/deceit
- vohu manah — good mind

## Punjabi/Gurmukhi conceptual terms
- Ik Oankar
- Naam
- hukam
- seva
- simran
- haumai

## Pali/Sanskrit Buddhist
- dukkha
- anicca/anitya
- anatta/anatman
- dhamma/dharma
- nibbana/nirvana
- sati/smrti
- samadhi
- metta/maitri
- karuna

## Rule
Do not build theology on an English gloss without checking the source-language semantic range.
