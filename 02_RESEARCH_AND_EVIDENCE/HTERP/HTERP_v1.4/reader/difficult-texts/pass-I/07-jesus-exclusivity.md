# Jesus Tradition — Exclusivity Claims

## Source cluster
Especially Johannine statements such as John 14:6, alongside Synoptic warnings and early-Christian mission claims.

## Source-critical point
John is a distinctive theological/literary Gospel. A saying in John cannot automatically be treated as equally source-proximate to the historical Jesus as Synoptic material merely because both are canonical.

## Strongest traditional reading
Jesus is the unique and exclusive mediator of salvation.

## Alternative readings
- access to God is through the way embodied by Jesus rather than institutional membership;
- Johannine community identity language;
- unique revelation without universal condemnation of the unevangelized;
- later Christological theology.

## Ethical problem
Exclusivity claims can become:
- coercion;
- contempt for outsiders;
- denial that truth exists elsewhere.

## Constructive decision
**SUSPEND final exclusivist metaphysics.**

Jesus remains central in the Abrahamic spine.
The framework does not presently declare that explicit assent to one Christological formula is the sole condition for divine acceptance.

## TRACE rule
Historical source analysis precedes doctrinal use.
