# Jesus Tradition — Gehenna and Judgment

## Problem
Jesus traditions contain serious judgment language, exclusion imagery, Gehenna sayings, destruction, and eschatological reversal.

## Historical context
Jewish eschatological judgment language predates Christianity and varies across Second Temple sources. “Gehenna” should not automatically be mapped onto every later Christian theory of an eternal torture chamber.

## Strongest traditional readings
- eternal conscious punishment;
- final destruction/annihilation;
- purgative/temporary judgment;
- symbolic warning;
- historical-prophetic judgment.

The texts do not settle all later systematic details uniformly.

## Ethical problem
Fear of hell has often been used to:
- coerce belief;
- traumatize children;
- suppress questions;
- justify exclusion.

## Constructive decision
**RETAIN moral accountability; SUSPEND detailed hell metaphysics.**

The Provisional Way may teach:
> actions matter and injustice has consequences.

It may not teach as established fact:
> every nonmember faces eternal conscious torture.

## Child safeguard
Graphic eternal-torment threats are not used as behavior control.
