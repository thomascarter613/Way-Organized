# Qur'anic Religious Pluralism and Exclusivity

## Source cluster
Q 2:62, 2:256, 3, 5:44–48, 5:72–73, 22, 60, 98, 109 and related passages.

## Observation
The Qur'an contains:
- strong criticism of particular Jewish/Christian theological claims and communities;
- claims for the truth/authority of its own proclamation;
- recognition of earlier revelation;
- positive or conditional statements concerning other communities;
- the principle that there is no compulsion in religion;
- calls for commonality and also boundary-making.

A one-line “Qur'an is pluralist” or “Qur'an rejects everyone else” description is inadequate.

## Constructive decision
**RETAIN freedom of conscience and serious truth claims simultaneously.**

The Provisional Way teaches:
- disagreement is real;
- not all propositions can be simultaneously true in the same sense;
- disagreement does not remove human dignity or civil/religious freedom;
- coercion cannot produce genuine faith.

## Norm
No legal penalty, social shunning, family coercion, or violence for merely changing religious conviction.
