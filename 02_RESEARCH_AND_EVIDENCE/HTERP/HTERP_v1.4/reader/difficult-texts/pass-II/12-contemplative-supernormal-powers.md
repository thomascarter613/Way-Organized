# Contemplative Claims of Supernormal Powers

## Scope
Buddhist, Yogic, shamanic, mystical, and later religious texts sometimes claim capacities such as:
- clairvoyance;
- telepathy;
- precognition;
- remote perception;
- miraculous physical effects;
- knowledge of previous lives.

## Historical conclusion
These are genuine claims in religious literature.

## Evidentiary conclusion
Textual antiquity does not verify the power.

A claimed siddhi/iddhi/miracle belongs under HTERP-AI/PRED when it makes an externally testable information or physical claim.

## Constructive decision
**Do not make supernatural ability a spiritual rank criterion.**

Any practitioner claiming testable powers is invited to:
- define claim prospectively;
- preregister;
- accept blinding;
- retain misses;
- permit independent replication.

Refusal to test does not prove fraud, but the claim receives no empirical authority.

## Spiritual rule
Compassion, truthfulness, justice, and humility outrank spectacle.
