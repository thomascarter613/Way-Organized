# Hadith Authority and Source Hierarchy

## Historical finding
Hadith criticism is itself a sophisticated historical tradition, but methods varied between:
- traditionists;
- jurists;
- schools;
- periods.

Isnad criticism, matn/content evaluation, corroboration, established practice, Qur'anic coherence, and later canonical status have not always been weighted identically.

Modern isnad-cum-matn and historical-critical work can sometimes trace reports to particular transmission periods or milieus.

## Constructive risk
Saying:
> “The hadith is sahih”
does not automatically answer:
- when the report's content first emerged;
- whether it preserves verbatim founder speech;
- whether its norm should be binding today.

## Decision
Hadith belongs to a **graded witness corpus**, not an undifferentiated Level-1 revelation block.

For major ethical/legal claims, require:
1. Qur'anic relationship;
2. earliest recoverable transmission;
3. isnad and matn analysis;
4. historical plausibility;
5. cross-source evidence;
6. ethical/constitutional review.

A solitary later report cannot override basic human dignity merely through inherited classification.
