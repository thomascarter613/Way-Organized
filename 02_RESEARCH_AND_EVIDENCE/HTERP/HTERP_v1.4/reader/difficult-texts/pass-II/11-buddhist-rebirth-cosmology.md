# Buddhist Rebirth and Cosmology

## Historical finding
Karma and rebirth are not marginal later additions to Buddhism. They are deeply embedded in classical Buddhist cosmology, including multiple realms and repeated rebirth.

Karma is not administered by a creator deity; intentional action shapes future consequences.

## Modern tension
Some modern/secular adaptations retain:
- mindfulness;
- ethics;
- no-self analysis;
while suspending literal rebirth/cosmology.

That adaptation should be named honestly rather than presented as simply “what Buddhism really taught.”

## Constructive decision
**SUSPEND rebirth/cosmology as HTERP-established fact.**

Retain as comparative wisdom:
- intention matters;
- habits condition experience;
- actions have consequences;
- attachment can perpetuate suffering.

Rebirth remains an O-category metaphysical possibility, not a required belief.

## Source
Damien Keown, *Buddhism: A Very Short Introduction*, karma/rebirth chapter.
