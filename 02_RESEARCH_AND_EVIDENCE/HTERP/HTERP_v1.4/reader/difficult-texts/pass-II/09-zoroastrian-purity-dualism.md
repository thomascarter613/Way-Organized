# Zoroastrian Dualism and Purity Development

## Historical finding
The religion represented by later Avesta/Pahlavi material is not identical in every respect with the Gathic layer.

Encyclopaedia Iranica reconstructs historical transformations involving:
- Gathic moral dualism;
- later Ahura Mazda/Ahriman formulations;
- Zurvanite developments;
- increasingly elaborate purity/pollution systems;
- a pantheon and ritual structures largely absent from the Gathas.

## Constructive risk
Reading ninth-century Pahlavi cosmology directly back into Zarathustra obscures development.

## Decision
**Prioritize Gathic material for the Zarathustra witness; later Zoroastrian systems enter as interpretive tradition.**

Retain:
- truth versus lie;
- moral choice;
- responsibility.

Suspend:
- detailed cosmic dualism as established metaphysics.

Reject:
- pollution rules that violate equal dignity merely because later tradition sacralized them.

## Sources
Encyclopaedia Iranica, “Dualism” and “Zoroastrianism i. Historical Review.”
