# Reader V — Gathas / Zarathustra

## Constructive role
Level 2 parallel prophetic witness.

## Corpus
The Gathas are Old Avestan hymns embedded in the Yasna:
- Ahunavaiti Gatha — Yasna 28–34
- Ushtavaiti — 43–46
- Spenta Mainyu — 47–50
- Vohu Khshathra — 51
- Vahishtoishti — 53

## Source significance
Often treated as the oldest Zoroastrian material and substantially associated with Zarathustra, though authorship/date questions remain.

## Key terms
- **Ahura Mazdā** — Wise Lord
- **aša** — truth/right order
- **druj** — lie/deceit
- **vohu manah** — good mind
- **spenta mainyu** — beneficent/bounteous spirit, with interpretation debated

## Reading questions
- What does Zarathustra claim to know from Ahura Mazda?
- Which passages are prayer/question rather than received answer?
- How does moral truth/order function?
- What is inherited Indo-Iranian background?
- What is reform/innovation?
- How do later Zoroastrian dualistic systems relate to the Gathic layer?

## Constructive use
The Gathas are especially valuable for:
- truth vs falsehood;
- moral agency;
- religious reform;
- ethical alignment.

They are not automatically declared additional scripture equal in status to Level 1.
