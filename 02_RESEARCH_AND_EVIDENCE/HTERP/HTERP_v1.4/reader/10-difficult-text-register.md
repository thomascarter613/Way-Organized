# Difficult Text Register — Initial

The register exists to prevent the future community from creating a curated “nice religion” by silently deleting difficult evidence.

## Torah
- flood destruction;
- Sodom narratives and interpretations;
- Akedah;
- plagues/firstborn;
- slavery laws;
- conquest/ḥerem;
- sexual/gender law;
- purity and disability questions;
- capital penalties.

## Prophets
- violent judgment imagery;
- nationalistic oracles;
- gendered metaphors;
- failed/deferred expectation questions;
- divine hardening.

## Jesus witness
- apocalyptic judgment;
- exclusivist sayings;
- family/discipleship tensions;
- hell/Gehenna interpretation;
- violence/nonviolence tensions in later reception;
- Gospel discrepancies.

## Qur'an/Muhammad witness
- warfare/conflict passages;
- legal punishments;
- gender/family law;
- slavery historical context;
- treatment of unbelief/apostasy in later tradition;
- exclusivist/pluralist tensions;
- hadith reliability and later legal expansion.

## Gathas/Zoroastrian
- moral dualism;
- fate of opponents;
- later purity/exclusion development;
- relationship of Gathic and later dualism.

## Sikh
- later community boundary/military history must not be projected backward;
- gender/equality ideals versus historical community practice.

## Buddhist
- cosmological/rebirth claims;
- gender/monastic hierarchy;
- karma and suffering;
- historical Buddhist violence despite non-harm ideals;
- later supernatural accretions.

## Rule
For every difficult text:
1. read it;
2. establish context;
3. identify textual/source issues;
4. study major interpretations;
5. state whether the constructive framework accepts, reinterprets, suspends, or rejects its normative force;
6. preserve the reasoning publicly.
