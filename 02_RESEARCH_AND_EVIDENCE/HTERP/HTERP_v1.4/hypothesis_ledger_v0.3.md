# HTERP Hypothesis Ledger — v0.3 After WP-002

## H0 — Construction / literary development / fraud
**Established partial cause.** WP-002 strengthens evidence for textual development in prophetic and Buddhist corpora and uneven oracle transmission. It does not justify universal dismissal.

## H1 — Cognitive / neurological generation or mediation
**Strongly established partial cause.** Sleep paralysis adds presence, figures, voices, terror, pressure, floating, and out-of-body states; bereavement adds benign experiences of deceased persons. Bare visions, voices, presence, and certainty therefore have lower discriminating value.

## H2 — Cultural/social shaping
**Pervasive and strongly supported.** Similar functional goals are implemented through bones, sanctuaries, odù interpretation, scripture, song, ritual, and visionary narrative. Interpretation tracks available ontology and authority structures.

## H3 — Transcendent impersonal reality
**Open, underdetermined.** Buddhist awakening, some Vedic material, and mystical traditions remain compatible with this model, but no WP-002 case independently verifies it.

## H4 — Transcendent personal intelligence/intelligences
**Open, underdetermined.** Paul gives strong first-person testimony and the historical corpus contains massive agentive claims, but natural/cultural controls prevent agent phenomenology from functioning as unique evidence.

## H5 — Mixed causal model
**Operationally required.** WP-002 shows that divination, visionary commission, awakening, sacred-land transmission, bereavement presence, and sleep paralysis are not one phenomenon.

## H6 — Category-conflation hypothesis
Some apparent religious convergence is created by researchers grouping unlike phenomena under “religion,” “revelation,” “mysticism,” or “spirituality.”

**Status: strongly supported as a methodological risk.**

## Strongest current empirical conclusion
Human cultures repeatedly develop or report interfaces with a domain construed as exceeding ordinary perception, but the interfaces, ontologies, contents, preservation systems, and mechanisms are heterogeneous. The metaphysical interpretation remains open.
