# Adversarial Governance Charter

## Team
At minimum:
- proponent representative;
- skeptic representative;
- neutral methods/statistics lead.

## Vetoes before data collection
Any of the three may block launch for:
- unblinding risk;
- undefined outcome;
- exploitable leakage;
- unsafe procedure.

Once registered, no side may alter the protocol unilaterally.

## Disputes
Disagreements are recorded publicly in an interpretation appendix rather than hidden.

## Positive result language
Proponent may state preferred source hypothesis separately.
Skeptic may state alternative explanations separately.
The common report states only what the data support jointly.

## Negative result language
A failed test applies to the operationalized claim/protocol, not automatically to every form of religion or transcendence.

## Replication ownership
Raw anonymized data and code should be available to independent analysts where ethically possible.
