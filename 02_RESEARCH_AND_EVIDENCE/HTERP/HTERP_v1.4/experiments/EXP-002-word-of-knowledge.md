# EXP-002 — Charismatic “Word of Knowledge” Trial

## Research question
Can claimants who report receiving divinely supplied personal information identify private target facts beyond chance, inference, and cold-reading controls?

## Two conditions

### Condition A — No-contact
Claimant receives only a random target code.

Best test of inaccessible information.

### Condition B — Minimal-contact
If claimant's theology requires a live recipient:
- target behind visual barrier or standardized remote audio;
- no free conversation;
- fixed prompt;
- no demographic introduction beyond preregistered minimum.

Analyze A and B separately.

## Target facts
Adults privately submit structured facts not readily searchable online.

Examples:
- randomly selected object currently stored in a sealed location;
- private but nontraumatic childhood event;
- preselected unusual hobby detail;
- random code/object assigned to target by independent coordinator after recruitment.

Avoid:
- medical secrets;
- sexual trauma;
- criminal allegations;
- information whose disclosure could harm family.

## Claimant task
For each target:
- provide up to N claims;
- label confidence 0–100%;
- distinguish image/impression/voice/inference if desired.

Limiting N prevents unlimited guessing.

## Primary outcome
Preregistered weighted factual accuracy against:
- target;
- matched decoys;
- nonprophetic control participants.

## Strongest design
Use experimenter-generated hidden targets unknown even to the participant, e.g.:
- one of many images;
- physical object;
- word/code;
randomized immediately before session.

This tests information access more cleanly than biographical facts.

## Theological interpretation
Claimant may believe success comes from God.
HTERP records that interpretation but primary result is only:
> information performance relative to control.

## Failure rule
A claimant cannot redefine misses as metaphor after unblinding unless the scoring rule allowed metaphor prospectively.

## Ethical boundary
No prophecy about:
- death;
- pregnancy;
- disease;
- damnation;
- relationship breakup;
- criminal guilt
is solicited in the experiment.
