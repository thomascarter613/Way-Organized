# Experiment Ethics and Safety Standard

## Participants
Adults unless a future protocol receives specialized independent child-research ethics review.

## No vulnerable-person exploitation
Do not recruit people in acute:
- bereavement crisis;
- psychosis;
- mania;
- suicidal crisis;
- severe dissociative destabilization
merely because such states may increase unusual experiences.

## No harmful messages
Experimental protocols do not solicit predictions/claims about:
- imminent death;
- disease diagnoses;
- damnation;
- criminal guilt;
- pregnancy;
- relationship catastrophe;
unless an independent ethics review establishes a compelling reason and safe handling.

## Clinical boundaries
Researchers do not tell participants:
- stop medication;
- reject clinicians;
- treat entity commands as medically authoritative.

## Grief
Mediumship participants receive:
- clear statement that the experiment does not establish survival after death;
- right not to receive reading contents;
- debrief resources.

## Privacy
Target facts are minimized, encrypted/secured, and separated from medium/claimant-facing systems.

## Fraud and adversarial testing
Participants are told that:
- deception controls exist;
- investigators may audit information pathways;
without accusing any participant personally.

## Religious dignity
A negative result is not described as proving someone's faith false.
A positive result is not described as proving their theology true.

## Withdrawal
Participants may withdraw subject to preregistered rules governing already-anonymized data.

## Debrief
Explain:
- what was tested;
- what was not;
- how blinding worked;
- when results will be interpreted.

## Safety stop
Independent monitor can halt a protocol for:
- serious adverse event;
- privacy breach;
- systematic unblinding;
- coercion;
- investigator misconduct.
