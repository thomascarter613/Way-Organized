# HTERP-WP-002 — Comparative Anchors

**Status:** completed baseline comparative pass  
**Version:** 0.3

## Scope
HTE-006, 007, 009, 014–017, 019, 024, 030, 031, 033, 034, and expanded HTE-047 sleep/dream controls.

## Objective
Scale the calibrated HTERP method beyond WP-001 while keeping four questions separate: evidence that a practice/report existed; evidence about subjective phenomenology; evidence about message/content; and metaphysical interpretation.

## Results

### W2-01 — Preservation systems are not commensurable
The Rigveda's specialized oral preservation, prophetic rolling corpora, early Buddhist formulaic oral composition, Pauline letters, Sikh manuscript formation, material divination archives, and living ceremonial knowledge represent different error models. A single “distance from founder” score is invalid.

### W2-02 — Historical independence is not evidential independence
Shang and Mari divination may have developed independently, but both answer universal uncertainty about war, weather, health, and rule. Independent origin of a practice is not automatically independent evidence for a transcendent source.

### W2-03 — Not every sacred tradition is an encounter event
Public Aboriginal songline/Dreaming traditions are better modeled as living sacred-law, place, ancestry, ceremony, and authorized transmission systems than as isolated founder revelations.

### W2-04 — Buddhist awakening is a category check
The Buddha is remembered primarily as attaining awakening through disciplined practice rather than receiving propositions from a creator deity. A global model limited to “which being spoke to which prophet?” is therefore biased.

### W2-05 — Paul is a strong ancient first-person self-report case
His undisputed letters provide first-person claims of revelatory/visionary experience. This strongly establishes self-report, not the external identity of the encountered object.

### W2-06 — Sleep and bereavement enlarge the control set
Sleep paralysis can produce felt presence, figures, voices, terror, pressure, floating, and out-of-body experience. Bereavement commonly produces benign sensed-presence and sensory experiences of deceased persons.

### W2-07 — Moralization is not universal
Shang, Greek, Ifá, and Maya divination often focus on practical uncertainty. Universal ethical reform is especially prominent in some prophetic and reform traditions, not in all claimed contact.

### W2-08 — Noetic certainty remains nondiscriminating
Awakening traditions, mystical states, seizures, psychedelics, and revelatory claims can all involve intense certainty. Certainty is phenomenology, not authentication.
