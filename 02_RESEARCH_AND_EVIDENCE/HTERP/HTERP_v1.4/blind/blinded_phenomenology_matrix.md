## Blind-pass observations
- Cases A–D all involve socially interpreted revelation, but their source structures are radically different.
- E and F demonstrate that high noetic certainty and sacred/revelatory phenomenology can arise under known causal manipulations.
- G demonstrates that convincing signs of agency or hidden knowledge can be manufactured.
- None of A–D currently contains a calibration-grade observation that by itself excludes E/F/G-type alternatives plus cultural transmission.
- The strongest distinction among A–D is not intensity but **message structure, source proximity, and institutional transmission**.
