# HTERP Hypothesis Ledger — v0.2 After WP-001 Calibration

## H0 — Construction, legend, literary development, fraud

### Updated evidence
**Strongly established as a partial cause.**
- historical traditions undergo literary and doctrinal development;
- fraud controls show contact phenomena can be intentionally simulated;
- source distance increases opportunity for retrospective elaboration.

### Limits
Cannot explain every sincere first-person experience merely by assertion.

### Current standing
**Required subset explanation; not sufficient universal explanation.**

---

## H1 — Cognitive / neurological generation or mediation

### Updated evidence
**Strongly established as a cause/mediator of some encounter phenomenology.**
- psilocybin causally increases mystical-type experience;
- neurological states can generate voice, presence, certainty, ecstasy, religious interpretation;
- dreaming supplies vivid agents and environments without ordinary external stimulus.

### Limits
Neural mediation is present in normal veridical perception too.
H1 alone does not logically rule out an external referent.

### Current standing
**Strong empirical support as partial explanation.**

---

## H2 — Cultural generation/shaping

### Updated evidence
**Pervasively supported.**
- Mari encounters are expressed through Mari's pantheon and institutions;
- Zarathustra works with inherited Iranian categories;
- Jesus' reported experience uses Jewish Spirit/scriptural categories;
- Qur'anic revelation engages late antique monotheistic and prophetic traditions;
- mystical interpretation changes with prior vocabulary and setting.

### Limits
Cultural shaping does not prove total cultural causation.

### Current standing
**Strong empirical support as interpretation mechanism; causal extent varies.**

---

## H3 — Transcendent impersonal reality

### Evidence compatible
- unitive/noetic experiences occur across religious boundaries;
- some traditions describe apprehension rather than a speaking being.

### Calibration challenge
- core mystical phenomenology is experimentally inducible;
- no calibration case yet demonstrates externally verifiable information uniquely predicted by H3.

### Current standing
**Open, underdetermined.**

---

## H4 — Transcendent personal intelligence/intelligences

### Evidence compatible
- ancient and modern cases contain agentive speech, commands, identity, and recurrent communication;
- some experiencers report coherent interpersonal relation.

### Calibration challenge
- voice/presence/agency experiences have neurological and psychological analogues;
- ancient cases usually lack information-leakage controls;
- fraud can imitate many external signs.

### Current standing
**Open, underdetermined. Strong update requires AI-3/PRED-3 quality evidence or better.**

---

## H5 — Mixed causal model

### Evidence
WP-001 confirms that the global corpus already contains demonstrably different mechanisms:
- institutional religious performance,
- literary transmission,
- pharmacological induction,
- neurological induction,
- culturally shaped interpretation,
- deliberate deception,
- sincere spontaneous experience.

### Current standing
**Operational working model.**
This is not a metaphysical conclusion; it is the minimum-complexity research posture able to accommodate known heterogeneity.

---

# Calibration conclusion

No case in WP-001 presently compels a transcendent explanation after H0/H1/H2 controls.  
No result in WP-001 disproves transcendent reality or noncorporeal intelligence.

The project therefore advances by looking for **discriminating evidence**, not by accumulating more intense testimony.
