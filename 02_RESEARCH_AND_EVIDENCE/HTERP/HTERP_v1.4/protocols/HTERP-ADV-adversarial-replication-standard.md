# HTERP-ADV — Adversarial Replication Standard

## Purpose
Prevent experiments on controversial transcendence claims from being trusted only by researchers already sympathetic to the conclusion.

## Required collaboration
- one investigator sympathetic to anomalous interpretation;
- one skeptical investigator;
- one neutral methods/statistics lead;
- protocol signed before data collection.

## Requirements
1. preregister hypothesis and exclusion rules;
2. independent randomization;
3. hidden target allocation;
4. prevent subject/investigator leakage;
5. full session recording;
6. retain all misses and null sessions;
7. multiple decoys;
8. blind judges where applicable;
9. effect-size and uncertainty reporting;
10. raw deidentified data release where ethical;
11. replication threshold specified in advance;
12. source-identification inference separated from anomaly detection.

## Decision language
Allowed:
> “This protocol detected information above its preregistered chance/control expectation.”

Not allowed without additional evidence:
> “Therefore spirits/deceased persons/God caused the result.”
