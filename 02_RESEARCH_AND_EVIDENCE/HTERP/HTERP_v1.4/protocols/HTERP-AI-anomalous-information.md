# HTERP-AI — Anomalous Information Protocol

**AI here means Anomalous Information, not artificial intelligence.**

## Research question
Are there well-documented cases in which a claimant appears to obtain specific information unavailable through ordinary sensory, inferential, documentary, or deceptive pathways?

## Admission criteria

A case enters Tier A only if:
1. target information is specific and objectively resolvable;
2. the claimant's statement is fixed before target revelation/outcome;
3. full statement is preserved, including misses;
4. information access pathways are documented;
5. independent adjudicators can score the result;
6. success criteria were defined prospectively or can be reconstructed without cherry-picking.

Tier B permits one missing control.
Tier C is historical/retrospective and hypothesis-generating only.

## Mandatory leakage audit
For each case record:
- who knew target information;
- who contacted claimant;
- all prior communications;
- public availability;
- social-media availability;
- inference from context;
- cueing;
- hot reading;
- cold reading;
- sensory leakage;
- device/network access;
- experimenter expectancy;
- selective memory;
- record alteration.

## Scoring

### Exact categorical targets
Use preregistered accuracy and chance baseline.

### Probabilistic predictions
Use Brier score:
`(forecast probability - outcome)^2`

### Free-response targets
Use multiple independent blind judges with decoy targets and preregistered similarity rubric.

### Multiple comparisons
Correct for:
- repeated guesses,
- multiple targets,
- flexible time windows,
- semantic looseness,
- post-hoc subdivision.

## Evidence levels
- **AI-0:** anecdote; not usable
- **AI-1:** documented but retrospective
- **AI-2:** prospective, weak controls
- **AI-3:** prospective, blind, leakage controlled
- **AI-4:** independently replicated under adversarial controls

HTERP will not treat any claim as strong evidence for H4 unless it approaches AI-3 or above.

## Historical cases
Ancient prophecies can be cataloged, but if the text cannot be securely dated before the claimed fulfillment they remain AI-1 at best.

## Adversarial replication
High-value cases should be reproduced with:
- believer-friendly investigator,
- skeptical/adversarial investigator,
- shared preregistration,
- independent statistician,
- public raw-data archive where ethical/legal constraints allow.
