# HTERP-WP-008 — Practice Prototype and Community Architecture

**Status:** completed as a 30-day pilot design  
**Version:** 0.9  
**Nature:** provisional and testable

## Purpose

Move the HTERP Constructive Framework from documents into lived practice.

The pilot is deliberately modest:
- no claim that ritual itself is divinely mandated;
- no extreme asceticism;
- no secret grades;
- no charismatic authority;
- no requirement to settle open metaphysics.

## Pilot objectives

During 30 days, test whether the framework produces:
- greater truthfulness;
- attention and recollection;
- ethical repair;
- study discipline;
- generosity/service;
- family/community coherence;
- humility rather than certainty inflation.

## Core rhythm

### Daily
- dawn orientation/prayer
- midday recollection
- evening examen
- scripture/wisdom reading
- brief silence

### Weekly
- seventh-day sacred interval
- shared gathering
- common meal
- study
- service/giving review
- rest

### Monthly
- one longer reflection/review
- evidence/doctrine questions recorded for HTERP rather than forced into answers
