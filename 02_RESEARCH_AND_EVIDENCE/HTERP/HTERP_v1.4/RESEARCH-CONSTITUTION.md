# HTERP — Research Constitution after WP-006

## Article I — Truth over inheritance
No tradition is protected from examination by age, authority, popularity, or sacred status.

## Article II — Experience and interpretation are distinct
An experience can be sincere while its interpretation is mistaken.

## Article III — Equal evidentiary standards
Claims favored by the researcher's own tradition receive no weaker controls than unfamiliar claims.

## Article IV — Confidence must be explicit
Use:
- established;
- strongly supported;
- plausible;
- possible;
- unknown;
- unsupported.

## Article V — Unknown is a valid conclusion
Uncertainty is not a defect to be filled with doctrine.

## Article VI — Earliest is not automatically truest
Source proximity improves some historical questions but does not prove metaphysical truth.

## Article VII — Later is not automatically corrupt
Later reflection may preserve, distort, clarify, or improve understanding. It must be evaluated, not dismissed by date alone.

## Article VIII — No secret salvific contradiction
Advanced teaching may require preparation. No hidden doctrine may secretly negate the public ethical core.

## Article IX — Human dignity before uncertain revelation
Unverified revelation does not justify coercion, abuse, exploitation, or suspension of ordinary safeguards.

## Article X — Revision remains possible
Any constructive theology arising from HTERP remains revisable when stronger evidence appears.
