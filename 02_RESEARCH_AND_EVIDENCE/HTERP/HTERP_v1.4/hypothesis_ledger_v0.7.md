# HTERP Hypothesis Ledger — v0.7 After First Major Synthesis

## H0 — Construction / fraud / literary development
**Established partial cause.**
Cannot explain every sincere experience, but must be tested in every case.

## H1 — Cognitive / neurological generation or mediation
**Strongly established partial cause.**
Explains many encounter features; does not logically decide external referent.

## H2 — Cultural / social shaping
**Pervasively established.**
Probably operates in virtually every interpreted report.

## H3 — Transcendent impersonal reality
**Open; no decisive discriminating evidence yet.**

## H4 — Transcendent personal intelligence/intelligences
**Open; experimental mediumship and prospective agent claims provide testable routes, not current proof.**

## H5 — Mixed causal model
**Operationally required.**

## H6 — Category conflation
**Strongly established methodological danger.**

## H7 — Progressive integration
**Historically supported pattern.**
Older names/cults/concepts are repeatedly absorbed into later systems. Metaphysical interpretation remains open.

## H8 — Practice-mediated access
**Open.**
Practices reliably mediate unusual states; whether any are externally perceptual remains unresolved.

## H9 — Anomalous information without identified source
**Critical open hypothesis.**
An anomaly, if confirmed, must be established before source attribution.

## H10 — One-source culturally filtered revelation
A single transcendent source may be interpreted differently through different cultural/cognitive systems.

### Evidence compatible
- recurring phenomenological families;
- recurring ethical themes;
- historical integrations;
- traditions themselves sometimes claim universal revelation.

### Evidence against/simple version
- early contradictions are substantial;
- natural mechanisms reproduce core phenomenology;
- no cross-cultural entity identity passes strong EID criteria.

### Status
**Coherent but currently underdetermined.**

## H11 — Multiple-transcendent-agent model
Different encounters may involve different noncorporeal intelligences.

### Evidence compatible
- diverse agent identities and messages;
- ancient traditions frequently assume plurality.

### Problems
- no independent entity authentication;
- contradiction/deception problem;
- natural agent-perception controls;
- no established hierarchy to an ultimate source.

### Status
**Open but evidentially weak.**

## H12 — Naturalistic sufficiency
Known/unknown natural human mechanisms may ultimately explain the full corpus without external transcendence.

### Evidence compatible
- strong H1/H2/H0 controls;
- no AI-4 anomaly;
- early doctrinal diversity.

### Limitation
Cannot be declared complete unless high-quality anomaly claims continue to fail and all relevant phenomena are adequately modeled.

### Status
**Strong live hypothesis, not proven universal conclusion.**

## Current comparative stance

HTERP should proceed as **metaphysically open but methodologically naturalistic-first**:

1. test ordinary mechanisms first;
2. do not infer transcendence from unexplained residue alone;
3. remain willing to update if discriminating evidence survives adversarial controls.


## Constructive status after WP-007
WP-007 does not change H0–H12. It creates faith/practice structures explicitly downstream of, and distinguishable from, the hypothesis ledger.
