# 30-Day Reading and Study Cycle

The cycle does not claim all readings have equal authority.
It trains comparative reading through the Abrahamic spine and selected witnesses.

## Days 1–7 — Torah / beginnings / covenant
1. Genesis 1–3 — creation, human vocation, moral awakening
2. Genesis 12–18 — Abraham traditions
3. Genesis 22 + 28 — testing and Jacob encounter
4. Exodus 3–4 — Moses and the divine name
5. Exodus 19–20 — Sinai and covenant
6. Deuteronomy 6 + 10 — love, allegiance, ethics
7. review using TRACE

## Days 8–13 — Prophetic critique
8. Amos 5
9. Hosea 6 + 11
10. Isaiah 1 + 6
11. Isaiah 40
12. Jeremiah 7 + 31
13. Ezekiel 18 + 36

## Days 14–19 — Jesus witness
14. Mark 1–2
15. Mark 8–10
16. Matthew 5
17. Matthew 6–7
18. Luke 10 + 15
19. selected passion/resurrection accounts compared across Gospels

Study question:
What is early witness, what is later theological framing, and what can be responsibly attributed to Jesus?

## Days 20–25 — Qur'anic proclamation
20. Qur'an 1; 96; 103
21. Qur'an 2 selections on Abraham/covenant
22. Qur'an 19 — Mary/Jesus
23. Qur'an 20 — Moses
24. Qur'an 49 + selected ethical passages
25. Qur'an 112 + selected divine-name passages

Study question:
What does the Qur'an itself say before later hadith/fiqh interpretation is added?

## Days 26–29 — Comparative witnesses
26. selected Gathas of Zarathustra
27. selected Guru Nanak / Guru Granth Sahib passages
28. selected early Buddhist ethical/awakening material
29. selected contemplative material: one Jewish/Christian/Sufi/Buddhist comparison

## Day 30 — Synthesis
Write:
- what appears common;
- what genuinely conflicts;
- what remains unknown;
- what changed in your practice;
- what you refuse to pretend to know.

## Copyright/source rule
The project should eventually create a source-reader with public-domain/openly licensed translations or citation maps rather than reproducing copyrighted modern translations wholesale.
