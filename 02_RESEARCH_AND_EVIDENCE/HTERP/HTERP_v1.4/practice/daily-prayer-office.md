# Daily Prayer Office — Pilot

## Postures
Any safe posture may be used.
A full cycle may include:
stand → bow → kneel/prostrate → sit in silence.

Posture is a tool for attention, not a measure of worth.

# Dawn

### Opening
> Most High, Source of truth and life, I turn toward you at the beginning of this day.

### Orientation
> Keep me from falsehood, cruelty, pride, and heedlessness.
> Form in me truth, justice, mercy, restraint, and courage.

### Reading
5–15 minutes from the assigned reading cycle.

### Silence
5 minutes.

### Intention
Name one concrete obligation for the day.

### Closing
> What I know to be right, strengthen me to do.
> What I do not know, teach me to seek without fear.

# Midday

> Most High, recall me from distraction.
> Let my words be truthful,
> my work honest,
> my power restrained,
> and my dealings just.

Silence for one minute.

Ask:
- Am I acting from fear, vanity, anger, appetite, love, duty, or truth?
- Is repair needed now?

# Evening

### Gratitude
Name three things received.

### Examen
- Where was I truthful?
- Where did I hide?
- Whom did I help?
- Whom did I harm?
- What governed my attention?
- What repair is possible?

### Confession
> I do not excuse what I can repair.
> Give me courage to tell the truth, restore what I can, and change.

### Closing
> Into what I do not control, I release myself.
> May truth remain when my certainty fails.

# Optional Abrahamic forms

Participants may additionally use:
- Psalms;
- the Shema;
- the Lord's Prayer;
- Qur'anic prayers;
- traditional Jewish/Christian/Islamic forms,
provided the original tradition is represented honestly.

No inherited prayer is required to be interpreted in a way contrary to its historical meaning.
