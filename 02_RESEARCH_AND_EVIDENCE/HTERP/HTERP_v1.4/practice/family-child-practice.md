# Family and Child-Friendly Practice

## Principle
Children are formed through practice, story, questions, and example—not forced professions of certainty they cannot evaluate.

## Daily family form — 5 to 10 minutes
- gratitude;
- one short story/teaching;
- one ethical question;
- one act of kindness planned;
- short prayer.

## Questions children are allowed to ask
All of them.

Acceptable adult responses include:
- “I don't know.”
- “Different people believe different things.”
- “Here is what this text says.”
- “Here is what historians think.”
- “Here is what we believe provisionally.”

## No fear-based formation
Do not use:
- graphic hell threats;
- divine punishment threats for ordinary childhood behavior;
- claims that questioning means moral corruption;
- coercive confession;
- isolation from safe outside relationships.

## Child fasting
Children do not perform adult fasting.
They may practice age-appropriate voluntary restraint:
- sweets;
- entertainment;
- waste;
paired with generosity.

## Child participation
Children may:
- read;
- ask questions;
- sing;
- help with food/service;
- keep a gratitude journal;
- learn source languages gradually.

## Safeguarding
All child programs follow the community safeguarding constitution.
