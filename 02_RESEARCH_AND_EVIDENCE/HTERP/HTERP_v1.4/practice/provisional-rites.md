# Provisional Rites

These rites are symbolic community practices, not declarations that HTERP has proven sacramental metaphysics.

## Entry / Covenant
1. period of study;
2. public questions;
3. voluntary affirmation of Public Covenant;
4. washing of hands/face or full immersion where desired;
5. community welcome;
6. meal.

No one is told the rite magically changes ontological status.

## Repentance / Repair
1. truthful naming of harm;
2. cessation;
3. restitution where possible;
4. apology without demanding forgiveness;
5. changed safeguards;
6. prayer.

Private confession to clergy is never required as the sole means of accountability.

## Marriage / Partnership blessing
Requires:
- free adult consent;
- legal compliance;
- explicit expectations;
- equality of dignity;
- financial transparency;
- freedom from coercion;
- community commitment to support safety.

Religious authority never requires a person to remain in abuse.

## Birth / Child welcome
Community commits to:
- care;
- education;
- truthfulness;
- protection;
- freedom to mature into their own convictions.

No infant is treated as morally guilty for lacking a rite.

## Illness
- prayer;
- presence;
- meals/practical care;
- qualified medical treatment.

Prayer does not substitute for medicine.

## Death
A simple form may include:
- respectful washing/preparation according to family wishes;
- plain shroud/coffin where desired;
- readings;
- lament;
- gratitude;
- burial/cremation according to law and family conviction.

Language:
> We entrust what we do not know to the Most High and refuse to counterfeit certainty about the fate of the dead.

## Memorial
Remember the life truthfully, including complexity.
Do not canonize every deceased member as secretly perfect.
