# 30-Day Rule-of-Life Pilot

## General instructions

For 30 days:
- do not add new obligations merely because they feel spiritually impressive;
- record practice honestly;
- missed practice is data, not moral catastrophe;
- never sacrifice necessary sleep, food, medicine, work, or family care to complete the pilot;
- track effects on behavior, not only feelings.

## Daily minimum

### Dawn — 10 to 25 minutes
1. intentional washing or face/hands cleansing;
2. stand/bow/kneel/prostrate as physically appropriate;
3. Dawn Prayer;
4. 5–15 minutes reading;
5. 5 minutes silence;
6. name one ethical intention.

### Midday — 3 to 7 minutes
1. stop;
2. Midday Prayer;
3. ask: “What is governing me right now?”
4. repair course if necessary.

### Evening — 10 to 20 minutes
1. Evening Prayer;
2. examen;
3. identify any repair owed;
4. gratitude;
5. 5 minutes silence.

## Weekly sacred interval

Prototype:
**Friday sunset through Saturday sunset**, reflecting the Torah/Sabbath spine.

This is a pilot choice, not yet a final universal rule.

Aim to reduce:
- nonessential commerce;
- unnecessary work;
- algorithmic/media distraction.

Increase:
- family;
- worship;
- study;
- outdoors/rest;
- service;
- common meals.

Essential care, safety, caregiving, and necessary employment are not moral failures.

## Weekly fast

Pilot option:
**one daylight fast each week**, sunrise to sunset, water permitted.

Exemptions include:
- children;
- pregnancy/breastfeeding;
- diabetes or other relevant conditions;
- eating disorders;
- illness;
- medication requirements;
- strenuous labor where unsafe;
- anyone advised medically not to fast.

Alternative:
fast from a nonessential consumption habit or provide service.

## Giving/service

During the pilot, choose one sustainable commitment:
- percentage of discretionary income;
- fixed amount;
- food/resources;
- or equivalent recurring service time.

No one is shamed for inability to give money.

## Daily journal fields

- practices completed;
- reading;
- dominant emotion;
- strongest desire/fear;
- act of truth;
- act of mercy/service;
- harm/repair needed;
- unusual experience, if any;
- confidence inflation? yes/no;
- sleep/health concern? yes/no.

## Day 30 review

Ask:
- Did I become more truthful?
- More patient?
- More just?
- More generous?
- More grounded?
- More willing to admit uncertainty?
- Did the practice damage sleep, health, family, work, or judgment?
- Did I become preoccupied with signs/visions?
- Which practices should continue, change, or stop?

The pilot succeeds by producing wiser practice, not extraordinary experiences.
