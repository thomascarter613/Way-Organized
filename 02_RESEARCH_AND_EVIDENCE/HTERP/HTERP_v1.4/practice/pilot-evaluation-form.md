# 30-Day Pilot Evaluation

Rate qualitatively: improved / unchanged / worsened / uncertain.

- truthfulness
- anger regulation
- generosity
- justice/fairness
- attention
- anxiety
- sleep
- physical health
- family relationships
- willingness to repair harm
- intellectual humility
- fixation on signs/visions
- pressure/guilt
- sense of meaning
- service to others

## Narrative questions
1. Which practice actually changed behavior?
2. Which felt performative or empty?
3. Which caused harm or unsustainable burden?
4. Which text most challenged prior beliefs?
5. Did the framework make uncertainty easier or harder to tolerate?
6. Did any unusual experiences occur?
7. Were those experiences interpreted more cautiously than before?
8. What should be removed?
9. What should be retained?
10. What remains missing?
