# Weekly Sacred Gathering — Pilot Order

Target length: 60–90 minutes.

## 1. Arrival / silence — 5 minutes
No announcements first.

## 2. Opening
> We gather to seek truth, practice justice and mercy, and remember what exceeds us.

## 3. Shared reading — 15 minutes
One Level 1 text.
A second comparative reading may follow.

## 4. Historical context — 10 minutes
A trained reader explains:
- date/provenance;
- literary context;
- major uncertainties.

Devotional reading and historical analysis are explicitly distinguished.

## 5. Reflection — 15 minutes
Not a sermon by unquestionable authority.
The facilitator states:
- text;
- interpretation;
- confidence;
- alternatives.

## 6. Silence / prayer — 10 minutes

## 7. Ethical accountability — 10 minutes
Community asks:
- who needs practical help?
- what harm needs repair?
- what service is scheduled?
- are funds/resources transparent?

No public forced confession.

## 8. Common meal
As practical.

## 9. Closing blessing
> May we leave less certain of ourselves and more faithful to truth, justice, mercy, and the good we can actually do.

## Monthly research forum
Once each month replace part of the reflection with:
- one HTERP case;
- one open theological question;
- one evidence update.
