# HTERP-WP-006 — First Major Synthesis

**Status:** completed  
**Version:** 0.7  
**Corpus:** 51 registered cases plus subcases and control families developed through WP-001–WP-005.

## Governing question

After dependency control, source criticism, preservation analysis, naturalistic controls, fraud controls, and modern experimental evidence:

> What, if anything, survives across the global historical record of claimed human contact with transcendent reality?

## Executive conclusion

HTERP now supports a strong historical/empirical conclusion:

> **Human beings across many cultures repeatedly report, cultivate, ritualize, and institutionalize experiences interpreted as access to realities, agents, knowledge, or sacred order beyond ordinary perception.**

It does **not yet** support a comparably strong metaphysical conclusion that:
- all such experiences have one transcendent source;
- any one historical religion uniquely possesses that source;
- all claimed agents are manifestations of one entity;
- no transcendent reality exists;
- anomalous information has been established to the level required to identify a discarnate or divine source.

The evidence instead supports a heterogeneous causal landscape.

## What WP-006 establishes

### S-01 — The encounter phenomenon is real as a human phenomenon
The reports, practices, texts, ritual systems, clinical states, and experiments are historical and empirical facts. Their interpretation remains open.

### S-02 — Known endogenous mechanisms explain important parts of the phenomenology
Dreaming, sleep paralysis, bereavement presence, psychedelics, epilepsy/neurology, absorption, dissociation, expectation, fasting, ritual, and trained attention can produce features otherwise described as revelatory or mystical.

### S-03 — Culture shapes experience reports deeply
Names, agents, cosmologies, moral interpretations, ritual expectations, and descriptions track cultural repertoires.

### S-04 — Culture is not therefore a complete explanation
Cultural shaping is compatible with:
- purely endogenous experience;
- an external but culturally filtered reality;
- multiple sources;
- mixed causes.

### S-05 — First-person certainty does not authenticate source
Noetic certainty can accompany controlled psychedelic states, neurological states, contemplation, prophecy, awakening, and channeling.

### S-06 — Later religion is often not identical with the founder/earliest layer
This is strongly supported, but the degree differs by tradition. Transmission may preserve, expand, reinterpret, systematize, or sometimes contradict earlier material.

### S-07 — Not all disagreement is late
Important contradictions appear at early or founder-proximate levels:
- creator-centered versus noncreator frameworks;
- one versus multiple divine agents;
- enduring personal identity versus no-self;
- exclusive worship versus plural divine practice;
- messenger revelation versus awakening through disciplined insight.

Therefore a simple model in which one pristine universal religion was later corrupted into all differences is not currently supported by the evidence.

### S-08 — Broad phenomenological convergence is real
Recurring features include:
- presence/agency;
- vision;
- voice;
- vivid dream;
- altered self-boundaries;
- changed time/space perception;
- noetic certainty;
- awe/terror/bliss/love;
- perceived commission or transformation.

But these features also occur in naturalistic controls. Their recurrence is evidence for a **human capacity-space**, not yet for one external source.

### S-09 — Ethical convergence exists but is partial
Several historically separated streams strongly value some combination of:
- truthfulness/integrity;
- restraint/self-mastery;
- compassion/generosity;
- justice/non-exploitation;
- humility/ego-decentering;
- disciplined attention/prayer/contemplation;
- transformation of character.

These values are neither unique to revelation nor universal in every encounter tradition. They are therefore candidate cross-cultural wisdom, not proof of a common revealer.

### S-10 — Anomalous-information evidence remains the highest-value discriminating frontier
Experimental mediumship is the strongest live candidate currently in the corpus, but positive and null results coexist. Even a robust anomaly would not identify its source without further evidence.

## Constructive-theology decision

WP-006 does **not** justify a binding creed.

It **does** justify beginning a provisional constructive framework with explicit confidence layers.

The project may therefore proceed to WP-007 under one rule:

> **No proposition becomes mandatory metaphysics merely because a historical tradition asserts it.**

WP-007 may construct:
- an epistemological constitution;
- a provisional ethical rule;
- public/advanced-practice layers;
- a canon/source architecture;
- a terminology for Ultimate Reality;
- theological branches explicitly labeled established / plausible / open.

It may not yet declare:
- one historical revelation uniquely final;
- one named entity to be definitively the source of all encounters;
- a final afterlife model;
- a closed canon;
- a salvific secret.
