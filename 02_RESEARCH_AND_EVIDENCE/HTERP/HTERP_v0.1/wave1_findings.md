# HTERP Wave 1 — Initial Comparative Findings
## Baseline pass, not final dossiers

This file records the first comparative pass. It is deliberately conservative: the aim is to identify what the evidence can already support and where later detailed dossiers must resist overclaiming.

## HTE-001 — Mari prophecy
**Mode:** MSG/AUD/VIS (exact phenomenology often unavailable)  
**Evidence character:** Contemporary or near-contemporary Akkadian administrative/letter evidence reports prophets and their performances to kings. This is strong evidence that prophetic intermediation was an actual social institution, not merely a later literary invention.  
**Source proximity:** Mostly P2 for the reported performance; subjective experience is much less directly accessible.  
**Preliminary finding:** Mari establishes that recognizable messenger-prophecy existed in the ancient Near East before the classical Hebrew literary prophets. It does not establish whether the claimed gods actually communicated.

## HTE-006 — Shang oracle-bone tradition
**Mode:** DIV  
**Evidence character:** Inscribed bones from the late Shang period directly document royal divinatory practice.  
**Source proximity:** Extremely strong for the existence of the practice; weak for subjective phenomenology because the inscriptions record questions/prognostications rather than introspective experience.  
**Preliminary finding:** A geographically distant civilization independently institutionalized procedures intended to obtain information from a nonordinary ancestral/divine domain. This is important evidence for recurrence of the *human practice*, not yet for recurrence of an identical experience or source.

## HTE-007 — Vedic rishis / Rigveda
**Mode:** INS/ILL, with divine encounter language varying by hymn  
**Evidence character:** The Rigveda is the oldest Sanskrit text, composed in the latter half of the second millennium BCE. Its poets understood sacred speech and ritual poetry as capable of penetrating and affecting cosmic order. Later tradition classifies Veda as shruti, “heard/revealed.”  
**Source proximity:** The hymns are ancient compositions associated with poetic lineages, but transmission was oral for a very long period.  
**Preliminary finding:** This is not simply biblical-style message prophecy. It is an alternative revelation model centered on inspired sacred speech, ritual efficacy, and insight into cosmic order.

## HTE-008 — Zarathustra / Gathas
**Mode:** MSG/VIS/INS  
**Evidence character:** The Gathas are the oldest Zoroastrian material and are widely associated with Zarathustra, though date, homeland, historicity, and exact authorship remain debated. The first four Gathas overwhelmingly address Ahura Mazda; Iranica describes Zarathustra as having a close revelatory relationship with Ahura Mazda.  
**Source proximity:** Potential P1, but authorship/date uncertainty requires a caution flag.  
**Preliminary finding:** Zarathustra is one of the strongest ancient non-Israelite candidates for sustained ethical-prophetic revelation: divine wisdom, truth versus lie, moral choice, and transformation of inherited religious categories.

## HTE-014–017 — Amos, Hosea, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel
**Mode:** MSG/VIS/AUD/JRN depending on prophet  
**Evidence character:** The prophetic books contain oracles, narratives, call visions, symbolic acts, and later literary shaping. Textual criticism demonstrates substantial transmission and redaction issues, especially in books such as Jeremiah and Isaiah.  
**Source proximity:** Mixed P2–P4 by unit; not one grade per canonical book.  
**Preliminary finding:** These texts cannot be treated as dictation transcripts. Nevertheless, they preserve a highly developed model of moralized prophecy in which claimed divine communication critiques rulers, cult, injustice, idolatry, and communal behavior.

## HTE-009 — Greek oracles
**Mode:** DIV/POS/MSG  
**Evidence character:** Dodona supplies thousands of inscribed consultation tablets; Delphi is richly attested in literature but no original Delphic oracle report survives.  
**Source proximity:** High for the reality of consultation at sites such as Dodona; uneven for actual divine responses and subjective states.  
**Preliminary finding:** “Greek oracle” must be split by sanctuary and evidence type. The practice of inspired divination is well established; the phenomenology of the Pythia is much harder to reconstruct than popular presentations imply.

## HTE-019 — Gautama Buddha
**Mode:** ILL  
**Evidence character:** Historicity is broadly accepted, but exact reconstruction of biography and sayings remains debated because the surviving canons derive from oral transmission and later textualization.  
**Source proximity:** Generally P3/P4 for surviving textual collections.  
**Preliminary finding:** The Buddha provides an essential contrast case: authority grounded primarily in awakening/direct insight rather than receipt of propositions from a creator deity. Any global model that defines “transcendent contact” only as a speaking supernatural person is therefore methodologically biased.

## HTE-023 — Jesus
**Mode:** prophetic teaching with reported VIS/PRS experiences in the Gospel tradition  
**Evidence character:** The Jesus tradition was transmitted orally and textually through early Christian communities. The kingdom/reign of God is consistently central in historical-Jesus scholarship, but the Gospels are literary-theological witnesses, not transcripts.  
**Source proximity:** Mainly P3 for Gospel traditions; some traditions may be earlier, but each saying/episode requires separate analysis.  
**Preliminary finding:** HTERP must separate (a) Jesus’ own reported religious experience and proclamation, (b) disciples’ experience of Jesus, and (c) later Christological interpretation. Combining them would destroy the central distinction of the project.

## HTE-024 — Paul
**Mode:** VIS/MSG/JRN  
**Evidence character:** Paul’s undisputed letters provide first-person claims of a life-altering appearance/revelation of Christ and later visions/revelations.  
**Source proximity:** P1 for the fact that Paul himself claimed such experiences.  
**Preliminary finding:** Paul is more valuable for phenomenological-history purposes than many ancient founders because we possess first-person material. That proves the claim and its life impact unusually well; it does not independently verify the external object of the experience.

## HTE-028 — Muhammad / Qur'anic revelation
**Mode:** MSG/AUD/VIS/DRM in the wider early tradition  
**Evidence character:** The Qur'an is an early source close to the founding community; early manuscripts have strengthened scholarly confidence that the Qur'anic corpus existed very early. Detailed narratives of the first revelation survive in later transmitted recensions that must be analyzed independently from the Qur'an itself.  
**Source proximity:** High for the existence and content of the early proclamation; lower for detailed phenomenology of the first encounter.  
**Preliminary finding:** HTERP must maintain at least two Muhammad corpora: **Qur'anic proclamation** and **biographical encounter tradition**. They answer different historical questions.

## HTE-030 — Guru Nanak
**Mode:** INS/ILL/PRS/MSG  
**Evidence character:** Sikhism offers comparatively early manuscript evidence. Scholarship on early Sikh manuscripts argues that a written tradition of bani may have begun during Guru Nanak’s lifetime, while later manuscript corpora illuminate early textual development.  
**Source proximity:** Potentially P1/P2 for compositions, depending on individual text and manuscript history.  
**Preliminary finding:** Nanak is an unusually valuable later comparison because founder-associated sacred composition and the formation of scripture are historically much closer together than in many ancient cases.

## HTE-031 — Yoruba Ifa
**Mode:** DIV/MSG  
**Evidence character:** Living and historically documented divination uses trained priests, a large interpretive corpus, palm nuts/chains, and ritual apparatus; museum documentation explicitly describes communication with Orunmila to obtain insight into destiny.  
**Source proximity:** Excellent for modern/early-modern practice; chronological depth of specific verses and procedures must be assessed separately.  
**Preliminary finding:** Ifa is not well represented by the category “prophecy.” It is a disciplined knowledge system of divination with specialist training and an enormous interpretive corpus.

## HTE-033 — Australian Aboriginal Dreaming traditions
**Mode:** cannot be reduced to one code; PRS/INS/ILL are provisional umbrella tags  
**Evidence character:** The term “Dreaming” covers a complex network of knowledge, law, ancestral beings, place, ritual, identity, and differing community traditions. Some knowledge is initiatory/restricted.  
**Source proximity:** Living oral traditions with very deep cultural continuity, but not a single unified system and not automatically a record of discrete “encounters.”  
**Preliminary finding:** HTERP must disaggregate by people, language group, and specific tradition. Treating “Aboriginal religion” as one encounter case would reproduce a colonial category rather than analyze evidence.

## HTE-034 — Maya traditions
**Mode:** DIV/VIS/MSG  
**Evidence character:** Archaeological and iconographic evidence strongly establishes divination, ritual communication with divine powers, visionary imagery, and calendrical prophecy. Recent archaeological scholarship documents divining tokens and dedicated divination spaces.  
**Source proximity:** High for ritual practice; variable/low for first-person subjective phenomenology.  
**Preliminary finding:** Maya evidence strengthens the case that ritualized attempts to obtain information from divine/supernatural domains arose outside the Old World prophetic complex.

## HTE-045/046 — Laboratory and psychoactive mystical experience
**Mode:** UNI/ILL/PRS/VIS  
**Evidence character:** Controlled psilocybin studies and validated Mystical Experience Questionnaire research show that pharmacological manipulation can reproducibly occasion experiences involving unity, sacredness/noetic quality, positive mood, transcendence of time/space, and ineffability.  
**Preliminary finding:** Cross-cultural recurrence of mystical phenomenology cannot by itself prove a common external transcendent source. Human neurobiology is demonstrably capable of producing at least some of the same phenomenological dimensions under controlled conditions.

## HTE-047 — Neurological comparison
**Mode:** VIS/AUD/PRS/ILL-like states  
**Evidence character:** Clinical literature reports religious or mystical experiences in a minority of patients with focal/temporal-lobe epilepsy, including sensed presence, visions, certainty, ecstatic states, and religious conviction.  
**Preliminary finding:** Neurological causation is a live explanation for some encounter reports. Historical attempts to diagnose ancient figures retrospectively are weak unless contemporaneous clinical evidence exists; HTERP will therefore use neurology as a mechanism/control, not as a license to diagnose prophets.

## HTE-048 — Fraud control
**Mode:** apparent POS/CHA/APP  
**Evidence character:** Modern Spiritualism supplies high-documentation examples of mediums exposed using ordinary techniques while claiming communication with the dead; Houdini’s investigations are one historically prominent example.  
**Preliminary finding:** A serious corpus requires intentional deception as a standing hypothesis wherever information, performance conditions, money, prestige, or authority create incentives.

# Initial cross-case results

## Result 1 — Global recurrence is strongest at the level of *contact structures*, not theology
Across geographically separated traditions we repeatedly find:
- information sought from beyond ordinary perception,
- specialists or unusually gifted individuals,
- visions/voices/dreams,
- ritualized procedures,
- claims of special knowledge,
- states interpreted as contact with agents or deeper reality.

The metaphysics attached to those structures varies enormously.

## Result 2 — “Prophecy” is only one branch
Messenger prophecy, technical divination, inspired sacred poetry, possession, otherworld journey, and awakening must remain distinct. A theory designed around Hebrew prophecy would badly misclassify much of the global record.

## Result 3 — Moralization varies
Many early divinatory systems concern royal policy, harvest, warfare, illness, ritual correctness, or future events. Strong ethical critique becomes central in some streams (notably Zarathustrian and Israelite prophetic traditions) but is not a universal property of claimed contact.

## Result 4 — Phenomenological convergence is real but underdetermines cause
Modern controlled studies can reproduce central mystical dimensions. This strongly supports a human-neurocognitive capacity for such states. It neither proves nor disproves transcendent reality, because an external reality—if real—would still have to be mediated by a nervous system.

## Result 5 — Cultural interpretation is strongly supported
People ordinarily name and explain extraordinary experiences using categories available to them. This makes the distinction between **raw phenomenology** and **interpretation** indispensable.

## Result 6 — Source quality is multidimensional
Paul gives unusually strong first-person evidence of claiming visions; Shang bones give unusually strong material evidence of divinatory practice; Mari gives unusually strong administrative evidence of prophetic institutions; none of these strengths is identical.

## Result 7 — The strongest future test is dependency-controlled specificity
General themes such as “there is more than ordinary reality” are too broad to discriminate hypotheses. More weight should go to **specific, independently recurring structures or information** that cannot easily be explained by shared culture, normal inference, literary borrowing, or known cognitive mechanisms.

# Immediate research rules derived from Wave 1
1. Split traditions into individual claims before comparison.
2. Score documentation and phenomenology separately.
3. Never infer objective entity identity from the experiencer’s label alone.
4. Never infer hallucination merely because a brain correlate exists.
5. Never infer transcendence merely because an experience is ineffable, transformative, or cross-cultural.
6. Treat predictions as evidence only when documented before fulfillment and sufficiently specific.
7. Treat information claims as evidence only after leakage, inference, prior knowledge, and retrospective editing are excluded.
8. Preserve “unknown” as a positive research outcome.
