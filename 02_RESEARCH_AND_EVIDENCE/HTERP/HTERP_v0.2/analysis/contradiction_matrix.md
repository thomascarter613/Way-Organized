The purpose of this matrix is not to decide winners. It identifies where encounter traditions make claims that cannot all be literally identical without reinterpretation.

## Key contradictions

### C-01 — plurality versus exclusive monotheism
Mari's religious world contains many legitimate divine agents. Qur'anic proclamation explicitly rejects worship of other gods. Both cannot be the same literal theology without reinterpretation.

### C-02 — agent-revelation versus non-agent illumination
Some mystical states include no speaking agent at all. If such states are treated as access to ultimate reality, they do not naturally map onto messenger-revelation without an additional theory.

### C-03 — induced certainty
Experimental and neurological cases can produce certainty/noetic quality without independent verification of content. Therefore “the experiencer was absolutely certain” cannot resolve theological contradictions.

### C-04 — intermediary identity
Later traditions may identify a messenger as an angel, deity, spirit, or psychological phenomenon. Entity labels cannot be accepted as self-authenticating.

## Research consequence

Contradictions should be localized by layer:

**raw phenomenology → experiencer interpretation → first report → textual redaction → institutional doctrine**

If contradiction increases sharply in later layers, a common-source theory remains more plausible than if incompatible claims are present in the earliest recoverable phenomenology itself.
