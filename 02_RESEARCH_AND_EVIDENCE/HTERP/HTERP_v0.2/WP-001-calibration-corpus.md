# HTERP-WP-001 — Calibration Corpus

**Status:** completed baseline calibration  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Scope:** HTE-001, HTE-008, HTE-023, HTE-028, HTE-045/046, HTE-047, HTE-048

## Objective

Stress-test the HTERP method against cases with radically different evidentiary structures before scaling the research system across the full global corpus.

The calibration asks three separate questions in every case:

1. **Report question:** how securely can we establish that the encounter, practice, or claim was reported?
2. **Reconstruction question:** how securely can we reconstruct what the experiencer experienced or communicated?
3. **Explanation question:** what causal hypotheses remain live after historical, cultural, cognitive, neurological, and fraud controls?

These questions are never collapsed.

## Calibration results

### CR-001 — Documentation strength and phenomenological strength are independent axes
Mari and Shang-style evidence can prove a religious practice existed while telling us little about the experiencer's internal state. Laboratory psychedelic work can document internal phenomenology exquisitely while providing no independent confirmation that the reported metaphysical interpretation is externally true.

### CR-002 — “Revelation” is not a single phenomenon
The calibration distinguishes:
- messenger prophecy,
- inspired sacred composition,
- visionary commissioning,
- sustained recitative revelation,
- unitive/noetic mystical states,
- neurological sensed-presence and certainty,
- deliberately staged pseudo-contact.

### CR-003 — First-person sincerity is not metaphysical verification
A first-person source can strongly establish that a person claimed an experience. It does not by itself establish the external identity or existence of the apparent source.

### CR-004 — Cultural interpretation is pervasive
Encounter reports use the conceptual vocabulary already available to the experiencer and community. This can shape both what is noticed and how it is named.

### CR-005 — Neurocognitive mechanisms are demonstrably capable of generating central “mystical” features
Controlled psilocybin administration can produce unity, sacredness/noetic quality, positive mood, transcendence of time/space, and ineffability. Rare focal seizures can produce intense certainty, bliss, sensed presence, voices, and religious interpretations. Therefore those features are not, individually, discriminating evidence for external transcendence.

### CR-006 — Neural mediation does not logically entail metaphysical falsity
Every ordinary perception is neurally mediated. Demonstrating a neural correlate or inducible state shows that the brain can generate or mediate the phenomenology; it does not by itself decide whether any particular historical experience had an external referent.

### CR-007 — Fraud must remain a standing adversarial hypothesis
Mediumistic and revelatory performances can be simulated through ordinary techniques. Claims involving hidden information require information-leakage controls and independent adjudication.

### CR-008 — The highest-value evidence is discriminating evidence
The program should prioritize:
- specific information unavailable by normal means,
- prospective predictions documented before resolution,
- independently recurring highly specific structures after dependency analysis,
- observations that differ in expected probability under H0/H1/H2 versus H3/H4.
