# HTERP Hypothesis Ledger — v0.1

## H0 — Constructed / invented / legendary / fraudulent
### Evidence already supporting applicability to some cases
- Human religious traditions demonstrably undergo literary development and retrospective expansion.
- Modern mediumship provides documented fraud cases.
- Institutional incentives can reward revelation claims.

### Limits
- H0 cannot simply explain away first-person experiences whose sincerity is otherwise plausible.
- Fraud in one case supplies no evidence that every case is fraud.

### Status
**Established as a real cause in a subset of cases; universal scope unsupported.**

---

## H1 — Cognitive / neurological generation
### Evidence already supporting applicability
- Psychoactive substances can reproducibly occasion validated mystical-type phenomenology.
- Focal/temporal neurological disturbances can sometimes produce sensed presence, visions, ecstatic states, and intense religious conviction.
- Dreams naturally generate vivid agents, narratives, voices, and apparent environments.

### Limits
- Showing a neural mechanism does not by itself establish that an experience has no external referent; every perception has neural mediation.
- Retrospective neurological diagnosis of historical figures is generally weak.

### Status
**Established as a real cause or mediator of some encounter-like experiences; universal explanatory sufficiency unproven.**

---

## H2 — Cultural construction/shaping
### Evidence already supporting applicability
- Experience reports are interpreted through culturally available categories.
- Similar encounter structures receive different entity-identifications and metaphysical explanations.
- Texts and institutions reshape earlier reports over time.

### Limits
- Cultural shaping does not establish total cultural generation.
- Some structural similarities may survive strong cultural differences.

### Status
**Strongly supported as a pervasive interpretive factor; degree of causal generation varies by case.**

---

## H3 — Transcendent impersonal reality
### Evidence potentially compatible
- Unitive and illumination traditions sometimes report ultimate reality without a speaking personal agent.
- Similar noetic/unitive structures recur in multiple traditions.

### Current evidentiary problem
- These phenomenological features can also be induced through known neurocognitive mechanisms.
- No agreed external measurement independently confirms the metaphysical interpretation.

### Status
**Live philosophical/metaphysical hypothesis; not historically or experimentally established by current corpus.**

---

## H4 — Transcendent personal intelligence/intelligences
### Evidence potentially compatible
- Many traditions report apparently intentional, information-bearing agents.
- Some reports describe commands, dialogue, self-identification, or unexpected information.

### Current evidentiary problem
- Agent-like experiences also occur in dreams, neurological states, psychoactive states, dissociation, and fraud.
- Most ancient cases lack documentation adequate to exclude ordinary information pathways.

### Status
**Live hypothesis; no corpus-wide establishment at present.**

---

## H5 — Mixed causal model
### Evidence
Known data already require multiple causes:
- fraud exists;
- literary development exists;
- dreams exist;
- neurological religious experiences exist;
- pharmacologically induced mystical experiences exist;
- sincere first-person experiences exist;
- culturally different interpretations exist.

### Status
**Adopted as the working research stance, not as a final metaphysical conclusion.**

---

# What could move the ledger?

## Evidence that would significantly strengthen H4
- specific information apparently unavailable to the experiencer,
- securely documented before independent verification,
- with fraud/leakage/inference excluded,
- replicated across independent cases;
or
- highly specific encounter structures recurring in genuinely independent populations beyond what known cognitive mechanisms predict.

## Evidence that would strengthen H3
- reproducible access to information or features of reality through nonordinary states that exceed chance/normal inference without requiring personal-agent models;
or
- robust cross-cultural phenomenology whose structure predicts independently discoverable facts.

## Evidence that would strengthen H1/H2 as sufficient explanations
- successful predictive models deriving reported encounter contents from neurocognitive state plus cultural priors,
- especially if they explain apparently anomalous information without remainder.

## Evidence that would weaken all transcendent hypotheses
- repeated collapse of apparently strong anomalous cases under better controls,
- demonstration that specificity tracks prior cultural exposure,
- failure of prospective tests while retrospective claims remain common.

# Provisional rule
**Transformative power is psychologically important but not, by itself, evidence of metaphysical truth.**
