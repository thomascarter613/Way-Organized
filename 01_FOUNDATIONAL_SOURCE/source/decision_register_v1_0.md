# v1.0 Decision Register

## Adopted in founding synthesis
- The Constitution of the Way is a religious constitution distinct from civil charter/bylaws.
- Entrenched rights and anti-coercion principles sit above ordinary teaching/practice.
- Companion is religious identity, not statutory corporate membership.
- Commissioning remains the present ministry authorization model; ordination remains reserved.
- Library of Witnesses remains open; no Way canon is declared.
- Sevenfold practice is the founding practice framework.
- Teaching status grammar is constitutionalized for the founding period.
- The Assembly may teach ethical safeguards more firmly than unresolved metaphysical claims.
- Founder status remains historical only; succession is system transmission, not appointment of an heir.

## Deliberately unresolved
- Final legal/public corporate name.
- Comprehensive metaphysics of God/ultimate reality.
- Christology, Qur'anic divine authorship/finality, Buddhist rebirth/nirvana metaphysics.
- Final afterlife doctrine.
- Comprehensive marriage/family theology.
- Ordination theology.
- Comprehensive sacred calendar.
- Formal doctrine of dual religious belonging.
