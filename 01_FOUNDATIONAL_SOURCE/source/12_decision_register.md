# v0.8 Decision Register

## Adopt now
- Library of Witnesses as the non-canonical research corpus.
- Evidence-layer, claim-class, confidence-state, and change-my-mind protocol.
- Seven foundational dossiers as research baselines, not closed doctrines.
- Provisional Theological Baseline as the boundary between what may presently be taught institutionally and what remains open.

## Explicitly not adopted
- A closed Assembly canon.
- A final doctrine of God/ultimate reality.
- Christological or Qur'anic revelation dogma.
- Rebirth, resurrection, immortal-soul, or postmortem-consciousness dogma.
- Any living office of infallible prophet, revealer, master, or doctrinal sovereign.
