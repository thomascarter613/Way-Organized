# 5. House of Prayer Operating Model
A House of Prayer is a stable local expression of the Assembly. It is a community before it is a building. A House may gather in a home, rented room, public facility, dedicated property, or online/hybrid environment where appropriate.

## Recognition stages
- 1. Gathering Circle - a recurring group exploring the Way; no claim to be an official House.
- 2. House-in-Formation - meets stated minimum practices, completes safeguarding and governance training, and has an accountable local Steward.
- 3. Recognized House of Prayer - formally recognized through a written instrument defining scope, responsibilities, financial arrangements, complaint routes, and review date.
- 4. Mature House - demonstrates stable practice, local formation, healthy leadership rotation, transparent finances, service, and ability to reproduce without dependence on one charismatic person.
- 5. Dormant / Closed House - recognition may end without shame when the community no longer functions. Property and records follow written legal arrangements; people remain free.

## Local leadership
- House Steward - coordinates logistics, scheduling, records, budget interfaces, and facility matters.
- Teacher - teaches within a defined area of competence and is subject to review and correction.
- Minister - provides defined religious or pastoral functions when commissioned.
- Safeguarding Contact - maintains reporting information and independent escalation access; should not be the sole local executive if avoidable.
- No local role may independently create debt, employ staff, open accounts, sign property agreements, issue credentials, or represent itself as a separate legal entity without written corporate authority.

## House review
- Each recognized House should complete at least an annual health review covering participation, safeguarding, finances, leadership concentration, complaint accessibility, teaching integrity, service, departures, and whether people can disagree or leave safely.

| Minimum function | Required at recognition | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular gathering | Yes | Creates an actual community rather than a nominal chapter. |
| Prayer / worship | Yes | Maintains the devotional center of the House. |
| Study / inquiry | Yes | Keeps teaching connected to evidence, sources, and ongoing learning. |
| Service / mutual care | Yes | Turns the Way outward into practical care. |
| Safeguarding contact and escalation path | Yes | Ensures protection does not depend on the local leader. |
| Local Steward | Yes | Coordinates operations without becoming local sovereign. |
| Teacher / Minister | No at first | May be shared, visiting, remote, or developed later. |
| Owned building | No | House means community/function, not property ownership. |
| Separate bank account or legal entity | No | Requires explicit written authorization and legal structure. |
