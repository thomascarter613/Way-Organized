# Comparative Source Notes

- My Jewish Learning, "The Shema" - daily morning/evening recitation and the integration of belief, remembrance, and practice. https://www.myjewishlearning.com/article/the-shema/
- My Jewish Learning, "The Amidah" - recurring prayer structure of praise, petition, and thanksgiving. https://www.myjewishlearning.com/article/the-amidah/
- Quakers in Britain, Yearly Meeting pages - worship, decision-making, learning, community, and age-appropriate participation. https://www.quaker.org.uk/our-organisation/about-yearly-meeting
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Ethics in Indian Buddhism" - moral discipline, compassion, meditative development, and wisdom as integrated formation. https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-indian-buddhism/
- SGPC, Sikh Rehat Maryada (English) - example of a mature tradition codifying conduct, prayer, community memory, and religious practice. https://sgpc.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Sikh-Rehat-Maryada-English-n.pdf

These are comparative structural references only. v0.7 does not reproduce their liturgies or claim doctrinal equivalence.
