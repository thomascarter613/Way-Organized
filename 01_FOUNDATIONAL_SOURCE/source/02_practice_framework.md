# Sevenfold Practice Framework

**Version:** 0.7  
**Date:** September 19, 2026  
**Organization:** UNDECIDED  

## Practices

- Attention: Become still enough to notice reality, motives, suffering, gratitude, beauty, conscience, and the presence/absence of God as experienced.
- Prayer: Address, listen, lament, praise, petition, give thanks, confess, remember, and dedicate oneself according to conscience and developing theology.
- Study: Engage sacred sources, history, languages, scholarship, science, philosophy, and competing interpretations.
- Discernment: Test experiences, claims, choices, motives, and communal proposals without treating authority as proof.
- Ethical practice: Cultivate truthfulness, mercy, justice, fidelity, non-exploitation, responsibility, courage, and repair of harm.
- Service: Act for the good of persons, communities, creatures, and the world without conditioning aid on religious conformity.
- Companionship: Share life, mutual care, hospitality, accountability, grief, celebration, and learning while preserving conscience and outside relationships.
