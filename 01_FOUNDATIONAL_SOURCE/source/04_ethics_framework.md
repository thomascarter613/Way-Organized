# Ethical Teaching Framework

**Version:** 0.7  
**Date:** September 19, 2026  
**Organization:** UNDECIDED  

## Commitments

- Human dignity: No person becomes a mere instrument of a leader, institution, ideology, donor, family, or majority.
- Truthfulness: Do not deliberately deceive, manipulate testimony, conceal material conflicts, fabricate revelation, or manufacture certainty.
- Non-coercion: Religious conviction does not authorize control of another competent adult's conscience, relationships, healthcare, property, livelihood, or exit.
- Justice and mercy: Oppose exploitation and unfairness while preserving compassion, proportionality, repair, and due process.
- Sexual and relational integrity: Consent, honesty, protection of minors/vulnerable persons, and avoidance of exploitative spiritual power are non-negotiable; detailed sexual ethics remain a separate theological project.
- Stewardship: Money, property, environment, time, information, and authority are entrusted goods rather than trophies of office.
- Repair: When harm occurs: stop harm, protect those at risk, tell the truth, make legally/ethically required reports, restore what can be restored, accept consequences, and learn.
- Peace and force: The tradition should study nonviolence, self-defense, policing, war, and protection of the vulnerable rather than assuming a position before completing the ethical inquiry.
