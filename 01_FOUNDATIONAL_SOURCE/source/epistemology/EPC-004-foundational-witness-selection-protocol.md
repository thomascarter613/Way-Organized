# EPC-004 — Foundational Witness Selection Protocol

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Working Constitutional Baseline  
**Parents:** EPC-001, EPC-002, EPC-003  
**Domain:** Candidate identification, source decomposition, comparative evaluation, and admission to the Foundational Witness Corpus

# Preamble

The Foundational Witness Corpus shall not be assembled by beginning with an already chosen religion and defending its inherited canon.

Nor shall it be assembled by collecting one text from every major religion for artificial equality.

Its purpose is to identify the strongest recoverable witnesses to transcendent encounter, moral transformation, revelatory teaching, spiritual insight, and humanity's relationship to ultimate reality, wherever those witnesses occur.

No tradition receives automatic representation.

No tradition receives automatic exclusion.

A candidate enters because its evidence and content warrant examination.

A candidate remains foundational only because continued examination warrants its authority.

# Article I — Unit of Selection

The fundamental unit is not the religion and not necessarily the book or named prophet.

It is the smallest reasonably recoverable witness-unit capable of meaningful evaluation.

A witness-unit may be a saying, saying cluster, hymn, oracle, law collection, narrative stratum, discourse, prophetic poem, teaching, closely related proclamation set, or where textual history permits an entire work.

    RELIGION
       ≠
    CANONICAL BOOK
       ≠
    TEXTUAL STRATUM
       ≠
    HISTORICAL TEACHING
       ≠
    FOUNDATIONAL WITNESS

# Article II — No Whole-Corpus Presumption

No inherited scripture enters FWC-A automatically en bloc merely because another religion receives it as a unit.

Composite literature shall be decomposed where responsible.

“Isaiah says” may be too imprecise; the proper unit may be a particular compositional stratum or recoverable prophetic core.

The same principle applies to every layered tradition.

# Article III — Candidate Tracks

A witness may be nominated through one or more tracks:

- R — Revelatory Witness: claimed communication from personal divine/transcendent source.
- I — Illumination Witness: awakened, contemplative, mystical, or direct apprehension.
- E — Ethical Witness: exceptional moral significance independent of metaphysical provenance.
- H — Historical Witness: indispensable to reconstructing a foundational movement or encounter.
- P — Practice Witness: foundational discipline of prayer, contemplation, ethics, community, or ritual.
- L — Liturgical Witness: exceptional formative significance in worship/recitation.

No track is intrinsically superior.

# Article IV — Candidate Statuses

- CAND-0 — Identified.
- CAND-1 — Preliminary Screen Passed.
- CAND-2 — Dossier Open.
- CAND-3 — Evaluated.
- CAND-4 — Foundational Candidate.
- CAND-5 — Provisionally Received.
- FWC-A — Foundational Witness.
- CAND-U — Unresolved.
- CAND-X — Not Admitted to FWC-A.

Failure to reach FWC-A does not imply falsehood or worthlessness.

# Article V — Admission Is Claim-Specific

A witness may carry different authority in different domains.

Example profile:

Historical authority: High.
Ethical authority: Very High.
Founder attribution: Moderate.
Cosmological authority: Unresolved.
Liturgical authority: High.

This is preferable to undifferentiated “inspired” or “rejected.”

# Article VI — Mandatory Candidate Dossier

Each serious candidate receives FWSD — Foundational Witness Selection Dossier.

Required domains include:
identity; language; date; geography; attribution; textual history; oral history; founder proximity; encounter provenance; genre; historical context; claim inventory; ethics; metaphysics; contradictions; independence; comparative parallels; reception; adverse evidence; open questions; authority profile; recommendation.

# Article VII — Source Freeze

Before theological evaluation identify as closely as evidence permits:
1. earliest recoverable form;
2. later additions/redactions;
3. significant variants;
4. traditional expansions;
5. later interpretive frameworks.

Later material may itself become a candidate but shall not be silently projected backward.

# Article VIII — Founder Recovery Rule

Distinguish:

    HISTORICAL PERSON
           ↓
    EARLIEST RECOVERABLE TEACHING
           ↓
    EARLY COMMUNITY MEMORY
           ↓
    EARLY TEXT
           ↓
    LATER TEXT
           ↓
    SYSTEMATIC TRADITION

The purpose is not automatically to privilege the historical founder over every later insight but to prevent later theology from masquerading as demonstrably original teaching.

# Article IX — Oral Transmission Is Neither Automatic Defect nor Guarantee

Evaluate the actual oral transmission system.

“Oral” shall never be a synonym for either “corrupt” or “perfectly preserved.”

# Article X — Textual Stability Is Not Founder Attribution

Textual stability and founder attribution are separate.

Likewise wording uncertainty does not automatically mean teaching uncertainty.

A teaching pattern can sometimes be historically stronger than one exact sentence.

# Article XI — Redaction Is Not Automatic Corruption

Editorial activity may distort, harmonize, reinterpret, preserve, arrange, contextualize, expand, or clarify.

Describe redaction rather than prejudging it morally.

# Article XII — Independent Convergence Rule

Before claiming convergence test common linguistic ancestry, cultural ancestry, trade/contact, textual borrowing, intermediary traditions, common political circumstances, and trivial universality.

Vedic rta and Avestan asa, for example, shall not be counted as two wholly independent discoveries when common Indo-Iranian ancestry substantially explains the similarity.

# Article XIII — Ethical Screening

Distinguish descriptive material, historical behavior, temporary regulation, universal prescription, metaphor, polemic, and binding command.

A candidate may remain historically foundational while receiving limited normative authority.

Antiquity does not immunize a passage from criticism.

# Article XIV — Contradiction Screening

Compare each candidate against:
1. internally stronger material;
2. other FWC candidates;
3. historical evidence;
4. empirical evidence;
5. TCP contradiction corpus;
6. established ethical constraints.

Record contradictions rather than harmonize automatically.

# Article XV — No Tradition Quotas

FWC-A shall not contain material merely because a religion or region “needs representation.”

Evidence and foundational significance are the criteria.

# Article XVI — No Abrahamic Presumption

Founding interest in Abrahamic traditions gives no automatic epistemic priority.

Torah, Prophets, Jesus traditions, and Qur'an must pass the same methodological requirements as Gathas, early Buddhist material, Rigvedic hymns, Nanak bani, and other candidates.

# Article XVII — No Perennialist Presumption

Desire for universal religion shall not force incompatible traditions into one message.

If one teaches permanent self and another no permanent self, the contradiction remains until explained.

# Article XVIII — Initial Candidate Universe

The register establishes investigative priority only.

None is admitted merely by listing.

## FWC-CAND-001 — Torah / Pentateuchal Witness Strata

Scope: Genesis–Deuteronomy decomposed into recoverable literary units.

Potential units:
- ancient divine-name traditions;
- patriarchal encounter narratives;
- Exodus/Moses traditions;
- covenantal units;
- Decalogue traditions;
- law collections;
- Deuteronomic teaching;
- priestly theology;
- creation traditions.

Initial status: CAND-1.

Primary issue: recover early Israelite witness versus later synthesis.

## FWC-CAND-002 — Early Hebrew Prophetic Witness

Scope: recoverable material associated with Amos, Hosea, Micah, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, and the Twelve.

Books are not initially indivisible units.

Priority themes:
justice; covenant fidelity; critique of ritual without righteousness; sovereignty; repentance; vulnerable people; encounter; restoration.

Initial status: CAND-1.

## FWC-CAND-003 — Recovered Jesus Corpus

Scope: RJC already under construction; not automatically the four canonical Gospels as complete works.

Priority:
teachings; parables; prayer; ethics; Kingdom proclamation; symbolic acts; identity-related material.

Initial status: CAND-2.

Primary question: what can responsibly be received as foundational Jesus witness independent of later Christological development?

## FWC-CAND-004 — Qur'anic Proclamation

Scope: Qur'an first as early proclamation corpus associated with Muhammad's movement.

Separate dossiers should address Meccan/Medinan strata where reconstructable, variants, readings, legal material, theology, biblical intertexts, ethics, eschatology, and Muhammad's claimed revelatory role.

Initial status: CAND-2.

Primary question: which propositions possess sufficient historical, theological, and ethical weight for foundational reception independent of later hadith/fiqh?

## FWC-CAND-005 — Old Avestan Gathas

Scope: seventeen Old Avestan songs traditionally associated with Zarathustra.

Priority:
Ahura Mazda; asa/Truth; druj/Lie; moral choice; encounter; righteous action; judgment; benefactor/restorer language.

Initial status: CAND-2.

Primary issue: separate Gathic religious vision from later Avestan/Pahlavi theology.

## FWC-CAND-006 — Early Buddhist Teaching Corpus

Scope: teachings recoverable through comparison of early parallel Buddhist traditions, not simply the entire Pali Canon as verbatim Buddha speech.

Priority:
suffering; craving; ignorance; ethics; contemplation; awakening; compassion; impermanence; dependent arising; no-self.

Initial status: CAND-1.

Primary issue: earliest recoverable Dharma versus later school metaphysics.

## FWC-CAND-007 — Guru Nanak Bani

Scope: hymns with strong Nanak attribution, separate from later Janamsakhi biography.

Priority:
divine unity; Divine Name; hukam; truthful living; haumai; equality; critique of formalism; experiential knowledge of God.

Initial status: CAND-2.

## FWC-CAND-008 — Early Rigvedic Hymnic Witness

Scope: selected early Rigvedic hymns relevant to inspired perception, cosmic order, divine-human relation, and truth.

Initial status: CAND-1.

Primary issue: whether particular hymns provide foundational transcendent insight beyond historical importance.

## FWC-CAND-009 — Ancient Near Eastern Prophetic Control Corpus

Scope: Mari, Assyrian, and related prophetic material.

Initial status: CAND-1 / methodological control.

Purpose includes preventing the invalid inference that the presence of prophecy proves unique ownership of prophecy by one later religion.

# Article XIX — Secondary Candidate Universe

Open for nomination:
- early Jain teaching;
- Upanishadic strata;
- Daoist materials;
- early Confucian ethics;
- Greek philosophical/revelatory traditions;
- early Christian texts outside canonical Gospels;
- Jerusalem Jesus-movement material;
- Pauline material separately evaluated;
- James/Jude traditions;
- early Islamic non-Qur'anic material;
- selected Sufi witness;
- Jewish wisdom;
- Psalms/prayer traditions;
- later Christian mysticism;
- responsibly reconstructable Indigenous revelatory traditions;
- additional PRC/TCP cases.

# Article XX — Historical Controls

Evaluate some material not expected to be canonized: Mari prophetic letters, royal ideological inscriptions, known late pseudepigrapha, forged revelations, failed prophecy records, and well-documented charismatic movements.

A protocol tested only against favored candidates is not a real protocol.

# Article XXI — Admission Gates

1. Identifiability.
2. Recoverability.
3. Provenance.
4. Transmission.
5. Attribution.
6. Content Integrity.
7. Ethical Admissibility.
8. Evidentiary Coherence.
9. Contradiction Disclosure.
10. Comparative Test.
11. Adversarial Review.
12. Authority Necessity.

If a witness can perform its legitimate role adequately in EWC-B or RWC-C, FWC-A elevation should not occur merely for prestige.

# Article XXII — No Numerical Canon Score

Do not reduce candidate evaluation to percentages.

Use structured qualitative profiles instead.

Example:
Provenance High; Textual stability Very High; Founder proximity Moderate; Encounter proximity High; Corroboration Moderate; Ethical authority Very High; Metaphysical certainty Unresolved; Redaction risk Moderate; Independence High; Open questions Significant.

# Article XXIII — Foundational Core vs Foundational Commentary

FWC-A may eventually contain:
- FWC-A1 — Primary Foundational Witness.
- FWC-A2 — Foundational Interpretive Witness.

This prevents a false binary between fully canonical and merely optional.

# Article XXIV — No Automatic Harmonized Scripture

If multiple candidates enter FWC-A, they shall initially remain in their own voices.

The community shall not rewrite Moses, Amos, Zarathustra, Jesus, Muhammad, Buddha, Nanak, or others into one vocabulary or pretend they taught one metaphysical system.

Constructive theology remains visibly distinct from source witnesses.

# Article XXV — Anti-Ventriloquism Principle

Do not rewrite candidate concepts to anticipate the community's later theology.

Do not change Buddhist Dharma into God where the text does not mean God; translate Ahura Mazda automatically as YHWH; make Qur'anic tawhid secretly Nicene; project conciliar metaphysics directly into Jesus; reduce rta or asa to generic God; or make Nanak merely repeat Islamic or Hindu theology.

Comparison requires preserving difference.

# Article XXVI — Translation Discipline

Each candidate should eventually provide:
1. earliest-language text where available;
2. literal/philological translation;
3. readable study translation;
4. critical notes for disputed terms.

Key terms may remain transliterated where English equivalence would distort.

# Article XXVII — Founder's Name Does Not Determine Admission

A historically weak saying attributed to a revered prophet receives no exemption.

A historically strong insight from an obscure or anonymous source may receive serious consideration.

# Article XXVIII — Anonymous Witnesses

Anonymous texts may enter FWC-A.

Anonymity is not disqualifying, but it prevents unsupported claims about eyewitness status, prophetic office, founder attribution, or direct encounter.

# Article XXIX — Miraculous Claims

Miracle narratives neither automatically qualify nor automatically disqualify a witness.

Classify reported event, historical claim, phenomenology, theological interpretation, and metaphysical causal claim separately.

# Article XXX — Candidate-Claim Register

Every significant proposition should receive a unique identifier.

Example:
- FWC-JES-P0001 — God's reign is central to Jesus' proclamation.
- FWC-GAT-P0001 — humans participate morally in choice between asa and druj.
- FWC-QUR-P0001 — ultimate divine reality is one and without peer.
- FWC-BUD-P0001 — craving contributes causally to suffering.
- FWC-NAN-P0001 — self-centered haumai obstructs truthful alignment with hukam.

# Article XXXI — Contradiction Register Integration

Incompatible candidate propositions automatically link to the TCP contradiction corpus.

Admission of both witnesses does not silently resolve disagreement.

# Article XXXII — Foundational Admission Does Not Require Metaphysical Agreement

FWC-A may preserve witnesses with unresolved metaphysical disagreement.

This may be preferable to premature resolution.

# Article XXXIII — Canon Formation Sequence

1. Candidate universe.
2. Source decomposition.
3. Historical/source dossiers.
4. Founder/revelation attribution.
5. Proposition extraction.
6. Ethical evaluation.
7. Comparative independence analysis.
8. Contradiction mapping.
9. Adversarial review.
10. Authority-profile recommendation.
11. Cross-candidate comparison.
12. Only then foundational admission.

No candidate is admitted merely because reviewed first.

# Article XXXIV — Initial Review Order

Recommended alternating order:

1. Old Avestan Gathas.
2. Recovered Jesus Corpus.
3. Early Buddhist Corpus.
4. Qur'anic Proclamation.
5. Early Hebrew Prophetic Witness.
6. Nanak Bani.
7. Torah/Pentateuchal Strata.
8. Rigvedic Hymnic Witness.
9. Ancient Near Eastern Prophetic Control.

The order has no theological significance; it prevents one tradition from defining the entire review vocabulary in advance.

# Article XXXV — First Review Question

For every candidate ask:

1. What is the earliest responsibly recoverable witness here, before asking whether we agree?
2. What does it actually claim in its own conceptual world?
3. Only then: should this community receive it?

# Article XXXVI — Final Selection Principle

FWC-A shall not be a Bible with additions, Qur'an with appendices, presumed progressive-revelation canon, anthology of world religions, or founder-curated proof-text collection.

It shall be a critically assembled corpus of the strongest witnesses that survive disciplined historical, textual, ethical, comparative, and epistemological review and are judged necessary for the community's foundational orientation toward transcendent truth.

# Article XXXVII — Governing Maxim

Begin with every serious candidate open.
Privilege none by inheritance.
Reject none by foreignness.
Decompose composite texts.
Recover before theologizing.
Preserve native concepts before comparing them.
Distinguish textual stability from founder attribution.
Distinguish spiritual profundity from historical certainty.
Count independent convergence only where independence survives investigation.
Expose contradictions rather than harmonizing secretly.
Require stronger justification for foundational authority than spiritual usefulness.
Admit slowly.
Leave unresolved what cannot responsibly be resolved.
Let no canon be assembled until the candidates have first been allowed to speak in their own voices.
