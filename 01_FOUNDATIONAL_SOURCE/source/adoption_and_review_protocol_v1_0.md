# v1.0 Adoption & Review Protocol

## Purpose
The v1.0 documents are the first integrated founding synthesis of the Way. They should be treated as a pilot religious constitution and baseline, not as immutable revelation.

## Recommended sequence
1. Founder review for internal contradiction, accidental dogmatization, and conflict with the anti-coercion framework.
2. Independent governance/integrity review focused on conscience, exit, founder power, safeguarding, and civil/religious authority boundaries.
3. Theological/research review focused on whether confidence labels match the v0.8-v0.9 dossiers.
4. Legal reconciliation: ensure the religious Constitution does not purport to override Tennessee corporate charter/bylaws, fiduciary duties, employment law, safeguarding duties, tax law, or property ownership.
5. Pilot community use: Seeker Orientation, Companion pathway, prayer/study/service gatherings, and House-in-Formation operations.
6. Record questions, harms, confusion, and unexpected dependency patterns during the pilot.
7. Formal review after 90 days and again after one full annual cycle.

## Adoption rule
Adoption of v1.0 means: "This is the Assembly's current founding constitutional and teaching baseline, subject to its own revision rules." It must not be described as infallible, divinely dictated, or immune from correction.

## Hard gates before public institutional launch
- Final legal/public name cleared.
- Tennessee formation board meets legal requirements and the internal independence target is underway.
- Charter/bylaws reconciled with the religious Constitution.
- Independent complaint/safeguarding route operational.
- Financial controls and bookkeeping operational.
- Founder/executive compensation remains unapproved until a disinterested process exists.
- Children/vulnerable-person programs remain closed until safeguarding prerequisites are complete.

## Questions deliberately deferred beyond v1.0
- Comprehensive metaphysics of God/ultimate reality.
- Final Christology, Qur'anic revelation/finality, Buddhist rebirth/nirvana metaphysics.
- Closed canon or Way scripture.
- Final afterlife doctrine.
- Comprehensive marriage/family theology and marriage rite.
- Ordination theology.
- Comprehensive sacred calendar.
- Formal doctrine of dual religious belonging.
