# v1.2 Execution Sequence

1. Select and clear the legal/public name.
2. Recruit at least two additional directors; target four additional directors.
3. Identify a Secretary different from the President.
4. Complete conflict/independence disclosures.
5. Insert registered/principal office information privately into final filing copy.
6. Obtain final Tennessee nonprofit counsel review of Charter/Bylaws.
7. File Tennessee charter and retain accepted/stamped copy.
8. Complete incorporator action and initial Board organization.
9. Apply for EIN only after legal formation.
10. Open bank/accounting/payment infrastructure under Board resolution.
11. Activate independent complaint/safeguarding routes before public launch.
12. Finalize three-year financial assumptions and full Form 1023 application.
13. Board reviews and authorizes Form 1023 filing.
14. File electronically through Pay.gov.
15. Begin/continue controlled pilot and first-year compliance calendar.
