# Current Foundational Authoring

This directory is the forward-looking home for **new foundational religious/theological source work** created after the repository's second-pass curation.

It intentionally begins nearly empty.

## Why this directory exists

The historical `../source/` tree contains multiple generations of work in one place:

- pre-v1.0 research and architecture;
- the v0.8 Theological Research & Library of Witnesses pack;
- exact editable/source counterparts of the v1.0 release source;
- some v1.2 execution-source artifacts;
- unversioned numbered documents whose version relationships are not fully normalized.

Moving or renaming that material would damage provenance and break existing references.

Therefore:

> **The legacy `source/` tree is preserved in place as authoring provenance. New unrelated foundational drafting should begin here instead of adding more lifecycle generations to the mixed legacy tree. Active numbered/linked series already established under `source/` may remain there until migrated as a whole.**

## What belongs here

New source documents intended to become or revise:

- foundational religious synthesis;
- teaching/practice baselines;
- constitutional religious texts;
- epistemological/revelation/canon governance drafts;
- companion formation frameworks;
- other future foundational releases.

## Active-series exception

The EPC protocol series and FWSD dossier series are already established under `../source/`. Keep those series together there unless a dedicated migration moves the whole series and updates its references atomically.

## What does not belong here

- HTERP research work — use `../../02_RESEARCH_AND_EVIDENCE/`
- corporate/institutional instruments — use `../../04_INSTITUTION_AND_GOVERNANCE/institution/`
- integrity/safeguarding working packages — use `../../05_INTEGRITY_SAFEGUARDING_AND_COMPLIANCE/`
- immutable releases — use `../../03_RELEASES_AND_BASELINES/`
- informal notes or chat responses — use `../../90_WORKING_NOTES_AND_SCRIPTS/`

## Promotion model

New material should normally move through:

```text
current foundational source
        ↓
review / crosswalk / status decision
        ↓
release candidate
        ↓
immutable release snapshot
        ↓
if applicable, separately adopted institutional instrument
```

Creating a source document here does **not** make it adopted doctrine.
