# Production Readiness and Finalization Plan

**Project:** The Way / The Assembly religious-community project  
**Current legal-name candidate:** The Assembly of the Way, Inc. — reserved pending Tennessee verification and final affirmation  
**Status:** Working production-control baseline  
**Date:** 2026-09-22

## 1. Purpose

This plan governs the transition from a large body of exploratory drafts, prior-generation constitutional instruments, legal/governance documents, religious-life documents, operational records, and IRS-support materials into a controlled, internally consistent, reviewable, adoptable, and print-ready corpus.

The immediate objective is not to create more content. It is to determine what already exists, what is authoritative, what conflicts, what must be merged or superseded, what remains incomplete, and what can be promoted to a final production state.

## 2. Governing Principle

> Reconcile before polishing.  
> Approve before printing.  
> Preserve superseded material without allowing it to compete with the canonical corpus.

During the finalization program, new drafting should be limited to:
1. filling a documented gap;
2. resolving a documented conflict;
3. implementing an approved decision;
4. correcting legal, factual, theological, structural, or editorial defects; or
5. producing a controlled derivative such as a print master, adoption copy, or public edition.

## 3. Source and Authority Discipline

Repository placement alone does not establish authority.

The working hierarchy for review is:

1. filed, executed, adopted, or formally ratified instruments;
2. current designated canonical drafts;
3. current approved working documents;
4. predecessor or pre-integration drafts;
5. research, notes, chat archives, and exploratory material;
6. historical/superseded material retained for provenance.

No historical document is deleted merely because it is superseded.

## 4. Wave 0 — Production Control

The following six control artifacts are the first items to complete.

### PR-CTRL-001 — Master Document Register

One record for every substantive artifact, including:
- document ID;
- canonical title;
- repository path;
- document family;
- current version;
- status;
- authority tier;
- parent authority;
- current canonical / predecessor / duplicate / historical designation;
- approval or adoption requirement;
- legal-review requirement;
- theological-review requirement;
- privacy classification;
- print requirement;
- superseded-by / supersedes relationship;
- unresolved placeholders;
- known conflicts;
- next action.

### PR-TERM-001 — Canonical Terminology Register

Resolve and control terms including:
- The Way;
- Companion;
- Assembly;
- House of Prayer;
- Covenant Membership;
- statutory corporate membership;
- Founder;
- Presiding Spiritual Leader;
- Ecclesiastical Council;
- civil Board;
- community / local community;
- movement / tradition / institution;
- any remaining Confluence-era or Keren haYachad-era terms;
- names of rites, offices, bodies, programs, and document classes.

Every retired term should retain a historical mapping so older documents remain intelligible.

### PR-AUTH-001 — Constitutional Authority Map

Map:
- root/foundational instruments;
- civil corporate instruments;
- ecclesiastical instruments;
- doctrinal/teaching authority;
- policies;
- operational standards;
- local-community instruments;
- forms and records;
- evidentiary records;
- research/advisory materials.

The map must identify which instrument controls when two documents conflict.

### PR-XWALK-001 — Supersession and Crosswalk Register

Map predecessor architectures to the emerging canonical architecture, including:
- FOUND-001–015;

- REL-CON-001;

### PR-DEC-001 — Open Decisions Register

Each unresolved matter receives:

- alternatives already considered;
- current recommendation, if any;

### PR-STYLE-001 — Editorial and Production Standard

- document IDs;

- authority labels;
- headers/footers;

## 10. Document Promotion States

Use the following production states:

1. **INVENTORIED** — located and identified.
2. **CLASSIFIED** — authority/family/status determined.
3. **RECONCILING** — conflicts and dependencies under review.
4. **CONTENT COMPLETE** — substance complete, no known substantive gaps.
5. **REVIEW READY** — ready for legal/theological/governance review as applicable.
6. **APPROVED** — content formally approved for adoption or publication.
7. **ADOPTED / EXECUTED** — formally effective where adoption or execution is required.
8. **PRINT MASTER** — typeset, proofed, pagination locked, signatures/placeholders handled.
9. **PUBLISHED / ISSUED** — controlled release.
10. **SUPERSEDED** — retained permanently for provenance but no longer operative.

“Final” should never mean merely “looks finished.”

## 11. Print-Readiness Gate

A document cannot receive PRINT MASTER status until:

- canonical title and ID are correct;
- legal/public/religious names are consistent;
- version and status are correct;
- authority and parent authority are stated;
- all placeholders are resolved or intentionally retained;
- defined terms match PR-TERM-001;
- cross-references are validated;
- conflicts identified in PR-XWALK-001 are resolved;
- required legal/theological/governance review is complete;
- adoption language is correct;
- signature and certification blocks are correct;
- tables of contents and page references are updated;
- headers, footers, numbering, typography, and margins conform to PR-STYLE-001;
- source file is preserved;
- archival PDF is produced;
- superseded predecessor is archived and cross-linked;
- the Master Document Register is updated.

But it should become final only after Wave 0 establishes the terminology, authority, and supersession controls needed to reconcile its earlier identity and institutional assumptions with the current Way / Assembly architecture.

This makes FOUND-001 the first substantive destination, while the six Wave 0 control artifacts are the first actual work.
