# PR-AUTH-001 — Constitutional Authority Map

**Status:** Reconciliation authority baseline  
**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Authority of this artifact:** Corpus-control / reconciliation only. It does **not** itself adopt, amend, execute, file, or make operative any religious or civil governing instrument.  
**Data file:** `PR-AUTH-001-CONSTITUTIONAL-AUTHORITY-MAP.csv`

## Purpose

The Way / Assembly corpus now contains several generations of religious constitutions, civil bylaws, ecclesiastical governance instruments, membership and ministry frameworks, subject-matter codes, released baselines, and historical predecessor documents. PR-AUTH-001 answers a narrow but essential question:

> **When two documents, bodies, or offices address the same subject, which one is supposed to control, and where does that authority stop?**

The map contains **38 jurisdiction entries**.

## 1. The governing model is a dual-lane constitution

The corpus does not support one unlimited institutional sovereign. It supports two coordinated lanes.

### Ecclesiastical lane

```text
REL-CON-001 — Foundational Religious Constitution
                    ↓
ECC-CON-001 / CH-006 — Ecclesiastical Constitution
                    ↓
Foundational Principles / Formal Doctrine
                    ↓
Institutional Charters
                    ↓
Constitutional / Subject Codes and Frameworks
                    ↓
Policies
                    ↓
Administrative Rules
                    ↓
Individual Decisions
```

REL-CON-001 itself states that a lower-order ecclesiastical instrument is invalid to the extent it irreconcilably contradicts controlling higher authority.

### Civil/corporate lane

```text
Applicable law
      ↓
Filed Charter / Articles (when they actually exist)
      ↓
Civil Bylaws / BYL-001 (when validly adopted)
      ↓
Board-approved policies / delegations
      ↓
Corporate resolutions, contracts, and administrative acts
```

This lane governs civil corporate powers such as the Board, officers, corporate actions, fiduciary administration, contracts, title, banking, and property acts.

**Current factual caution:** the repository does not yet evidence a filed charter, final legal name, EIN, or completed organizational adoption. The chart above is therefore the intended constitutional architecture, not a claim that those instruments are presently operative.

## 2. Neither lane absorbs the other

The core integration rule is:

> **Ecclesiastical authority decides religious questions within its jurisdiction; civil fiduciary authority decides whether and how corporate assets and legal powers may lawfully be exercised.**

Accordingly:

- Board control of money does not create doctrinal authority.
- Religious approval does not compel an unlawful or imprudent corporate expenditure.
- A ministry credential does not authorize contracts, debt, property transfer, or spending.
- Covenant Membership does not create statutory corporate membership.
- The CMA is not the Board.
- The Ecclesiastical Council is not the Board.
- Founder or PSL status does not create corporate agency by implication.

## 3. Concurrent jurisdiction is a real third category

Some matters genuinely require both lanes.

The clearest existing example is designated sacred/principal religious property. Current drafts contemplate:

1. Civil Board approval for title, fiduciary, financing, restrictions, and legal consequences;
2. Ecclesiastical Council approval for the religious purpose/significance;
3. CMA concurrence if the superior constitutional system makes that a reserved power;
4. compliance with donor, trust, and other lawful restrictions.

Where both approvals are required, one body's approval does not erase the other's jurisdiction.

This should become the model used by **JUR-001** for all cross-domain questions.

## 4. Actor authority map

### Founder

**Founder is not an authority source by itself.**

A founder can possess authority only because another valid instrument grants the founder a particular office or reserved power. Founder status alone does not confer:

- ownership;
- doctrinal infallibility;
- ministry credential;
- treasury control;
- immunity;
- power to manufacture the CMA electorate;
- corporate agency;
- or a succession right.

This is a major reconciliation point because earlier founder-control design exercises conflict with the later v1.0 and anti-capture architecture. PR-XWALK-001 must identify those variants explicitly rather than blending them.

### Presiding Spiritual Leader (PSL)

PSL is a provisional office title. The office may exercise only powers granted by REL-CON/ECC-CON and subordinate valid instruments. The PSL may not stand above the constitution, waive CMA reserved powers, or convert spiritual leadership into corporate authority.

### Ecclesiastical Council (EC)

The EC is the principal conciliar ecclesiastical governance body in the later architecture. Its ordinary jurisdiction remains subject to:

- REL-CON-001;
- ECC-CON-001;
- valid CMA reserved powers;
- CCG constitutional review;
- EAC/accountability jurisdiction;
- civil boundaries where a matter requires corporate action.

MIN-001 expressly places ultimate ecclesiastical authority over ministry credentials in the EC rather than in the PSL acting alone.

### Covenant Member Assembly (CMA)

The CMA holds **participatory and reserved powers**, not general supremacy.

It may not:
- seize ordinary EC jurisdiction merely because a majority disagrees;
- waive higher constitutional requirements;
- expand its own reserved powers through an ordinary vote;
- become the Civil Board by implication.

Where a CMA reserved decision also requires corporate action, both the CMA process and the separate lawful Board process remain necessary.

### Council of Constitutional Guardians (CCG)

The intended role is constitutional/jurisdictional review: vote validity, reserved-power boundaries, electorate integrity, notice, anti-packing, and similar questions.

**Corpus gap:** the canonical `CONST-GUARD-001` directory contains no substantive instrument. The CCG therefore appears throughout other drafts before its own charter/code has been fully migrated or completed.

### Ecclesiastical Accountability Council (EAC)

The intended role is independent ecclesiastical accountability for serious misconduct and related findings.

**Corpus gap:** the `ACCOUNT-001` directory likewise lacks its own substantive instrument in the canonical tree. EAC authority currently appears embedded in other documents.

### Civil Board

The Board is the civil fiduciary body. It controls civil corporate matters within the eventual charter/bylaws and applicable law, but does not gain doctrine, worship, ministry-credential, or other ecclesiastical jurisdiction merely because it controls corporate resources.

## 5. Critical hierarchy rule for doctrine

REL-CON-001 currently places:

```text
REL-CON-001
   ↓
ECC-CON-001
   ↓
Foundational Principles / Formal Doctrine
```

This means ordinary doctrine cannot override the two superior constitutional layers.

However, REL-CON-001 also expressly says the **exact relationship between Formal Doctrine and specific constitutional provisions requires further definition**. That remains an open integration issue for ECC-CON-001 and DOC-001.

Therefore CH-007/CH-008 should **not** be finalized before this relationship is resolved.

## 6. Release v1.0 versus REL-CON-001

This is now one of the most important crosswalk issues.

The immutable v1.0 release calls the **Constitution of the Way v1.0** the first coherent religious constitutional baseline for pilot/community use, and the v1.1 future-board resolution contemplates recognizing it as the religious constitution.

Later, the canonical institutional tree contains **REL-CON-001**, which identifies itself as the **Supreme Ecclesiastical Constitutional Instrument** and establishes a substantially more detailed constitutional hierarchy.

These facts cannot simply be merged by assumption.

For finalization purposes:

- **release_v1.0 remains an immutable founding religious synthesis and source baseline**;
- **REL-CON-001 is the current candidate root ecclesiastical constitution in the canonical institutional architecture**;
- PR-XWALK-001 must determine whether REL-CON-001 **supersedes, restates, incorporates, preserves, or partly replaces** the Constitution of the Way v1.0.

Until that crosswalk is complete, neither should be silently edited to pretend the relationship is already settled.

## 7. FOUND-001–015

The FOUND-series is classified as **pre-integration source authority only**.

It can contribute language, principles, mechanisms, and design intent, but its provisions do not outrank or automatically amend the later REL/ECC/BYL architecture merely because they were drafted earlier or labeled constitutional.

The proper process is:

```text
FOUND provision
      ↓
identify subject and intent
      ↓
compare release_v1.0
      ↓
compare REL-CON / ECC-CON / BYL / subject code
      ↓
choose preserve / revise / split / reject / supersede
      ↓
record in PR-XWALK-001
```

## 8. A real drafting defect identified during this review

MEM-001 presently states, in present-tense language, that:

> the corporation “has no statutory members under its Charter.”

But the repository controls simultaneously show that no filed charter/current corporation is yet evidenced.

The **design decision** may indeed be a nonmember corporation, but the sentence is presently stronger than the evidence.

During final reconciliation it should be converted to conditional/future language until formation actually occurs, for example:

> “The Corporation shall be organized without statutory members unless the filed Charter expressly provides otherwise.”

After actual filing, it may be updated to a factual statement supported by the charter.

The same evidence discipline should be applied throughout all pre-populated governance drafts.

## 10. Production rule generated by this map

No document may be finalized merely by polishing its prose.

Before promotion to `CONTENT COMPLETE`, each governing document must answer:

1. **What superior instrument authorizes it?**
2. **What subject/domain does it govern?**
3. **Who is the competent actor?**
4. **What concurrence is required?**
5. **What is explicitly outside its jurisdiction?**
6. **What higher instrument defeats it in conflict?**
7. **What evidence would be required before calling it adopted/executed/filed?**

If those answers cannot be stated, the document remains **RECONCILING**.
