# PR-TERM-001 — Canonical Terminology Register

**Status:** Production-control baseline for reconciliation  
**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Authority:** Editorial/corpus control only; this register does not itself adopt religious doctrine, legal names, offices, or governance powers.  
**Data file:** `PR-TERM-001-CANONICAL-TERMINOLOGY-REGISTER.csv`

## Purpose

PR-TERM-001 establishes one controlled vocabulary for the finalization program so that older and newer document generations do not silently redefine the same people, institutions, offices, membership categories, or authority concepts.

The register currently contains **89 controlled terms**.

## Core identity architecture

The production baseline is:

> **The Way → Companion(s) → The Assembly → House(s) of Prayer**

These terms answer different questions:

- **The Way** — What is the religious path/tradition?
- **Companion** — What is a person who freely walks that path with others called?
- **The Assembly** — What is the broader gathered religious community called?
- **House of Prayer** — What is a stable local expression of that community called?

**The Assembly of the Way** is retained as the present **working public compound name**, including in current pilot materials. It is not, by that usage alone, proof of a legal corporation or final legal name.

## Critical distinctions

### 1. The Way is not the corporation

The Way is the religious path/tradition. The Corporation is the civil legal entity that may serve it.

### 2. The Assembly is not the Board

The Assembly is the broader religious community. The Board of Directors is the civil fiduciary governing body of the corporation.

### 3. The Assembly is not the Covenant Member Assembly

**The Assembly** includes the broader religious community of Companions and welcome participants.

**The Covenant Member Assembly (CMA)** is a specific formal ecclesiastical governance body composed of eligible Covenant Members.

No governing document should abbreviate the CMA as simply “the Assembly.”

### 4. Companion is not the same status as Covenant Member

For reconciliation drafting:

- **Companion** is the native religious identity.
- **Covenant Member** is formal ecclesiastical membership under MEM-001.
- **statutory corporate member** is a separate civil-law category.

The working crosswalk is that Covenant Membership is a more formal status within the religious community and should ordinarily be treated as a subset of the broader Companion identity, while not every Companion need be a Covenant Member. The exact eligibility relationship remains a controlled issue for MEM-001 reconciliation.

### 5. House of Prayer is not necessarily a building

A House of Prayer is first the stable local community. It may gather in borrowed, rented, public, outdoor, residential, or owned space.

When a governing or property document means the physical location, prefer **House of Prayer site**, **facility**, **meeting place**, or the specific property name.

### 6. Church and congregation are external/descriptive categories

The project does not need to use “church” as its native identity simply because federal tax law uses a church classification.

Use:
- **House of Prayer** for the native local religious community;
- **congregation** where external/legal/factual language calls for it;
- **church** where tax, legal, comparative, or historical usage requires the category.

### 7. Founder is a historical relation, not a self-executing office

“Founder” identifies origin. It does not, from the word alone, create civil, ecclesiastical, doctrinal, ministerial, financial, succession, or disciplinary powers.

Any founder-era reserved power must be found in an actual governing instrument and mapped in PR-AUTH-001.

### 8. Presiding Spiritual Leader remains provisional as a title

Later governance documents use **Presiding Spiritual Leader (PSL)**, but REL-CON-001 itself identifies the final title of that office as unresolved.

Therefore the office may be referenced during reconciliation, but the title is not yet treated as permanently settled.

### 9. “Religious Body” should be phased out as ordinary prose

Some later constitutional drafts use **Religious Body** extensively. For final drafting, prefer **The Assembly** or **religious community** unless “Religious Body” performs a precise defined technical function.

PR-AUTH-001 and PR-XWALK-001 will determine whether it remains a formally defined constitutional term.

### 10. Canon must not be confused with repository control

- **Library of Witnesses** = broad source collection.
- **HTERP proto-canon / research source-priority architecture** = research/constructive-theology reading priority.
- **binding doctrine** = actually adopted doctrine through the defined process.
- **canonical institutional repository/path** = repository-control language, not scripture.
- **Canon** = reserved for an explicitly constituted authoritative textual collection if one is ever adopted.

## Historical names

**Keren haYachad** is classified as a historical predecessor identity associated with the earlier FOUND-series.

**The Confluence / Confluence** is classified as an earlier naming candidate.

They remain discoverable in provenance and crosswalk records but should not appear as current proper names in newly reconciled governing documents unless deliberately restored.

## Legal-name control

The current institutional constant remains:

> `ORG.LEGAL_NAME = [[CORP_NAME]]`

The repository also uses **The Assembly of the Way** as a current working name in pilot materials.

Accordingly:

- **The Assembly of the Way** = working public/community name;
- **The Assembly of the Way, Inc.** = reserved legal-name candidate only;
- **[[CORP_NAME]]** = required legal drafting placeholder until the legal name is verified and authorized.

No production-final charter, bylaws, EIN record, bank resolution, tax application, or executed governance instrument should guess this value.

## Drafting enforcement rules

1. Do not use **member** unqualified in governing/legal documents.
2. Do not use **Assembly** to mean the Covenant Member Assembly.
3. Do not use **The Way** to mean the corporation.
4. Do not use **House of Prayer** to imply ownership of real estate.
5. Do not use **church** to imply recognized federal church status.
6. Do not use **Founder** as though the word itself grants authority.
7. Spell out council/office names before using EC, CCG, EAC, CMA, or PSL.
8. Use **revelation claim** where the evidentiary status of an experience is at issue.
9. Do not shorten HTERP's research source-priority architecture to **the canon**.
10. Do not call a document **adopted, executed, filed, or final** without the evidence/lifecycle state supporting that word.

## Status meanings in this register

The terminology statuses are drafting controls, not ecclesiastical adoption votes.

- **CANONICAL_DRAFTING** — default term for new reconciliation drafts.
- **TECHNICAL_CANONICAL** — controlled technical term in the current institutional architecture.
- **WORKING_PUBLIC_NAME** — current public working name, not necessarily legal name.
- **PROVISIONAL / CANDIDATE / RESERVED** — intentionally unsettled.
- **EXTERNAL_LEGAL / EXTERNAL_REGULATORY** — useful for law, tax, institutions, or outside audiences rather than native religious identity.
- **HISTORICAL / LEGACY** — retained for provenance, not normal new drafting.
- **AVOID** — should not be used as an organizational category/title in current drafting.
- **EVIDENCE_BEARING_STATUS** — may be used only when the relevant evidence exists.
