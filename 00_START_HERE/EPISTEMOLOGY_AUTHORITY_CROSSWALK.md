## Common core already aligned across the corpus

The reviewed documents substantially agree on the following:

1. **Reality/truth outranks institutional declaration.**
2. **A claimed encounter and the interpretation of that encounter are distinct.**
3. **Sincerity, intensity, charisma, altered state, office, or claimed divine source do not self-authenticate revelation.**
4. **Human perception, memory, transmission, and interpretation are fallible.**
5. **Confidence should be stated and uncertainty preserved.**
6. **Contemporary revelation cannot bypass ordinary safeguarding, consent, accountability, or governance.**
7. **Historical-critical and empirical evidence may be used; evidence is not suppressed to preserve authority.**
8. **Research or constructive-theology priority does not automatically equal binding doctrine.**
9. **No closed canon is presently established by the reviewed foundational-source posture.**
10. **The HTERP proto-canon is best read as a governed source-priority/reader architecture unless a separate adopted instrument gives it stronger status.**

## Real unresolved governance questions

### AQ-001 — Where should the authoritative epistemic constitution live?

The rules are distributed across foundational source, HTERP constructive work, and institutional integrity policy.

A future consolidation should distinguish:

- research methodology;
- religious/doctrinal epistemology;
- institutional decision procedure;
- contemporary revelation discernment;
- canon/source-library governance.

### AQ-002 — What is the formal status of the “Abrahamic Prophetic Spine”?

HTERP currently treats it as the chosen constructive center.

The foundational source does not yet establish a closed canon.

A future adopted instrument should say whether the spine is:

- identity-defining study priority;
- liturgical source priority;
- doctrinal source priority;
- foundational witness corpus;
- or some combination.

### AQ-003 — “Proto-canon” terminology

The term can be mistaken for “canon already selected.”

Until formally resolved, repository guidance should use:

> **HTERP proto-canon / research source-priority architecture**

rather than simply “the canon.”

### AQ-004 — Comprehensive EPC material

The existing repository already contains the core principles of a fuller epistemological constitution, but not one clearly consolidated and adopted EPC document spanning:

- definitions;
- revelation discernment;
- canon admission/status;
- evidence classes;
- authority limits;
- revision procedure.

A future drafting pass can consolidate these materials without pretending the result was previously adopted.

## Curation rule

Do not resolve these questions by deleting the older documents.
