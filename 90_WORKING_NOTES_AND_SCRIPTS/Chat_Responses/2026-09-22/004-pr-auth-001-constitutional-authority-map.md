# PR-AUTH-001 Constitutional Authority Map

**Date:** 2026-09-22

Completed the constitutional authority reconciliation baseline.

The corpus is now treated as a **dual-lane constitutional system**:

- ecclesiastical: REL-CON-001 → ECC-CON-001 → foundational principles/formal doctrine → charters → codes/frameworks → policies → rules → decisions;
- civil: applicable law → filed charter → civil bylaws → Board policies/delegations → corporate acts.

Neither lane automatically absorbs the other. Expressly concurrent matters require each competent authority to act in its own domain.

Important findings:
- Founder status is not itself an authority source.
- PSL remains constitutionally subordinate and its title is provisional.
- EC has principal conciliar ecclesiastical governance, while CMA holds defined participatory/reserved powers rather than general supremacy.
- CCG and EAC/accountability authority is referenced throughout the corpus but their standalone constitutional instruments remain missing from the canonical tree.
- release_v1.0's Constitution of the Way and later REL-CON-001 need an explicit supersession/incorporation crosswalk.
- MEM-001 contains a present-tense assertion that the corporation has no statutory members under its Charter even though the repository does not yet evidence a filed charter; this must be corrected during finalization.
