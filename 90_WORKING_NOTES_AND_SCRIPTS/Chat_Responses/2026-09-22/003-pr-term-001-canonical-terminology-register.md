# PR-TERM-001 Canonical Terminology Register

**Date:** 2026-09-22

Completed the canonical terminology reconciliation baseline.

The principal identity sequence is:

**The Way → Companion(s) → The Assembly → House(s) of Prayer**

Key controlled distinctions:
- The Way is the tradition/path, not the corporation.
- The Assembly is the broad religious community, not the Board and not the Covenant Member Assembly.
- Companion is religious identity; Covenant Member is formal ecclesiastical membership; statutory corporate member is a distinct civil-law category.
- House of Prayer is first a local community, not necessarily a building or separate legal entity.
- “church” and “congregation” remain available as external/legal/regulatory terms without displacing native vocabulary.
- Founder is a historical relation, not a self-executing office.
- PSL remains a provisional office title.
- Religious Body is marked for reconciliation/phase-out in ordinary prose.
- Keren haYachad and Confluence are preserved as historical naming layers, not current drafting terms.
- The Assembly of the Way is the current working public compound name; the legal corporate name remains unresolved until verified.
