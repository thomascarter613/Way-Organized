# FWSD-005 — Early Hebrew Prophetic Witness

**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Status:** CAND-3 — Evaluated; not canonically admitted  
**Authoritative dossier:** `../../../../01_FOUNDATIONAL_SOURCE/source/foundational-witness-dossiers/FWSD-005-early-hebrew-prophetic-witness.md`

The fifth Foundational Witness Selection dossier evaluates source-controlled early Hebrew prophetic witness rather than treating the final prophetic books as verbatim transcripts of individual prophets.

Key findings:

- prophecy belongs to a broader ancient Near Eastern/Eastern Mediterranean phenomenon and is not uniquely Israelite merely by existing;
- Amos remains a strong early writing-prophet anchor;
- Hosea, Micah, Isaiah ben Amoz, Jeremiah, and Ezekiel retain varying historical recoverability;
- Isaiah must be stratified, especially distinguishing Isaiah ben Amoz from exilic Isaiah 40–55;
- Jeremiah's Masoretic and Alexandrian forms preserve two materially different ancient editions;
- the strongest ethical core links worship with justice, condemns elite exploitation, and subjects rulers and sacred institutions to moral judgment;
- violent judgment, collective punishment, national polemic, and sexualized metaphors require explicit adverse-evidence treatment;
- the candidate remains CAND-3 with a strong recommendation for CAND-4 only after first-cycle cross-candidate comparison.
