## Substantive result

The Early Hebrew Prophetic Witness remains **CAND-3 — Evaluated** and is a serious future **CAND-4 — Foundational Candidate**, but it is **not canonically admitted**. Any FWC-A decision is deferred until the first cross-candidate comparison cycle is complete.

The dossier evaluates recoverable prophetic units and strata rather than treating the books of Amos, Hosea, Micah, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, and the Twelve as stenographic transcripts of single historical prophets.

The strongest recoverable core includes:

- prophetic encounter and commission;
- justice and social accountability;
- worship that cannot substitute for ethical fidelity;
- leadership accountability;
- protection of vulnerable people;
- false-prophecy awareness;
- election/covenant without immunity from judgment;
- repentance and restoration;
- expanding divine sovereignty;
- inner transformation/new-covenant themes;
- and the historical development of prophetic tradition itself.

The dossier explicitly distinguishes historical Isaiah from later Isaianic strata, records Jeremiah's major Masoretic/Greek textual plurality, preserves Ezekiel's exilic visionary setting without treating vividness as proof of metaphysical causation, and treats the Twelve as both individual compositions and an edited anthology.

No composite truth score was assigned.
