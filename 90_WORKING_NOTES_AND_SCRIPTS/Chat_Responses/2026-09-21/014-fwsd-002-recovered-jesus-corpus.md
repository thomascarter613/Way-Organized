## Substantive result

The RJC remains **CAND-3 — Evaluated** and is a serious future **CAND-4 — Foundational Candidate**, but it is **not canonically admitted**. Any FWC-A decision is deferred until the first cross-candidate comparison cycle is complete.

The dossier deliberately evaluates the earliest recoverable Jesus witness rather than the New Testament or four Gospels en bloc. It distinguishes historical Jesus material, earliest post-Easter belief, Gospel-level theological framing, and later doctrinal development.

The strongest recoverable anchors include baptism by John; Kingdom/Reign of God proclamation; repentance and Israel restoration; parabolic teaching; healing/exorcism activity; disciples and the Twelve; a core Lord's Prayer tradition; restrictive divorce teaching; final meal tradition; enemy-love/non-retaliation; Torah/Jewish interpretive context; Temple action; crucifixion under Pontius Pilate; and very early resurrection/vindication belief among followers.

The dossier gives materially lower historical-wording confidence to Johannine long discourses, explicit Logos self-identification, pre-existence sayings as historical Jesus speech, Nicene/Chalcedonian ontology as direct founder teaching, and later ecclesiastical or sacramental systematization.

It also separates the historical fact of **early resurrection belief** from the historical question of **what metaphysically caused the reported experiences**.

No composite truth score was assigned.
