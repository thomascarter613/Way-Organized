# FWSD-006 — Guru Nanak Bani Foundational Witness Selection Dossier

**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Status:** CAND-3 — Evaluated; not canonically admitted  
**Authoritative dossier:** `../../../../01_FOUNDATIONAL_SOURCE/source/foundational-witness-dossiers/FWSD-006-guru-nanak-bani.md`

FWSD-006 evaluates source-controlled Guru Nanak Bani rather than Sikhism as a whole.

Key findings:

- Guru Nanak is historically secure and the bani transmission is comparatively close to the founder;
- manuscript scholarship supports early oral and written transmission before the 1604 Adi Granth;
- the source freeze distinguishes historical Nanak, early bani, pothi traditions, the 1604 Adi Granth, and later canonical/reception layers;
- Janamsakhi biography is preserved as reception evidence but does not automatically control founder reconstruction;
- central source concepts include Ik Oankar/Oneness, hukam, haumai, nam, sabad, grace, truthful living, and householder-compatible spiritual transformation;
- Sikh vocabulary must not be flattened into imported English/Abrahamic/Advaita categories;
- rebirth, Divine provenance, and the precise ontology of Oneness remain metaphysically unresolved;
- equality ideals are distinguished from later caste and gender practice;
- the candidate remains CAND-3 with a strong recommendation for CAND-4 only after first-cycle comparison.
