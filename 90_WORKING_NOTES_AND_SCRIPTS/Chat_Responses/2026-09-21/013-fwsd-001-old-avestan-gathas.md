## Substantive result

The Old Avestan Gāthās remain **CAND-3 — Evaluated** and are a serious future **CAND-4 — Foundational Candidate**, but they are **not canonically admitted**. Any FWC-A decision is deferred until the first cross-candidate comparison cycle is complete.

The dossier source-freezes the candidate to the five Gāthās / seventeen Old Avestan hymnic chapters and explicitly excludes Yasna Haptaŋhāiti, Younger Avestan material, later Pahlavi theology, mature Ahriman/Ohrmazd systematization, and developed Saošyant doctrine.

The strongest positive factors are antiquity, recoverable Old Avestan identity, direct orientation toward Ahura Mazdā, the centrality of aša/truth, moral choice, thought-speech-action responsibility, judgment, and substantial independence from Abrahamic witness traditions.

The principal limitations are uncertain absolute dating and geography, disputed single-author Zarathustrian attribution, long oral transmission before written fixation, philological difficulty, late surviving manuscripts, and a substantial risk of projecting later Zoroastrian doctrine backward into the Gāthās.

No composite truth score was assigned.
