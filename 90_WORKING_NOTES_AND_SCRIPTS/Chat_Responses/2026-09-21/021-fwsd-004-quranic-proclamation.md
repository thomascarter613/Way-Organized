FWSD-004 has been completed under EPC-004.

## Substantive result

The Qurʾanic Proclamation remains **CAND-3 — Evaluated** and is a serious future **CAND-4 — Foundational Candidate**, but it is **not canonically admitted**. Any FWC-A decision is deferred until the first cross-candidate comparison cycle is complete.

The dossier source-freezes the candidate to the Qurʾanic proclamation and its earliest recoverable textual history rather than later Islam en bloc. It distinguishes the dominant ʿUthmānic consonantal text, early manuscripts, the Ṣanʿāʾ palimpsest lower text, regional codex evidence, qirāʾāt, and internal chronological/literary strata from later sīra, ḥadīth, tafsīr, kalām, and fiqh.

The strongest recoverable core includes:

- one sovereign creator God;
- creation as divine sign;
- claimed divine communication/revelation;
- Muḥammad as messenger/warner/proclaimer;
- resurrection and judgment;
- moral accountability;
- mercy and repentance;
- charity and care for vulnerable people;
- justice and truthful dealing;
- prayer/remembrance;
- anti-idolatry;
- continuity and dispute with earlier prophetic traditions;
- Qurʾanic Jesus as Messiah and messenger but not God;
- and historical community formation from Meccan proclamation to Medinan regulation.

Textually, the dossier records both the strong evidence for a very early written archetype behind the dominant ʿUthmānic text type and the equally important evidence that some early textual plurality existed, especially in the Ṣanʿāʾ palimpsest lower text.

No composite truth score was assigned.
