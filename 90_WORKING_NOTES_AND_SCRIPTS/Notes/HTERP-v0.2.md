The calibration changed the project in useful ways. Mari demonstrates that recognizable prophecy was an actual ancient institution documented outside the Bible, but its records give us much better evidence for prophetic **performance** than for the prophet's subjective experience. ([OUP Academic][1])

For Jesus, the dossier separates the strong historical case that he was baptized by John from the considerably harder question of whether Mark's reported baptismal vision reproduces Jesus' own experience exactly. The baptism itself is treated by scholars such as Joel Marcus as unusually secure historically, while the kingdom of God remains central to reconstruction of Jesus' proclamation. ([Cambridge University Press][2])

For Muhammad, the source model is now explicitly bifurcated. The Qur'an is an early proclamation corpus supported by very early manuscript evidence, while the detailed first-revelation story belongs to a separately transmitted biographical tradition whose recensions can be critically reconstructed. Görke and Schoeler's recent work argues that substantial material can be traced to the early ʿUrwa corpus, while also documenting variation in the first-revelation narrative. ([University of Birmingham][3])

The strongest methodological result comes from the controls. Controlled psilocybin research can reproducibly produce unity, sacred/noetic certainty, positive mood, altered time/space, and ineffability; neurological cases can produce sensed presence, voices, ecstatic certainty, and intensely religious interpretations. ([PubMed Central (PMC)][4]) This means those phenomenological features **cannot by themselves distinguish revelation from neurocognitive generation**.

But that does **not** establish that transcendence is unreal. It establishes a higher evidentiary threshold.

So the hypothesis ledger now reads approximately:

**H0 — construction/fraud:** demonstrably explains some cases.
**H1 — neurocognitive mechanisms:** demonstrably explains or mediates some cases.
**H2 — cultural shaping:** strongly and pervasively supported.
**H3 — impersonal transcendent reality:** still open, currently underdetermined.
**H4 — personal noncorporeal intelligence(s):** still open, currently underdetermined.
**H5 — mixed causal model:** required as the operational research position.

The decisive shift is therefore from asking:

> “Was this experience powerful, transformative, ancient, or sincerely believed?”

to asking:

> **“Does this case contain evidence that differentiates transcendence from known cognitive, cultural, informational, literary, and fraudulent alternatives?”**

That is why HTERP-AI and HTERP-PRED now impose harsh standards on purported inaccessible information and prophecy: prospective documentation, immutable timestamps, leakage audits, predefined failure conditions, blind adjudication, base-rate comparison, and retention of misses.
