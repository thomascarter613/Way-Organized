A few important substantive choices are now locked in. The credential hierarchy is **aspirant → candidate → authorized religious worker → commissioned minister → licensed minister → ordained minister**, with specialty endorsements layered separately. Office, credential, employment, corporate authority, and spiritual worth are explicitly distinguished. Founder status does not equal ordination, credentials cannot be purchased, and ordination requires an auditable progression through formation, supervised ministry, examination, safeguarding/character review, conciliar approval, rite, and registry entry.

The ordination track now has a **720-hour internal formation model**: 360 hours of prescribed coursework, 80 hours of personal/spiritual formation, 220 hours of supervised ministry practicum, and 60 hours of integrative assessment. That is our institutional rigor standard, not a statutory IRS minimum.

The workbook gives us live registers for candidates, the CH-026 course catalog and completion ledger, practicum hours, examinations, credential standing, minister personnel, religious literature, adult/youth education programs, actual education sessions, and continuing ministerial formation. It begins with zero actual candidates, credentials, completions, or program sessions rather than fabricating operational history.
