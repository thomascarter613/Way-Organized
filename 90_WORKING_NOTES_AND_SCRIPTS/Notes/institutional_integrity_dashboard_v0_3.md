The package deliberately does **not** manufacture facts that only the organization can supply. Tennessee requires the charter itself to say whether the corporation **will or will not have members**, so that remains an explicit filing gate. Tennessee law also distinguishes the organizational process depending on whether the initial directors are named in the charter, and permits appropriate written organizational/board action under specified consent requirements. ([Justia Law][1])

I also preserved a separate federal classification gate. IRS church status is a facts-and-circumstances determination; if the organization seeks recognition specifically as a church, Schedule A accompanies Form 1023, and an applicant seeking recognition as a church is not eligible to use Form 1023-EZ. The binder therefore gathers evidence rather than encouraging us to manufacture “church characteristics” prematurely. ([IRS][2]) The current compliance notes also reflect FinCEN's August 2026 final rule exempting domestic U.S. companies from BOI reporting, while requiring that status to be rechecked if law or guidance changes. ([Financial Crimes Enforcement Network][3])
