## Day One production batch is ready

I worked through the Day One execution queue and converted it into an operational artifact set. The legal structure is built around the current Tennessee nonprofit/religious-corporation framework and the federal §501(c)(3) organizational requirements. Tennessee permits a nonprofit to have no statutory members and requires at least three natural-person directors; the current Tennessee nonprofit charter form also distinguishes public-benefit/religious status and member status. ([Justia Law][1]) The charter materials also incorporate the IRS organizational-test requirements concerning exempt purposes and permanent dedication of assets on dissolution, while the EIN workflow is sequenced after legal formation. ([IRS][2])

I also completed final visual QA of the DOCX files page-by-page. That process caught and corrected a Word numbering problem and improved the CH-006 jurisdiction matrix into a real repeating-header Word table. Unverified entity-specific information remains deliberately represented as `[[PLACEHOLDERS]]`; I have not guessed the corporation's final legal name, addresses, directors, officers, EIN, worship location, or similar filing facts.
