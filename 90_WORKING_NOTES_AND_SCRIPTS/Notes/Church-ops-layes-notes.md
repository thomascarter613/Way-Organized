Completed the **CH-013 through CH-023 Congregational Operations Layer**. The full batch has been rendered and visually checked across all 53 DOCX pages, and the companion workbook passed formula/error and visual QA.

This layer is built around the current IRS questions that matter most at this stage: regularly scheduled services, average member/nonmember attendance, an established place of worship, an established congregation, number of members, the membership process, and member rights. Current IRS guidance defines an established congregation as people who regularly attend and participate in services at an established location, and notes that it generally is not merely members of one family. ([IRS][1])

The workbook is deliberately initialized at **zero members, zero services, zero attendance, zero meetings, and zero teachings**. Nothing in it implies activity that has not happened. It separately tracks physical member/nonmember attendance, synchronous remote participation, membership/household information, service summaries, congregational meetings, teaching records, recurring schedules, worship sites, and the supporting evidence log.

CH-018 also cannot truthfully be finalized until we choose the **actual recurring worship day, time, and location**. That is intentional: the current IRS instructions specifically ask for regular days/times, service activity, average attendance, and the established location rather than accepting a purely aspirational schedule. ([IRS][1])
